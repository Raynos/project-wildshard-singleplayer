How the shortlist was picked

Score = style 35%, seam 20%, length 10%, tempo 10%, key 10%, clean 15%. Style match comes from CLAP (laion/clap-htsat-fused) comparing each take to the three style briefs; its own vocals guess called almost every full-band take vocal, so the voice check is source separation instead (htdemucs vocal-stem share of energy: over 10% costs the take, over 20% keeps it off the shortlist); tempo and key from librosa; the loop seam is the most similar pair of bar-aligned cut points; loudness and dropouts from ffmpeg. One shortlist place per model, so each style is heard from three different models, and nothing under 30 s (those are loop or sting material, not a theme).

  • Speeds/memory measured on the M5 Max (128 GB), one model at a time, 2026-09-22.
  • Only MiniMax Music 3 and HeartMuLa write a full 60-90 s theme. Stable Audio Open 1.0 stops at 47 s (stitching or outpainting needed for a theme); SAO Small is loop/sting material.
  • Key/BPM control: none of the four holds the brief's D major / BPM reliably. SAO 1.0 hits the BPM most often (it reads 'N BPM' from the prompt); MiniMax mostly lands in G or C major regardless of the prompt; HeartMuLa has no tempo/key input at all.
  • Voices: HeartMuLa is a song model and hums/sings under many takes (demucs finds a voice-like stem in most); some MiniMax takes also sing despite '[Instrumental]' lyrics. Takes with >20% vocal-stem energy are kept off the shortlist.
  • ACE-Step 1.5 was set up and smoke-tested (MLX, MIT) but dropped by the user as too heavy for this machine, so it has no takes here.
  • Licences differ: HeartMuLa is Apache-2.0; Stable Audio Open needs Stability registration for commercial use and a 'Powered by Stability AI' credit (free under USD 1M revenue); MiniMax Music 3 needs 'MiniMax-Music3' shown in the game's UI (free under USD 20M revenue).
