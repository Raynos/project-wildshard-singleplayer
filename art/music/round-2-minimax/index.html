How the picks were made

Loop themes: CLAP style match 30%, groove 25% (drums and bass present across the take, measured by htdemucs separation, because the game's tension layer is exactly those stems), bar-aligned loop seam 20%, clean audio 15%, and full length with no fade-out 10%. Title: style 45%, seam 15%, clean 20%, full 20%. Any take whose separated vocal stem carries more than 20% of the energy is out, because MiniMax sometimes sings despite an instrumental prompt.
