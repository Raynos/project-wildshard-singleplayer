Wildshard sound effects, round 1

Every sound the game makes that is not music, rendered locally by two Stable Audio 3 models. Each cell is that model's best take for the family out of three, ranked by CLAP against the description; "rank 1" means the audio matched its own description better than any of the other 50 families and the three foils. A take ships only when it ranks in the top 5; otherwise that set leaves the family on the synth. Both sets are in the game (Settings switches between them); say which model should win each family, or which to leave on the synth.
