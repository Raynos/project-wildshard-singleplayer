## R1 gap list (R0 against T0) and fixes

- **Night Market's bunting and banner posts on every lane.** The lane seams are now Mosslight's own: a ruined stone
  stump at each crossing corner and motes low along the lane.
- **A bare grey plaza.** Each plaza quarter now has moss patches, a rubble heap and two runs of column stumps. Each
  garden has two rubble heaps.
- **Light, warm-grey paving.** The palette is teal-grey: R0's brightness, matching T0, with less red. A first darker
  take (r1) overshot to near black in first person.
- **A pale, flat sky.** The sky and fog are darker and teal. The targets also paint clouds, which our sky gradient
  cannot draw: that gap stays.
- **The tower reads lilac.** The landmark texture is tinted toward grey-green.
- **Big mint light discs under every lamp.** The lamp pools are 3 m instead of 4.5 m, and the lamp and gate pools are a
  darker green.

**Ruling (G872): R1 ships.** It closes the bunting, tone and pool gaps. Draws are unchanged at 86–142 a view.

## R2 gap list (R1 against T0) and fixes

- **No moss in the plaza's joints**, T0's strongest first-person cue. Broken moss runs, 8 to 14 cm wide, now lie along
  part of about a third of the flagstone joints within 62 m of the tower, on the kit material's own running bond
  (`jointMoss`). They replace R1's flat moss rectangles, which read as green boxes from the air. A first take, solid
  15 cm strips down whole joints, read as circuit lines and was redone.
- **Mint windows.** The window glow palette is a deeper emerald (`#0fb040`). It reads a little greener but still
  paler than T0's emerald: left for the next round.

**Ruling (G872): R2 ships.** The plaza tiles grow about 51 KB each, inside tonight's 100 KB a round; the phone wire stays
under its ceiling.
