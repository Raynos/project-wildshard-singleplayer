# Round 17 — tap the ammo chip to reload (E357 J13), 2026-10-01

Mockups, not picks. Jake took round 16's `J13-B` direction (no new disc: tap the ammo chip, top left, to reload); these
are three takes on how the chip shows it is a button and what a tap does. Each is a codex `image_gen` edit of the same
live iPhone portrait capture as round 16 (390 × 844, 3×, phone tier, build `b243f0c2`; Pine Hollow, lever-action rifle
after three shots, chip `4 / 7 + 21`). Every other HUD element (AIM · DODGE · JUMP, FIRE, LOOK, MOVE, HOVER, swap ring,
minimap, quest pill) is unchanged. Images are 852 × 1846 (codex output size).

- `J13-chip-A.jpg` — the chip is the button: a brighter cyan border with corner brackets and a reload ring-arrow glyph
  at its right end behind a hairline divider, no words. A second inset frame under it ("ON TAP") shows the tap moment:
  a fingertip on the glyph, the pips filling (6 of 7 lit), the count `6 / 7 + 19`. JOURNAL drops below the inset (the
  inset is an illustration of the tap, not a permanent second chip).
- `J13-chip-B.jpg` — the chip is unchanged; because the magazine is part-empty, a small "RELOAD" tab (ring-arrow glyph,
  same glass and hairline, corner brackets) hangs under the chip's right end. Tapping the chip or the tab reloads; the
  tab is gone when the magazine is full.
- `J13-chip-C.jpg` — mid-reload: the chip's border is the progress bar (top and right edges lit cyan, the bottom filling
  right to left, the left edge a dim track), pips at 5 of 7, `5 / 7 + 20`, "RELOADING" under the chip. At rest the chip
  carries a dim "TAP" hint next to the count (shown here in place of the old diagonal-arrows icon).

Two codex rolls per variant; the kept roll per letter: A roll 1 (roll 2 lit 5 pips for `6 / 7`), B roll 2 (roll 1 had
no corner brackets on the tab), C roll 1 (roll 2's progress border read less clearly). None garbled; no HUD drift.
