# E15 — the engine-true concept trailer (TRAILERS Part E, E474, 2026-10-10)

Jake picked it after the AAA-looking P15 cinematics (*"imply a product that would cost millions to make. And I've got
$1,000"*). Every keyframe is a **real frame of the alpha trailer** (in-engine capture), edited by OpenAI Image 2.5
(codex image_gen) only to remove the alpha tag / caption and add our **in-game Nalati kokpar rider** at the same game
quality (`ref-kokpar-rider-turntable.jpg`, the model's turntable from `~/ml/img2mesh/final-hy/`). Animated with LTX-2.5
image-to-video; cut by `scripts/steam-trailer/p15/assemble.py ride <e15 scratch>` on MiniMax take 702 of
`scripts/steam-trailer/e15-jobs.json` (an indie folk cue, not an orchestra).

- `key-e1.jpg` — Nalati's grass and herd (alpha 16.5 s) + the rider galloping toward the camera.
- `key-e2.jpg` — the grid aerial over Driftwood (alpha 54.5 s) + the rider small on the highway.
- `key-e3.jpg` — Driftwood's wreck beach (alpha 12.5 s) + the rider galloping past.
- `key-e4.jpg` — Pine Hollow's road (alpha 26.5 s) + the rider riding into the woods.

The rider keeps one model across worlds (the game doesn't restyle the player); the worlds change around him.
