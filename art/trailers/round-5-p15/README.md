All images are OpenAI Image 2.5 (codex image_gen); the shots are LTX-2.5 image-to-video from the keyframes; the cut is
`scripts/steam-trailer/p15/assemble.py`.

- `sheet-rider.jpg` — the Crossing's rider and white horse in two styles (painterly / faceted low-poly toon), the
  reference for X1–X4. Identity anchors: white horse, long red cloak.
- `sheet-party.jpg` — the Party's four heroes, each in its own style (low-poly toon pirate, oil-painted steppe
  horsewoman, photoreal ranger, neon samurai), the reference for P1–P3.
- `sheet-runner.jpg` — Leap of Faith's explorer (yellow scarf, striped wing-glider), the reference for L1–L4.
- `key-x1 … key-x4` — the Crossing: head-on painterly gallop; mid-leap over the highway (the re-made X2, after the first
  take never jumped); the toon top-of-leap over Driftwood's surf; charging the photoreal pine border.
- `key-p1 … key-p3` — the Party: four styles of feet on the dashed line; the walk away from the camera; the Antler King.
- `key-l1 … key-l4` — Leap of Faith: off Sky Reach's edge; top-down freefall over the grid of shards with the roc; the
  swoop over Nine Dragon's rooftops; the tile-and-neon dragon.
