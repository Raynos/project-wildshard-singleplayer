## Prompts

Each prompt is `common` + the card's own block + the codex task line (the exact files are in
`scripts/steam-trailer/t15/v3-postcards/prompts/`).

**common:** A flat, straight-on scan of the FRONT of one printed picture postcard, filling the entire image edge to edge
(no table, shadow, perspective, hands, curl), 3:2. A real mass-printed postcard, offset lithography on matte uncoated
card: visible halftone rosette dots, a limited palette of four or five inks, slight misregistration, flat chalky ink.
Not a glossy render or AI concept art: no glow, bloom, lens flare, god rays, golden hour, dramatic sunset or fantasy
maximalism. One soft light (daylight or overcast). Few objects, a simple composition with calm empty areas. An even plain
warm-white border on all four sides, ~4 % of the width, empty. Lettering inside the picture. Top right: a small
perforated stamp ("WILDSHARD", "10") cancelled by a circular postmark with wavy lines reading "WILDSHARD HIGHWAY" /
"10 OCT". No other text.

**driftwood:** Driftwood Isle in a faceted, flat-shaded, untextured low-poly toon style as a 1950s linen-postcard
printer would print a low-poly game scene: a small sand island, faceted grey rocks, three low-poly palms, a short
wooden pier, a slightly crooked white lighthouse with a coral-red band and cap; a flat faceted turquoise sea, a few flat
clouds in a plain midday sky. Palette: turquoise, sand, coral red, paper white, navy. Lettering: brush-script
"Greetings from" over chunky 1950s slab capitals "DRIFTWOOD ISLE" in coral red with a navy drop shadow.

**nalati:** Nalati, a wide high steppe in loose painterly gouache, as a 1930s travel postcard: green-gold grassland,
three white felt yurts, two grazing horses, low blue snow-capped mountains under a soft overcast sky; lots of open grass
and sky. Palette: sage, ochre, slate blue, cream, umber. Lettering: thin-caps "Greetings from" over condensed Art Deco
capitals "NALATI" in umber.

**pine:** Pine Hollow, a photograph reproduced as an early-1900s Photochrom: a misty clearing in a tall pine forest on
an overcast day, one small dark log cabin with a stone chimney and a wisp of smoke, mossy ground; no creatures, fire or
sun rays; muted hand-tinted flat tints. Palette: pine green, moss, umber, grey-blue mist, cream. Lettering: italic
"Greetings from" over "Pine Hollow" in Edwardian serif capitals and small capitals.

**ninedragon:** Nine Dragon Stack, a dense vertical city of stacked towers seen from a rainy street, as a 1980s Hong
Kong souvenir postcard: overcast blue-grey evening, fine rain streaks, a wet street; neon signs as flat fluorescent spot
inks (hot pink, cyan), no glow, no legible writing; a few figures with umbrellas; calm. Palette: slate blue-grey, black,
fluorescent pink and cyan, paper white. Lettering: bold 1980s italic sans, "Greetings from" over "NINE DRAGON STACK" in
white with a hot-pink offset shadow.
