# V2 · The map that draws itself — what happens between the frames

- **0–2.5 s** on cream paper, a pen rules one square plot, then Driftwood draws inside it stroke by stroke (crow-quill,
  indigo: island, palms, crooked lighthouse, pier, a sailboat), signed *@mara*.
- **2.5–4 s** the two-lane highway is drawn around that plot, clockwise, with roundabouts at the corners: black edges, a
  vermilion centre dash (the one constant line all through).
- **4–8 s** the camera follows the pen and pulls back; each neighbour arrives about a second after the last, each in its
  own hand — Nalati (brush), Pine Hollow (technical-pen hatching), Signal Dunes (pencil), Nine Dragon (crayon), each
  signed.
- **8–12 s** sixteen more plots fill in around them in other hands (marker, stipple, charcoal, ballpoint); roads run
  off every edge; one plot stays empty with a dashed vermilion border and *yours* written in it. A cartouche is ruled
  (~6.6 s) and *Every shard / built by a player.* is handwritten into it from 9.9 to 11.35 s.
- **12–15 s** end card over the held map. Corner tag on 0–12 s.
- **Sound:** a hand-made chamber cue (felt piano, pizzicato, glockenspiel; MiniMax take 501); a pen nib scratching on
  paper under the drawing (Stable Audio 3).
