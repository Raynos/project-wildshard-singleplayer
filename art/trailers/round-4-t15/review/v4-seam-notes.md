# V4 · The seam — what happens between the frames

- **One shot, 12 s, no cuts.** A low camera on the dashed centre line of a straight two-lane highway running to the
  horizon. Left of the road: a painterly oil-painted steppe with two yurts and blue mountains (Nalati). Right of the
  road: a flat-shaded low-poly toon beach with palms, rocks and a turquoise toon sea (Driftwood). The kerbs are the seam.
  Soft overcast light.
- **Motion:** camera projection of the one painting onto its depth mesh: a 0.6 m push forward while the lens narrows
  from 86.7° to 70°, sine-eased over 12 s. The palms, streetlights and far coast shift against each other (real
  parallax); nothing in the picture moves by itself (no wind, no waves).
- **5.2–11.6 s** caption *EVERY SHARD IS BUILT BY SOMEONE ELSE.* (large, centred low over the road).
- **12–15 s** end card over the held last frame. Corner tag on 0–12 s.
- **Sound:** a sparse cinematic cue (solo piano and quiet strings, opening wide around 6 s; MiniMax take 502); a gentle
  wind over grass and distant surf (Stable Audio 3).
