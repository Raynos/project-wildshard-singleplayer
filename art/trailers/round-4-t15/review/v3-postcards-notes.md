# V3 · Postcards — what happens between the frames

- **Locked top-down camera** on a printed highway map on paper (two-lane roads with dashed centre lines, roundabouts,
  square plots with plot numbers, a legend box *WILDSHARD · HIGHWAY MAP · SHEET 7*).
- **0.7, 2.3, 3.9, 5.5 s** four printed postcards are thrown in from off-frame, one every 1.6 s, each landing on its own
  plot: Driftwood Isle, Nalati, Pine Hollow, Nine Dragon Stack. Each falls, its trailing edge slaps flat, it skids
  ~20 px and settles at a slight angle; long soft shadow in the air, tight on contact. Each card: halftone print, white
  border, *Greetings from …*, a stamp and postmark, a strip *BUILT BY @…*.
- **6.55–7.35 s** a blank card is pushed in from the bottom into the plot labelled *PLOT 05·13 · OPEN*.
- **7.5–8.2 s** the dashed planned roads around it print in, connecting it.
- **8.15–10.4 s** a Claude Code-style prompt types on the blank card: *> greetings from:* then large *your world*; a
  block cursor blinks to 12 s.
- **12–15 s** end card over the held table. Corner tag on 0–12 s.
- **Sound:** a warm vintage travel tune (brushed snare, upright bass, vibraphone; MiniMax take 502); a paper slap on
  wood for each card, a slide for the blank card, soft typing under the text (Stable Audio 3).
