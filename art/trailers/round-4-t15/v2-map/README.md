# round-4-t15 / v2-map: the drawings of T15-2 *The map that draws itself* (E471, TRAILERS §3b)

Ink drawings by OpenAI Image 2.5 (codex `image_gen`, one generation each, square 1254 px, saved as JPEG ≤ 500 KB).
`scripts/steam-trailer/t15/v2-map/prep.py` skeletonises each one into ordered pen strokes and the page
(`map.html`) reveals the ink along those strokes, so every line draws on along its own path. The five `plot-*` are
the four readable neighbours plus the first plot; the sixteen `extra-*` are everyone else's plots, filling the map
as it runs off the page (repeats are mirrored and never sit next to themselves).

`sheet.jpg`: frames at 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 s of `content.mp4`.

Every prompt is the shared block below followed by the file's own HAND + SUBJECT line.

**Shared block:**

> A hand-drawn illustration on plain white paper, one tile of an illustrated map, made for a hand-drawn animation. Ink only: dark marks on a pure white background. No colour, no grey wash, no paper texture, no drop shadows, no border, no frame, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no signature, no compass rose, no legend. One square plot of land seen from a high bird's-eye three-quarter view, like a small vignette on an old hand-drawn pictorial map; the drawing fills the square image with a small white margin on every side. Few objects, clear readable silhouettes, plenty of white space between things. It must look drawn by a skilled person by hand with real, visible imperfections: wobbly lines, uneven spacing, a few overshoots. Not digital, not vector-clean, not ornate, not fantasy-maximal, no glow, no dramatic lighting, no scenery beyond the plot.
