WHY THE GOLDEN RATIO IS RUNESCAPE

Old School RuneScape doubles the XP per level about every 7 levels, from 83 XP for level 2 to 13 million at 99. Squeeze its 99 levels into our 20 and each of our levels is about 5 of theirs, about ×1.64 a level. The golden ratio (×1.618) is the same curve. Doubling (×2) would be far harsher than RuneScape: about 2,000 hours to 20.

FIRE EMBLEM GROWTH

  • A new character per shard. The first time you enter a shard, the dice roll you a character: each of the 8 attributes gets a base value (3–7) and a growth rate, the 8 rates being a shuffled pool (85, 70, 60, 55, 45, 40, 35, 30 %). One run you are a tank, the next a glass-cannon archer.
  • Every level-up rolls each attribute: +1 with its growth chance. No points, no choices, no respec. Over 19 level-ups an 85 % stat gains about 16; a 30 % stat about 6.
  • A cap of 30 per attribute (Fire Emblem caps too). Reachable only by a high-growth stat with luck.
  • Enemies use the same 8 attributes with fixed average growths per type, so a level 12 Welder is always the same Welder.
  • The combat formulas are re-tuned for these smaller numbers (an average stat at level 20 is about 15, not 24).

RECOMMENDATION

  • The golden ratio, ×1.618: it is the RuneScape curve at our scale. Level 10 in a few hours, level 15 a long weekend, level 20 about 60 hours of grind in one shard.
  • Enemies pay 10 × level, drop-off below you, 0 at 6 under.
  • Fire Emblem growth as above, with pure dice: no "blank level" protection, matching your no-streak-guard call.
