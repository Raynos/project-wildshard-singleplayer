HOW A MISS SHOULD FEEL

Kitchen Sink v2 · levels and combat · 2026-11-02 · draft for Jake

SHORT ANSWER

Keep your dice exactly as ruled: 12 % fail at equal level, +9.75 % a level, 90 % at +8, no streak guard, and enemies roll on you too. Change only what a failed roll looks like. Never show the word MISS for a blade that visibly touched the enemy: the game already uses grey MISS for a bolt that really flew past, and a second meaning is exactly what made Morrowind feel broken.

Instead, every failed roll becomes something the enemy did: it DODGEs (it slips aside, agile enemies), your blade DEFLECTs off its armour with sparks and a clang (tough enemies, and almost everything far above you), or it PARRYs (only enemies that own a parry move). Against a skull-level enemy the fight sounds like a blacksmith's shop: clang, clang, clang, thunk. You learn "too tough" from the sound before you read a number.

That is option E below. Recommended numbers are at the end, then four questions.

WHAT OTHER GAMES DO

Seventeen games that mix stats or dice with real-time hits. The pattern is loud: action games almost never show a dice whiff on a hit that looked like it landed. They turn the stat gap into damage, armour and reactions, and the few that keep a hidden roll dress it as something the defender did.

The techniques, in one line each

  • Hit table (WoW): one roll, the outcomes stacked; level gap moves the slices. Wildshard already does this.
  • Visible dodge or parry animation (WoW, Witcher 1, KCD): the failure is the defender acting.
  • Deflect with sparks and a clang (Monster Hunter, Dragon's Dogma, Souls): the failure is the defender's armour.
  • Glance or graze (WoW glancing, GW2 Weakness, Black Desert 2024): reduced damage instead of zero.
  • Entropy evasion (PoE) and PRD (Dota): streaks shrink, the average stays. Both are streak guards in spirit, so they're out under your ruling.
  • Bad-luck protection (a hit after N fails): ruled out by you, rightly; it makes skull enemies beatable by persistence.
  • Odds before the swing (VATS %, ME1 reticle): good in slow modes, noise in fast melee. A con colour on the nameplate does the job.
  • Hit-stop (Amalur, every Souls game): the world freezes for 45 to 140 ms on contact. Its absence is the cheapest "that didn't count" signal there is.
  • Miss text style: games that keep words use the defender's verb (Dodge, Parry, Block, Evade), never the attacker's failure (Miss), unless nothing touched.

WHAT WILDSHARD ALREADY HAS

  • Grey MISS in engine/ui/Combat.ts for a bolt that lands in a tree or flies past. That word is taken.
  • Hit-stop on blade contact: 60 / 90 / 140 ms for light, combo and heavy (SweptMelee.ts).
  • A clang path for a blade striking stone or timber: sparks from fx/Impacts.ts, a short 45 ms stop, a small kick. A deflect is that path pointed at an enemy.
  • Flinch, hit flash, stagger and push on the procedural enemy rigs (AnimalSim.ts). A dodge is a push without the stun.
  • HurtArc: the red arc that shows where a hit on you came from.

FIVE WAYS TO SHOW A FAILED ROLL

Frames are at 30 fps (Low Power Mode, the worst case). "f0" is the contact frame. Every option keeps your numbers: the failure rate at each level gap is identical; only the dress changes.

A Plain dice MISS
f0Blade touches the enemy. No hit-stop, no flash, no flinch. Grey MISS pops at the contact point.
f1–f27MISS rises 40 px and fades. The swing finishes as if through air. A soft whoosh.

  • Cheapest: one word, one sound
  • Totally honest about the dice
Cons
  • A blade that visibly touched and did nothing reads as a bug (Morrowind)
  • Same word as the aim MISS: you can't tell "I aimed badly" from "dice"
  • At +8, nine MISSes in ten feels like the game is broken, not like the enemy is strong

B The enemy acts: DODGE or PARRY
f0Blade reaches the enemy. No hit-stop. The enemy starts a slip: 0.4 m sideways, away from your swing, leaning 12°. Pale-blue DODGE at the contact point; a short whoosh passes. A parry-capable enemy instead raises its arm: gold PARRY, a bright ring.
f1–f4The slip completes (133 ms). Your blade sweeps through where it stood.
f5–f12It eases back to its stance; it can attack again at once.
Pros
  • The failure is the enemy's skill, so a strong enemy feels strong
  • Fits agile enemies (Crate Mite, Plumb Drone, Arc Sentry)
Cons

  • A heavy Riveter Bulwark hopping aside 80 % of the time looks silly
  • At +8 the enemy is a dancing blur

C Every hit lands: GRAZE for reduced damage
f0Normal contact, half hit-stop (30 ms), light flinch. A small grey number: 25 % of a clean hit.
f1–f27The number rises and fades; a dull thud instead of the meaty hit sound.

  • Feels best moment to moment: no blade ever does nothing

RECOMMENDATION: E, WITH THESE NUMBERS

The roll is untouched. Fail % = clamp(12 + 0.5·(AGI_def − PRE_att) + 9.75·max(0,g) − 2·max(0,−g), 2, 90), g = defender L − attacker L. No streak guard. Then one more draw only to pick the dress of a failure:

  1. Parry: 25 % of failures, only if the defender owns a parry move and faces you. Otherwise skip.
  2. Dodge share of the rest: d = AGI / (AGI + armour), armour = TGH against a blade or arrow, RES against energy; then d − 0.1·max(0,g), floor 0.1. A much stronger enemy doesn't bother moving.
  3. Deflect: everything else.

Words in the existing float style (rise 40 px, fade 0.9 s). On you: the same words at 70 % size by the crosshair. No word ever on a hit.

Sounds (MOSS-SoundEffect v2 and Stable Audio 3 Medium, better take ships, ±5 % pitch)

  • Deflect: short metal clang with a ring tail, 3 takes; a duller one for your own armour.
  • Dodge: a fast cloth-and-air whoosh, 3 takes; stereo-panned when it passes you.
  • Parry: a bright blade ring, 2 takes.
  • Glance: a dull thud, 2 takes. Crit: a sharp crack over the clean hit, 2 takes.

Hit-stop

Dodge 0 ms · deflect 45 ms (the stone clang's) · glance 30 ms · clean 60 / 90 / 140 ms (today) · crit +40 ms.

Telling the player the odds

No % on screen. The lock-on nameplate's level in its con colour (LVH2) is the warning; the clangs are the proof. The first time you deflect three times running on an orange-or-worse enemy, a one-time hint: "DEFLECT: it's above your level. Level up or bring a better weapon."

FOUR QUESTIONS

1. Which look?A plain MISS · B dodge and parry · C graze, never zero · D entropy · E deflect, dodge, parry.Recommended: E. It keeps your real misses and makes them read as the enemy being strong.
2. Keep the word MISS only for aim misses?Dice failures would never say MISS; they say DEFLECT, DODGE or PARRY.Recommended: yes. Two meanings for one word is Morrowind's mistake.
3. Does a deflect stay at 0 damage?Monster Hunter-style chip (1 damage) would make skull enemies grindable.Recommended: 0. Your 90 % at +8 stays a real wall; the tenth swing still bites in full.
4. When an enemy fails on you, show it?Silver arc, sparks at the screen edge, a clang, a small DEFLECT or DODGE by the crosshair; or silent.Recommended: show it. It makes your armour and agility feel earned as you level.
