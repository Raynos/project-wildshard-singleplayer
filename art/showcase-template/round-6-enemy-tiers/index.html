ELITES VS MINI-BOSSES

Kitchen Sink v2 · one-page explainer · 2026-11-02 · ask E523

SHORT ANSWER

In our engine they are the same thing. A "mini-boss" is the job a fight does in the level (a big named enemy that guards a step up). An "elite" is the system we already have that runs exactly that kind of fight. My recommendation: one tier called elite, and the kitchen sink's three mini-bosses are elites. Below them sit rare variants, above them the boss.

THE FOUR RUNGS

COMMON
The pack enemy of a band. Respawns on a timer, no name, no bar of its own.
Kitchen sink: Crate Mite, Pallet Jack, Plumb Drone, Welder Unit, Riveter, Arc Sentry. Pine: deer, boar, elk, bear. Driftwood: crab, monkey, boar, bear.
RARE VARIANT
The same enemy type in a rarer, tougher form: a different look, more HP and damage, a better reward. It spawns by chance among the commons. Still no name and no lair.
Pine today: coat rarity (common, uncommon, rare, legendary) and lodge contracts for "a rare coat". Driftwood: the black bear beside the brown bear. Kitchen sink: each type rolls uncommon or rare (for example the Lamp-Eye Riveter), +1 or +2 levels, extra coins and a trophy.
ELITE = MINI-BOSS
One named enemy with a lair. It notices you, fights, then walks home and heals if you run (leash). Phase 2 at half HP with a signature move. A banner names it when you get close. It can appear only at dusk, at night or in storms, respawns after a while, and drops a skin or finish.
Engine: src/game/eliteSystem.ts + Elite.ts. Nalati: 5 (Aqbars, Kokbori, Qyran, Qara Batyr, Argymaq). Pine: 4 (Ironhide, the Ghost Stag, Blackpaw, the Imperial Bull). Kitchen sink: Big Pallet L6, the Vault Warden L12, Foreman Unit 7 L18.
BOSS
A scripted encounter in its own arena: an intro, several phases, telegraphs and hazards, its own music stems, the end of the story, a legendary reward.
Engine: src/game/Boss.ts, src/game/boss/. Driftwood: the Drowned Captain. Pine: the Antler King. Nalati: the Golden King, Jel Ata. Kitchen sink: the Crate Titan L20 with a cutscene intro and the Titan Cleaver.

RECOMMENDATION

  • One word, "elite". The three mini-bosses are elites on the elite host. No separate mini-boss system, so no extra Wave 1 host.
  • Add rare variants as a row on each enemy type (chance, look, +levels, reward), generalised from Pine's coat rarity so Driftwood and Pine can use the same row later.
  • Show off the elite rules: one of the three elites (Foreman Unit 7) only comes out at night or in storm hour, so the time and weather rule is part of the showcase.
  • The boss stays separate: the Crate Titan on the boss host, with the cutscene intro.
