A levelled, MMO-style test shard in the orange-and-grey dev-map look. Every engine feature in one 500 × 500 m cell, built only from shared hosts, data rows and AssemblyScript. You arrive at level 1 and grind to a level-20 boss.

Plan ≈ 96 % decided. Levels, attributes and the combat maths are locked. In progress: all 40 mockups remastered from the grey blockout.

THE MAP

  • West: no enemies, no rewards. A walled town, 120 m: a gate plaza with three quest givers, the contract board and the trophy wall; Main Street with Tinker Rook, the weapons shop and empty shopfronts; six NPCs. Around it: the weapon range, a physics yard, the riding course with its paddock and loaner horse, a six-station movers run, the grapple course with a 99 m zip into town, a climb yard, the Rock with its squeeze cave, the hover park. Personal-best boards on all five courses; 6 vistas, 4 caches, 10 Gold 1×1s.
  • East: combat only. North-east: bands L1–3, 4–6, 7–9, 10–12 and the Training Vault portal. South-east top: band L13–15 (the stealth yard) and band L16–18; south-east bottom: only the gauntlet and the Crate Titan. 60 packs, 129 commons by day, 13 chests, 36 crates, 10 Gold 1×1s.
  • Underneath, at 0 m²: the Training Vault mini dungeon.

LEVELS AND YOUR CHARACTER

  • Levels 1–20, per shard. Each shard keeps its own level; the road and every new shard start you at 1. Only the showcase has levels: the other shards keep today's flat damage, stats and HP, and get upgraded later.
  • XP only from kills. An enemy pays 10 × its level, less when it's below you, nothing at 6 under. The XP to the next level grows ×1.618 a level (RuneScape's curve squeezed into 20): 10 kills for level 2, ~110 for 11→12, ~3,000 for 19→20. About 60 h to level 20.
  • Attributes grow automatically. Each shard rolls you a new character with a balanced aptitude spread: some strong attributes, some weak, never a worse total. Each level-up rolls average step × aptitude × a swing, so late levels can jump +10 or more. Each attribute has a cap just above its average.
  • Seven attributes (average L1 → L20, cap): VIT = your HP, 100 → 600 (660) · STR melee damage and stagger, 20 → 120 (132) · DEX ranged damage, landing hits with every weapon, melee crits, 20 → 120 · AGI dodging and ranged crits, front-loaded 20 → 80 · DEF armour and deflects, linear 20 → 80 · MDEF resists burn, shock and blast, a late bloomer 10 → 80 · LUK crit size and lucky saves, 10 → 20.
  • Level-up never stops a fight. In combat: a ring, a sting, the LV chip flips, a one-line gain chip, and your HP refills. The full tick-up card, each gain in turn, waits until you're out of combat. The Character tab shows your attributes against par, your strong ▲ and weak ▼ ones, and the derived numbers.
  • Death costs 25 % of the level's XP and never delevels. No mid-fight heals; out of combat (5 s after the last hit) HP refills in 12 s. Enemies that lose you walk home and reset.

COMBAT

  • Weapons are bought at a level-locked weapons shop: sword with combo and parry, spear, bow, crossbow with iron, pitch and broadhead ammo, lever rifle. Forge I–IV upgrades raise every weapon.
  • Real dice. 12 % of attacks fail at equal level, rising to 90 % when the target is 8 levels above you. No streak guard. Enemies fail against you by the same rules.
  • A failed hit is something the enemy did: a DEFLECT (sparks, clang, recoil), a DODGE (it slips aside) or a PARRY. "MISS" means only a shot you aimed wrong. Floating numbers: white hits, big yellow crits, small grey glances, the words on fails.
  • The maths (WoW-classic style): damage = weapon × Forge × STR or DEX × K / (K + armour), so armour takes a share and never walls you. DEX against AGI nudges the fail roll by up to 8 points. Crits 10 % base, up to 40 %, ×1.6–1.7. Lucky saves (2–4 %) flip a fail either way. Above your level, up to 40 % of hits glance for half.
  • Pacing: a common at your level dies in ~7 swings (~9 s) and kills you in ~11 hits; +3 levels is a coin flip, +6 a loss. Elites ~1 min, the Titan ~3.5 min. Ranged does ~10 % less damage a second than melee.
  • Skill on top: a timed parry (0.15 s) cancels a hit and makes your next hit a crit. Hits fill an enemy's stagger bar (STR fills it faster) for a 1.5 s stun. Energy hits burn, shock or knock back.
  • HUD: a bottom XP bar with an LV chip; nameplates within 25 m, coloured by level gap; a read-only Character tab. An enemy cap keeps the phone smooth: full AI within 60 m, at most 16 animated.

QUESTS, ECONOMY, WORLD

  • 12 quests, four per tier, freeform and level-locked (a grey "!" until you're high enough): kill, collect, explore, elite hunts and set pieces (storm hour, the keycard, the Training Vault, the gauntlet, the Titan). Each quest leg ends on a checkpoint card; the shard ends on a shard-complete card.
  • Kill contracts for every enemy type, elite and the Titan: Scrip, a trophy and a joke title the first time.
  • Scrip comes from kills, chests and contracts; grey kills pay nothing. Every kill drops one salvage part for its robot type. Tinker Rook sells one-off upgrades and turns parts into ammo. Forge I–IV; a 20-slot pack. Collectibles pay perks, elites drop finishes, trophies fill the hub wall.
  • The Training Vault: a four-room shrine (puzzle, fight, climb, the Vault Warden, a chest). Dying restarts the room; solved puzzles stay solved.
  • World: 20 + 4 minute days. Storm hour turns the whole cell stormy until four beacons are lit; rare natural storms after that.
  • Audio: an orchestral score (hub, west, two east themes that build band by band, elite themes, the Titan's phase stems), 8 stings, ~95 new sounds added to the shared set, ambience zones.
  • Developer-only until your phone pass. Each zone is written as a copyable recipe in the shard's own source.
