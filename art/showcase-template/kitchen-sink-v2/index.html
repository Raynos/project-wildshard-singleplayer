KITCHEN SINK v2

The Showcase Template (Template 3) · docs/plans/KITCHEN-SINK.md · planning, nothing built · 2026-11-03

One new shard that shows off every engine feature in one place: a levelled, MMO-style zone in a polished orange-and-grey workshop look, built only from shared hosts, data rows and AssemblyScript (no custom TypeScript). It fits in one 500 × 500 × 500 m cube. You arrive at level 1 in a calm town, play with every toy, then fight east through six bands of robots to a 15 m boss.

Plan ≈ 97 % decided. Built as 87 vertical slices: the west (33), then the east (54).

  • West: a 120 m town in the middle; every toy on its own labelled ground around it.
  • North-east: bands L1–3, 4–6, 7–9, 10–12 and the Training Vault's gatehouse.
  • South-east: band L13–15 (the stealth yard) and L16–18; below them the gauntlet's three courts and the small Titan gate with its portal arch.
  • Underground, inside the cube: the Training Vault at 30–50 m down, the Crate Titan's arena 150 m down (a 104 m floor, stands, its own sky). Every shard may pack its cube like this; nothing may leave it.

YOUR CHARACTER

  • Levels 1–20, per shard. XP only from kills (10 × the enemy's level, nothing for enemies 6+ below you). The curve grows ×1.618 a level: about 60 h to level 20.
  • Seven attributes, grown for you (no points to spend): VIT is your HP, 100 → 600 · STR melee damage and stagger · DEX ranged damage, landing hits, melee crits · AGI dodging, ranged crits · DEF armour · MDEF resists burn, shock and blast · LUK crit size and lucky saves.
  • Level-up never stops a fight: a quick chip and a full heal in combat; the full card once you're safe.
  • Death costs 25 % of the level's XP. No mid-fight heals; out of combat you refill in 12 s.

COMBAT

  • Weapons: you start with a sword and buy the spear, bow, crossbow (iron, pitch and broadhead bolts) and lever rifle. Basic ammo refills free in town; special bolts come from the bench.
  • The maths: armour takes a share of each hit (WoW-classic style). 12 % of attacks fail at equal level, up to 90 % at +8; a fail shows as DEFLECT, DODGE or PARRY. Above your level some hits glance for half.
  • Pacing: a common dies in ~7 swings (~9 s) and kills you in ~11; +3 levels is a coin flip; elites ~1 min; the Titan ~3.5 min.
  • Skill: a timed parry on the GUARD button makes your next hit a crit; strength fills an enemy's stagger bar.
