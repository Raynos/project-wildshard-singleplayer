LATER RULINGS (11-02)

  • Levels are per shard: level 1 on the road and in every new shard; you return to your level in each shard. Attributes grow automatically, no choosing.
  • Misses: real dice, 12 % at equal level, up to 90 % at +8 levels, no streak guard; enemies miss you by the same rules. How a miss looks and feels is being researched.
  • Death costs 25 % of the level's XP. No mid-fight heals, out-of-combat regen only.
  • Enemies: commons → rare variants → elites (no "mini-bosses": Big Pallet L6, Vault Warden L12, Foreman Unit 7 L18, plus night elites Sir Nibbles, Hot Mess, Riveting Rita) → the Crate Titan L20 (first-time skippable cutscene, the Titan Cleaver). Storm hour counts as night. Packs respawn ~90 s after you leave. Grey kills pay nothing.
  • Economy: Scrip; one salvage part per robot type; Tinker Rook's upgrades and ammo bench; Forge I–IV; several level-locked shops incl. a weapons shop; a 20-slot pack.
  • Quests: 12 (4 per tier) from three tier givers; kill-contracts pay Scrip + a trophy + a title. HUD: a bottom XP bar with an LV chip; nameplates within 25 m.
  • World: 20 + 4 minute days; storm-hour quest plus rare natural storms; west toys save personal bests and feats; Developer-only until Jake's phone pass.
  • Wave 1 normalises every host it touches and migrates Driftwood, Pine Hollow, Nalati and the rest onto it in the same row; HOST-AUDIT and SHARD-PLATFORM-NEXT keep their own rows. An enemy cap (AI within 60 m, at most 16 animated) joins it. Zone recipes live in the showcase's own source.

GREY BLOCKOUT

round 1 done It fits with no cuts. West 47 % used, east 78 %, 22.7 % of the cell free. The interiors (mini dungeon, cave) sit under the cell behind portals at 0 m² of surface. Zone sizes come from real game numbers: walk 4.3, sprint 7.2, gallop 13, hoverboard 14 m/s; grapple reach 38 m; Driftwood's boss arena r 22.

  • The hub sits on the centre line, and its east gate is the only way into the combat half. A seam wall runs north to south; the east road is walled, so arrivals from the east neighbour land on a safe road.
  • The six bands snake outward to the L20 boss in the far south-east corner, about 270 m from the hub. The mini-bosses sit where the bands change.
  • Round 2 notes: shrink the hub to a radius of 25–30 m (80 m reads empty at eye height); add cover and props between enemy packs; merge the west vault into the east mini dungeon (Jake's ruling); drop the fixed 9-leg chain for freeform level-locked quests.
