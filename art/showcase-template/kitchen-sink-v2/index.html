OPEN QUESTIONS

  • Does it all fit? 12 zones plus the candidates below in 500 × 500 m. The clean-room grey blockout is answering this.
  • Can weather and night be per zone? The weather host and day clock look cell-wide, so a storm zone 100 m from a sunny weapon range may be impossible. That changes zone 10.
  • Which additions go in, and which go into portal interiors so they cost no ground area.
