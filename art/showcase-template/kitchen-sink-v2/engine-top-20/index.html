Showcase Template · engine and host work · 6 Nov 2026

Twenty features carry 87 % of the Kitchen Sink's platform work

The showcase needs 1,342 hours of engine, host and shard-migration work, plus 770 hours of levels and art. The top 20 below are 1,171 of those platform hours. None of them is built yet: main has no levels code, no cutscene timeline, no shared boss host and no interior section today.

851 h engine and hosts491 h moving other shards770 h showcase content

Most of it is rebuilding, not inventing

Only six of the 20 are new. The biggest costs come from systems that already work for one shard and have to work for any shard.

NEW6 features, 276 h. Nothing shipped does this today.
REBUILD8 features, 567 h. It works, but it's hardcoded to a shard, stuck in the plugin, or split across several paths.
EXTEND6 features, 328 h. A shared host exists and gains new rows or rules.

17 of the 20 touch a shipped shard or a template. Across the 20, 439 hours (37 %) are spent moving shards rather than building the feature.

The hours have aged

These estimates date from 3 November, before the multi-repo rulings. I have not re-estimated them, so treat them as relative weight rather than a schedule.

  • Saves wipe at a breaking promotion (J149). The old-save shims and fixtures in the plan can go, which should cut hours on items and quests.
  • Every new row is now a release. The schemas moved to the shardfile repo, so a new section means a tagged release and a version bump. That adds cost to almost every feature here.
  • New features start in the new repos (J119). The slice files still point at singleplayer paths, and src/format no longer exists there.
  • "Migrate every shard in the same slice" is now the staging dance. The shard-changing features below are the first real list of breaking changes for that plan.

The top 20

Ranked by platform hours × complexity. Hours come from the plan's slices. Complexity is my own 1 to 5 rating: how many shards it touches, how exact the proof must be, and how much else waits on it. Every bar uses the same scale.

  1. 1

    Items, shops and payouts

    REBUILD120 h

    Any shard declares its own currency, pack, owned items, shops, kill payouts and salvage as rows. Forge tiers and upgrades become data too.

    Why it's heavy. Item lists in game code still name Driftwood and Pine ids, and the shop runs on the plugin. Both shards have to pay out exactly what they pay today: a full Driftwood clear still pays 92.

    Shards it changes Driftwood, Pine, Sky, Signal, templates

  2. 2

    One quest path and the contract board

    REBUILD108 h

    One way to author quests: givers, the grey and yellow "!", kill and hold steps, level locks, and a shared kill-contract board.

    Why it's heavy. Pine's quest runtime has to be taken apart into rows and scripts, which is 38 of these hours by itself. Every other quest shard needs proof that its quests still run the same.

    Shards it changes Pine, Driftwood, Nalati, Sky, Signal, Nine, templates

  3. 3

    One boss path

    REBUILD82 h

    One boss section and one host for phases, telegraphs, hazards, adds, shields, rewards and retries. A boss becomes rows plus its own AssemblyScript.

    Why it's heavy. There are five boss paths today. All seven shipped bosses move: Captain, Antler King, Storm Titan, Golden King, Roc, Matriarch and the template boss. 73 % of the hours are that move.

    Shards it changes Driftwood, Pine, Nalati, Sky, Signal, templates

  4. 4

    Levels and combat maths

    NEW70 h

    Opt-in levels 1 to 20, seven attributes, and a dice stage for crit, glance, DEFLECT and DODGE. HP from VIT, regen, XP and death cost come with it.

    Why it's heavy. It sits inside the damage pipeline every shard runs, and damage has no outcome or damage type today. Other shards stay on flat combat, so each one needs proof nothing moved.

    Shards it changes None change. All are checked.

  5. 5

    Weapons as rows, ammo and parry

    REBUILD70 h

    Bow, crossbow and rifle become weapon-family rows. Ammo types get effects, and melee gets an opt-in parry and riposte.

    Why it's heavy. Pine's held equipment scored 2 of 6 on the host bar, and firearms still live in the plugin. Parry is only an animation clip today, and it needs a new touch control.

    Shards it changes Pine, Nalati

  6. 6

    Enemy attack kinds

    EXTEND84 h

    Wall stuns, creature projectiles, energy damage, floor hazards, hit zones, guard arcs, enemy parry, a stagger bar, and burn, shock and blast on hit.

    Why it's heavy. Each one is small, and most robot movement is already rows. They all enter the same hit path, so they have to land one at a time.

    Shards it changes Sky, Driftwood, Nalati, Pine, maybe Signal

  7. 7

    Movers and director out of the plugin

    REBUILD58 h

    Sliders, spinners, lifts and wave stages become real hosts reached from rows.

    Why it's heavy. The player never sees this, but other work waits on it. Quests, Nine's portal and the boss paths all import this scaffolding, so the director has to move before the boss path.

    Shards it changes Sky, Driftwood, Pine, Signal, Nine, templates

  8. 8

    Interiors, portals and the 500 m cube

    REBUILD58 h

    One interior host for caves, vaults and arenas, walk-in portals, and a validator that keeps every space inside the shard's cube with no overlaps.

    Why it's heavy. Pine's cave, Nine's portal and Nalati's arena each do this their own way. The showcase needs a Vault at y −40 and a Titan arena at y −150, and the two never load together.

    Shards it changes Pine, Nine, Nalati

  9. 9

    In-engine cutscenes

    NEW44 h

    A timeline with a real camera: authored shots, letterbox, captions, music cues and hold to skip, all as rows.

    Why it's heavy. The engine has no camera except the player's. The new owner borrows the render camera and must always hand it back. Five boss intros and Driftwood's finale move onto it. IN-GAME-CUTSCENES still waits for your go.

    Shards it changes Driftwood, Pine, Nalati, Sky, Signal

  10. 10

    Sealed rooms and group waves

    NEW44 h

    A shrine host: rooms that seal, a checkpoint per room and a shrine card, plus director inputs such as "wave 2 is dead". The Vault and the gauntlet both use it.

    Why it's heavy. No host exists, only pieces in the arena room, plates and director. Dying restarts one room while solved puzzles stay solved. It has one real user unless Driftwood's sea cave becomes the second.

    Shards it changes Templates, maybe Driftwood

  11. 11

    Traversal rows

    EXTEND58 h

    Conveyors, jump pads, a shared rope-bridge deck, hooks in the world, and ziplines baked by the SDK.

    Why it's heavy. Zipline, ride and grapple rows are refused unless a shard declares a quest page, and that link has to go. Rope sway has to leave Driftwood.

    Shards it changes Sky, Driftwood, Nine

  12. 12

    Elites, rare and night variants

    EXTEND58 h

    One rarity row (uncommon 8 %, rare 2 %), night-shift swaps, elite phases, typed drops, named stings and interruptible wind-ups.

    Why it's heavy. The rows are simple. The hours go on moving Pine's coats and night thralls and Nalati's elites without changing them.

    Shards it changes Pine, Nalati, templates

  13. 13

    World courses and saved records

    NEW51 h

    Timed courses out in the world, personal bests that save, placeable dummies and a weapon loan zone.

    Why it's heavy. Courses only exist today in dev rooms far above the map, and their bests are never saved.

    Shards it changes Nalati, Nine

  14. 14

    Enemy cap and pack spawner

    EXTEND38 h

    At most 12 enemies awake and 16 posed. Packs get levels, group respawn at about 90 s, and a sleep pose.

    Why it's heavy. The cap runs on every shard, so replays have to prove it. The showcase needs it most: up to 15 enemies in one portrait view is an estimate that has never run on a phone.

    Shards it changes Every shard for the cap. Driftwood, Pine, Nalati for spawns.

  15. 15

    One weather system

    REBUILD34 h

    Weather and day rows that fill the shared sections, with a hold rule so storm hour lasts until the four beacons are lit.

    Why it's heavy. Few hours, wide reach. Weather code is tied to arrows and wildlife, and its wind drives Sky's and Signal's painted looks, so four shards need their looks checked.

    Shards it changes Pine, Nalati, Sky, Signal

  16. 16

    Board tricks and grind rails

    NEW39 h

    Board events, trick rows and a trick score, plus rail rows with their own rail law. The board's look moves into the game layer.

    Why it's heavy. A rail is a new movement law. The board is on every shard and the highway, so the look move touches them all, though only as path changes.

    Shards it changes Every shard, paths only

  17. 17

    Combat HUD

    EXTEND38 h

    XP bar, LV chip, level-up moment, nameplates, floating numbers and the Character tab.

    Why it's heavy. Health bars and floating text already exist, so much of this is new data on old pieces. Levelled nameplates need about 120 px, and every other shard's HUD has to stay the same.

    Shards it changes None change. All are checked.

  18. 18

    Scramble climb

    NEW28 h

    A real climb mode for scramble faces, and the first shipped use of ladder rows.

    Why it's heavy. The climb is a new driven mode in the character motor, with its own feel and collision work. There is no migration, so all the risk is in the mode itself.

    Shards it changes None

  19. 19

    Interactables and hinges

    REBUILD37 h

    One section for plates, levers, doors, barrels and beacons, plates that feel any body, and a hinge row for see-saws.

    Why it's heavy. The plate puzzle has shipped three different ways, and no joints exist anywhere yet.

    Shards it changes Driftwood, Pine, Signal, templates

  20. 20

    Collectibles, trophies and finishes

    EXTEND52 h

    Collectible sets, bench views, record feats, a trophy wall section, and finish and title grants.

    Why it's heavy. This is the simplest design on the list. Its weight comes from moving Driftwood's glass and plaques, Pine's mounts and Nalati's finishes, which removes two custom sections.

    Shards it changes Driftwood, Pine, Nalati

Three things decide the order

  • The director moves before the boss path. One boss class lives inside it.
  • Movers move before the quest board. Three quest modules import the mover code, so both edit the same files.
  • Weather comes after the bow work. Weather reads the arrow code that the bow slice changes.

What's outside the 20

The other 171 platform hours: the honest ruler and host register 36, the required shard-complete card 28, audio hosts 24, stealth 22, the robot Blender pipeline 16, the loaner horse 14, and 31 hours of small work spread across content slices. The ruler comes first in the build, but it's tooling rather than a feature.
