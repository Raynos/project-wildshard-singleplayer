THE GAME AND ITS LOOK
A real in-game print-screen (iPhone portrait) of Wildshard's "Showcase Template": a super fun, polished, playful theme-park world that shows off every feature of the game. Art style: SIMPLE BUT POLISHED stylized 3D, like a finished, well-funded stylized action game (clean readable shapes, bevelled and rounded edges, soft hand-tuned materials, toy-like charm, saturated but tasteful colour, crisp warm sunlight with soft shadows and ambient occlusion, a bright blue sky with a few soft clouds, light atmospheric depth). The shard's identity is a WORKSHOP / TEST-YARD theme in a warm ORANGE and cool GREY palette with cyan accents: painted steel, warm concrete, timber, canvas awnings, safety stripes, bunting, banners, planters and small trees, lamps, pennants, signage with personality. It must look finished and inviting, never like a level-design placeholder.
