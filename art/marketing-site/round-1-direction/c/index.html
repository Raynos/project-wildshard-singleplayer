what is a shard?

A world made of shards.

Each shard is 500 × 500 × 500 metres, built by one author and placed on a shared grid. A server-owned highway runs between them; glowing seams stitch them together.

500 ma cube per shard, one author each
5 × 5the first grid, around a fixed centre
1server-owned highway between them
Seamsglowing edges that stitch shards together

is a stranger's shard safe on my phone?

Strangers' worlds, safe on your phone.

A shard ships data and baked assets, never code that can touch your browser. Every shard is metered, deterministic and built against a versioned API.
