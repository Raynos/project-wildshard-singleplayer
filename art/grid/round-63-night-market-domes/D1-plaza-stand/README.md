## Prompt set

**Grid:** nine portrait views at `(0,1.7,-45)`: steep up with
gate roof low in frame; southwest/southeast plaza aerials;
west canal bridge; north hero toward the full gate; east canal
bridge; south along the avenue; close flagstones below the eye;
and a northern aerial looking back south over the gate's rear.
Preserve the three-tier navy/gold gate with three red-post open
bays, soft warm lanterns, red stalls, round tree crowns and canals.

**Details:** isolate each grid tile into one 1024×1536 portrait.
Retain its composition, camera and layout; B2 supplies the gate
identity only. Clean matte low-poly surfaces, simple paving joints,
soft lamp pools, no photographic grit or added landmarks. Header:
`D1 · n · TARGET · view`. Gate stays out of side/back views;
view 8 stays at close ground scale rather than an aerial plaza.

These are art targets, not runtime captures or measured costs.
