# Night Market figures · G755 (2026-10-26, sp-o1)

Jake (G755): the Night Market figures become hand-built Blender low-poly figures with simple painted faces (two dots
and a line), toon shaded. They are `scripts/blender/open-plots/kit.py` `scripted_figure` (the script is the source,
`public/assets/open-plots/kit/figures.glb` is committed, 6a9c8cb1ae): a vendor in an apron and cap, a shopper with a
hair bun, an elder in a straw hat with a cane, ~360 flat-shaded triangles each. The TRELLIS.2 takes are retired.
