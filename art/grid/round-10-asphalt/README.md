# round-10-asphalt: Which asphalt road, and which crossroads?

Ask E438 (SHARD-PLATFORM mockups), after Jake's G73 on round-2-highway: *"No it has to be an asphalt road"*. Every frame also
follows G72 (one landmass: Driftwood's lagoon sits inside its own cell, no sea under the road), G74 (neutral buffer strip
that fades softly into each shard), G75 (each shard keeps its own grade, neutral grey-blue road) and G68 (safe zone: bare
hands, no ATTACK button).

Made with codex `image_gen` (gpt-image), editing the same real iPhone-portrait captures as round-2:
`progress/shard-platform/sf18b/grid-crossroads.jpg` (hands-only grid HUD) and
`progress/{driftwood-isle,pine-hollow,nalati-grasslands}/20261002-0011-0d59505c/` (shard looks), plus
`art/grid/round-3-seams/B-buffer-biome.jpg` for the buffer strip. These are mockups for art direction, not renders: the
images are more painterly and detailed than the game renders today.

**Re-rolls:** cr-A was re-rolled once because the HUD drifted (the minimap and quest chip had swapped, and the hoverboard
ran through the bottom panel). cr-B and cr-D were re-rolled with a stricter HUD layout line because DODGE and JUMP had
moved into the panel. cr-D was rolled three times in all. The kept take (the second) has the right HUD layout and
correct sign arrows, but its hands are smoother low-poly than the capture's. The third take drifted further and its sign
arrows were wrong.

**Recommended: A on both boards.** The country road reads as asphalt at a glance. Its dashes and marker posts sell
30 m/s, and its gravel shoulders melt into the neutral buffer, so it suits every shard style without looking urban. The
fingerpost matches it and names all four shards. Worth taking from B: a distance sign ("PINE HOLLOW 400 M") on the
approach to each crossroads.
