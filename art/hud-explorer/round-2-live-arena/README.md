# Shared practice arena · first live build

[Driftwood portrait capture](driftwood-practice-portrait.jpg) shows the in-engine room with its cyan grid, three material variants, first-person starter sword, and touch HUD. The room is also available from the Pine Hollow, Nalati, and Nine Dragon title cards. Local portrait QA entered each shard with its starter weapon and damaged a target with both sword and crossbow.

This is an implementation checkpoint against the [approved target](../round-1-arena/C-humanoid-armor.jpg). The current dummies have the correct humanoid construction and materials but simpler armor shapes and surface detail than the target. Their visual sculpt is still in flight.
