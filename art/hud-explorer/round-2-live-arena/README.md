# Shared practice arena · first live build

[Driftwood portrait capture](driftwood-practice-portrait.jpg) and [Pine Hollow crossbow capture](pine-hollow-practice-portrait.jpg) show the in-engine room with its cyan grid, three material variants, first-person starter weapons, and touch HUD. The room is also available from the Nalati and Nine Dragon title cards. Local portrait QA entered each shard with its starter weapon and damaged a target with both sword and crossbow. After grouping rigid details by material, the Pine Hollow arena view measured 104 draw calls at 390×844, down from 271 on the initial build.

This is an implementation checkpoint against the [approved target](../round-1-arena/C-humanoid-armor.jpg). The current dummies have the correct humanoid construction and materials but simpler armor shapes and surface detail than the target. Their visual sculpt is still in flight.
