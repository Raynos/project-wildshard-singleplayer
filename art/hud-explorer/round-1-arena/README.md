The three targets, left to right in the current proposal, are **wood frame + wood armor**, **straw body + cloth armor**, and **wood frame + steel armor**. Their bodies remain stationary; they can animate their joints and react to hits. Leave room between them for readable combo, heavy attack, ranged hit/miss, damage-number, and first-person weapon-animation practice. The room has a simple colliding floor and four walls with cyan grid lines. Its exact dimensions remain a build choice after visual review.

The three dummy turntables check material and silhouette; the arena sheet checks space, camera angles and target separation. Treat these as one focused review, without a batch of user toggles or nine separate approval requests.
