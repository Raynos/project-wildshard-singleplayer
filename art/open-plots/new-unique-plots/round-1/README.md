## Common prompt

The attached image is an in-game screenshot of Wildshard, a stylised 3D game played as an iPhone portrait Safari web app. It shows the Night Market open plot from about 40 m up: a flat square 500 m plot seen from above and behind the player, lit by the engine (clean stylised PBR, soft bloom, baked warm light pools, crisp geometry).

Make a NEW in-game screenshot of a DIFFERENT open plot of the same game, described below. Keep: the same engine rendering (stylised 3D game render, not concept art, not a painting, not photoreal), a similar elevated camera about 35-45 m up looking down across the plot, the square plot reading as one small hand-built world about 500 m across with the paved road entering at the bottom of the frame, and the plot's signature landmark large and dominant in the upper half of the frame at the end of the road. Replace EVERYTHING about the Night Market: its red gate, red stalls, canals, lanterns, palette and light are all gone.

Quality bar: far richer and more detailed than the reference. Dense hand-crafted set dressing on every tile, layered heights (raised terraces, steps, walls, bridges), varied silhouettes, readable materials, a strong focal composition, a sky and lighting that belong to this world alone. Small detailed props everywhere, but no people, no animals, no characters.

Never include: any text, letters, numbers, signs with writing, billboards, posters, logos, HUD, UI, buttons, minimap, watermark, device frame, phone notch or browser chrome. No hologram. No floating lanterns.

Output: exactly ONE portrait image, 1024 x 1536.

## saltwind-lighthouse

THE PLOT: SALTWIND LIGHTHOUSE, a North-Atlantic fishing headland in a passing squall. Late afternoon, heavy slate-grey storm clouds with one bright break of silver light on the sea, slanting rain, wet reflective stone. Palette: slate grey, wet granite, whitewash, signal red, deep sea green, a little ochre lichen. Style family: stylised maritime village, chunky readable shapes.
Signature landmark: a tall red-and-white striped lighthouse on a raised granite headland at the top of the frame, its lantern room glowing warm white with a visible light beam sweeping out through the rain over the sea.
Dressing: the road becomes a wet cobbled quay; a curved stone harbour wall with mooring bollards; tidal flats with shallow pools and two small fishing boats leaning on the sand at low tide; whitewashed stone cottages with dark slate roofs and red doors climbing in terraces toward the lighthouse; drying nets on poles, lobster pots, stacked crates, coiled rope, buoys, a slipway, a wooden boathouse; sea-thrift and gorse tufts in the rock; white surf breaking on the rocks at the plot's edge.

## patchwork-fairground

## fossil-badlands

THE PLOT: FOSSIL BADLANDS, a palaeontology dig camp among banded clay mesas. Soft overcast pearl-white sky with a low warm sun glow, gentle diffuse light. NOT a sand desert: no dunes, no palms, no sandstone temple. The ground is hard eroded clay: mesas, buttes and hoodoos striped in bold horizontal bands of terracotta red, cream, rust and lavender-grey, with grey-green sage scrub. Palette: terracotta, bone white, cream, rust, sage, canvas khaki. Style family: stylised natural-history expedition, chunky layered rock forms.
Signature landmark: a colossal dragon skeleton, bone white, half excavated from a dig pit at the top of the frame, its long spine and ribs arching high over the pit, its horned skull resting on the pit's edge, wooden scaffolding and ladders against the ribs.
Dressing: the road becomes a plank boardwalk and packed-earth track; terraced excavation trenches with string grids; canvas expedition tents and a big mess tent; timber sorting tables with bone fragments; crates, sieves, wheelbarrows, a hand-cranked crane lifting a plaster-jacketed bone; a small timber lab hut; mine-cart rails into a mesa; layered strata cliffs ringing the plot.

## mushroom-hollow

THE PLOT: MUSHROOM HOLLOW, a fairytale woodland village living under giant glowing toadstools. Deep blue night, a clear starry sky with a thin crescent moon. Light comes from bioluminescence: magenta and pink toadstool caps glowing from beneath, teal glowing moss along the paths, small soft pools of light, drifting spore motes. Palette: navy, indigo, magenta, hot pink, teal, warm cream windows. Style family: storybook fairytale, rounded soft shapes. NOT alien: no crystals, no aurora, no monoliths.
Signature landmark: the toadstool tower, a towering mushroom at the top of the frame whose thick cream stem is a house with round windows and a spiral stair wrapping it, crowned by a huge glowing magenta cap with white spots.
Dressing: the road becomes a mossy cobbled lane edged with glowing teal moss; little cottages built into mushroom stems with round wooden doors and warm windows; clusters of smaller glowing caps of many sizes; giant fallen-log bridge over a dark glossy stream; fern banks, gnarled roots, flower bells, a well, tiny vegetable gardens, picket fences, stepping-stone paths; a ring of glowing mushrooms in a clearing.

## jungle-ziggurat

THE PLOT: JUNGLE ZIGGURAT, a living temple-town in a misty rainforest. Humid bright morning, white mist in the canopy, god rays through the trees, everything wet and lush. NOT a ruin: the temple is intact, clean, painted and lived in. Palette: deep jade and emerald foliage, warm limestone, turquoise and saffron painted bands, red-ochre banners, white waterfalls. Style family: stylised adventure jungle, big readable leaves.
Signature landmark: a tall stepped pyramid at the top of the frame, clean pale limestone with turquoise-and-saffron painted bands, a broad central stair up to a shrine on top with a jade-tiled roof, banners hanging from its terraces.
Dressing: the road becomes a stone causeway over a jade pool fed by waterfalls; rope-and-plank bridges between treetop platforms; thatched stilt houses; giant tropical leaves, ferns, palms and buttress-rooted trees; carved stone steles with painted patterns (no writing); hanging vines; stone water channels; terraced gardens of flowers; a canopy walkway ringing the plaza.
