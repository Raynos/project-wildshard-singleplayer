# Conch Duelist weapon-continuity candidate review

Date: 2026-10-22. Scope: one bounded two-context built-in image-generation attempt, two calls total. Self-review followed by an independent actual-pixel review.

## Verdict

Partial improvement, not recommended for selection as a canonical replacement. The broad shield is much clearer in left-facing panels 02/07 in both contexts, but rigid attachment/occlusion continuity is still not established. Explorer 03/08 relocate the far-side shield toward the shell's rear, and World far-side silhouettes remain ambiguous. Preserve both candidates as evidence; retain the selected v3 sheets as confirmed by independent review. No Site, manifest, STATE, Git, model, runtime, or source-original edits were made.

## Authority and explicit inference

The original approved conch-duelist-lowpoly.webp remains authoritative. In its frontal view, the broad cream/coral shield is screen-left and the narrow blade is screen-right. The working interpretation is anatomical right forelimb = shield, anatomical left forelimb = blade.

The side-camera mapping is inferred: left-facing 02/07 expose the shield-side forelimb nearest the camera, while right-facing 03/08 expose the blade-side forelimb nearest. Hidden attachment geometry is not visible in the approved hero and is not recovered or proven by these edits. Screen headings alone cannot establish a correct rigid turntable.

The visible blade remains well separated beside the torso rather than reliably occluded on the far side. A single coherent front-limb chain cannot be verified.

- 07 Elevated left: head still faces screen-left and near-side broad shield now matches the broad-shield category of 02. Its angular outline and arm visibility differ; the far blade projects toward the back/right of the body. Not a rigid-construction pass.
- 08 Elevated right: head still faces screen-right and the near-side blade remains visible. As in 03, broad shield is displaced rearward; an additional pale wedge near the face makes far-side attachment/occlusion ambiguous.

Dark grid, cyan stage rings, portrait 3-by-3 layout and full silhouette framing survive. Footer now explicitly says AI TARGET MOCKUP and discloses inferred hidden surfaces. No measured-camera claims were added.

## Cross-context and preservation limitations

- Shield category improves in both left-facing pairs, but the Explorer shield is bulkier/rounder than the World shield. Exact asymmetric geometry is still unstable.
- The built-in edit is not a pixel-local mask operation. Every non-target panel changes at least somewhat. Read-only pixel analysis in pixel-comparison.json records mean absolute channel deltas of 2.651-3.725/255 in the Explorer non-target panels and 3.494-4.600/255 in the World non-target panels. These are image-difference diagnostics, not anatomy metrics.
- Both candidates are 1024 by 1536 RGB PNGs. Raw generated bytes are preserved untouched.
- Explorer raw PNG is 1,842,893 bytes; World raw PNG is 2,268,741 bytes. Both exceed normal repository preview-image limits and must not be silently committed or hidden in runtime paths. No storage pins were added.
- No regenerated model renders were used or substituted. There is no canonical-completion, quality-completion, phone-performance, or runtime claim.
- Exactly one candidate per context was generated. No follow-on generation or image-editing fallback was attempted.
