# Medusa native-owner candidate 1

Editable implementation slice, not gameplay admission. Selected complete-character-next remains
3.2925/5 versus retained v2's fresh 3.015/5. No new artistic rating, native render, physical contact,
phone performance, or Arena/playable claim is made.

## Implemented here

- `medusaNativeSource.ts` loads the actual selected packed geometry and converts its 27 absolute
  binds to native local joints without moving the source, actor root, flight heights or living volumes.
  It creates the real AnimalSim flight, NativeCreaturePose and existing BurstFlyerBrain with the
  selected pulse strike and home. It does not install a second gameplay scheduler or register a species.
- `medusaNativeClips.ts` retains the selected live channel equations and adds explicit native
  transition ownership. A lethal contact captures the actual local endpoint, then smoothly blends
  toward the existing terminal dead fold. It is an authored source candidate, not an accepted animation.
  No root channel, floor offset, dominant-influence replacement, collider padding or captured corpse
  policy is introduced. Normal engine root-flinch behavior is retained.
- `medusaWeightedVertices` evaluates every original weighted vertex from the real native pose.
  This gives a source-faithful contact-design oracle rather than a convenient convex substitute.
  It is checked independently against THREE's actual weighted skin.
- `audit_source.py` pins the selected artifacts without copying any binary and counts exact mesh
  influences. There are ten mesh parts, 17,226 vertices, and 2,304 blended vertices. The rig has 27 bones.
- The separately approved `nativeRigidCorpse.ts` change treats bodyY as a finite pivot coordinate,
  not a strictly positive extent. Zero/negative pivots are allowed; real extents and resulting
  Float32 principal inertia stay strictly positive. It changes neither source height nor mass law.
  Synthetic resource regressions cover allocation/disposal and invalid inputs, not Medusa contact.

- The actual-source continuation test exposed a previously hidden NaN: the shared pose law divided
  nonzero flight speed by Medusa's zero leg length before entering its custom-rig branch. The
  approved `animalPose.ts` correction skips terrestrial stride advancement for declared flight,
  while retaining flight/custom timers and engine root behavior. No leg length or phase reset is
  fabricated. Real Drift Ray, Gale Wisp, Storm Roc, Dune Ray and Matriarch clip regressions retain
  their time-driven motion; the grounded Sky Goat retains the exact original stride frequency.

## What this is not

This source assembly has no admitted corpse recipe and is deliberately not wired to Practice or
Arena. Its fallback remains the unsafe standard flight death; the focused tests retain that failure.
The source adapter is not the complete borrowed-world owner and does not prove wall collision,
loot, death/Retry/reload, gameplay render equivalence or physical-device quality.

## Exact bounded flying-owner boundary

The following is a proposed coordinated slice, not changes made here:

1. Add `src/game/practice/PracticeNativeFlyer.ts` and a focused real-Medusa fixture. It should borrow
   one Arena physics/player/events/combat scope and one clock, create one page-body SimHost, retain
   `spec.flight`, and install the real burst policy and strike/home ports. Its stepping follows the
   existing pre/physics/post split, with no callbacks authored in creature data. Do not simply remove
   the grounded decision checks in PracticeNativeActor or omit the flight field from its spawn.
2. Reuse native pose transition registration and the actual selected joints/clips. Preserve the
   living vertical capsule, root/bind origin, flight heights and burst behavior. Existing
   `practiceNativeRecipe` rejects flight and `practiceNativeSpawn` omits flight, so neither is an
   unchanged flying recipe. Factor a narrowly reviewed common rest-hierarchy helper if needed.
3. Preserve the concrete CharacterMotor handoff transaction and reservation cap. SimHost already
   creates CharacterMotor for page-body flyers. It already skips `entity.step` while the corpse owns
   root, avoiding FlightMotion's rejected ground fall after successful handoff. Existing body sync
   also skips physical corpses. No AnimalSim death callback or second physics step is needed.
4. Verify live motor location before admission: AnimalSim deliberately excludes flight from ground
   motor movement, while SimHost only resets the motor at creation. A flying installer must retain
   the established flight trajectory and explicitly synchronize the living body owner without
   pretending a reset is grounded movement or changing flight collision semantics silently. The
   exact existing legacy flyer collision contract must be compared before adding that bridge.
5. Keep the living vertical capsule separate from native corpse selection. Both packedCreatureBodies
   and AnimalFactory currently reject capsuleAxis=y with dims.rigidCorpse; legacy AnimalView selects
   upright by that axis. Prefer a separately typed, source-pinned native corpse recipe admitted by
   the new native installer, not deleting general legacy validation or changing capsuleAxis to z.
   This requires coordinated schema/loader/installer work, not an asset-only exemption.
6. Reuse NativeCorpseOwner/NativeRigidCorpse after a real geometry-faithful recipe passes. The
   zero-pivot correction in this slice removes one invalid coordinate assumption, not the above
   selection/shape restrictions. Reserve before admission; an unavailable lease rejects the
   candidate before combat, never silently falls back to the unsafe standard death.

## Remaining actual corpse-source constraint

No faithful <=8-part rigid corpse is established for this selected geometry. Eight independently
articulated three-joint ribbons plus bell and organ are not eight rigid shapes. The 2,304 blended
vertices cannot be proven by attaching their bind positions to one dominant bone. Convexifying a
combined bell/ribbon group may fill visible negative spaces; enclosing points is not enough.

This count is not a mathematical impossibility proof for every future source redesign. A proposed
bounded recipe must be tested against the exact weighted oracle over live lethal phases and the
complete animated death fold, then real floor/sloped/edge contact and runtime lifecycle. Do not
raise shape caps, replace the creature with a fixture, author a giant padded hull, or promote an
unsupported contact claim to finish this pass. A source redesign must preserve the approved eight
ribbons and complete editable art, and keep the selected best candidate as baseline.
