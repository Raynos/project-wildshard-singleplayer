# Matched static source review preparation

2026-10-20 21:44 UTC. One actual Blender render pass completed. This is builder
inspection and review evidence, not independent likeness or animation acceptance.

## What was actually rendered

Twenty original PNGs: selected predecessor and articulated candidate at the nine
inherited Conch cameras, plus the original hero camera. Original resolution is
496×700 for the nine views and 780×960 for hero. Original Cycles sample counts,
AgX, factory World behavior, floor, material roughness and lights are retained.
No model, camera, stance, joint pose or root adjustment was made for framing.

The predecessor is converted by B=Ry(pi) into actor +Z. Both models then use the
same proper actor-to-Blender transform C=(-x,z,y); C B recovers the original
Conch studio orientation. No reflection, hidden render-root yaw or winding flip.
Native linear corner colors and normals are transported consistently. Imported
positions exactly match float32 source positions. Blender's custom-normal
normalization/quantization is not bit-identical to source; measured maximum
vector delta is 0.00009713997379265157, recorded in the original capture receipt.
This studio is not the engine toon shader and provides no runtime shading score.

Within each pair, camera, lights, resolution, sample count, seed and floor are
verified equal. Both source hashes and all 46 source-freeze files remain unchanged.
The frozen renderer and initial delivery scripts did not change during capture.

## Builder's visible findings and limits

All nine paired views and hero were inspected on the three paired sheets and hero
board; raw front frames were also opened. Both complete models are visible and
framed. No gross changed silhouette, absent appendage or reversed-facing result
is apparent in these static pairs. The shell/face, braced shield, weapon and
crouched limb silhouette remain recognizably matched to the retained predecessor.
This is a narrow visual observation, not a numerical likeness score.

The meaningful concessions are still NOT accepted by these pictures:

- Fixed torso/head/eyes/shield and locked weapon elbow look unchanged at rest;
  their lost articulation/readability cannot be judged from a static comparison.
- Closed knee/cuff cap surfaces are mostly internal at bind. These images do not
  expose them through a bend or establish absence of armor/cap interference.
- Sole cuts are real source changes of at most the original 7.55/9.53 mm below
  the plane. The original floor hides the sole underside, and the full-body
  frames are insufficient to accept the precise new sole-cap silhouette.
- The broad braced-shield support envelope and obstacle tradeoff are not visible
  collision proof. Static appearance does not admit motion, contact or recovery.

No old source/native score is replaced or inherited. Independent visual review,
articulation range, actual contact/motion and runtime remain pending.
