Unaccepted source-only revision. No build, promotion, visual score, runtime contact,
phone performance or human-fun acceptance. Motion3 and published hosts are unchanged.

## Why this revision exists

The rejected original freeze SHA256 is
`3e4b3f77dd7597ac0d9ae40d3377b00c149a7cdca708ca3ad71ab8f5262eebd3`.
Its complete freeze, source-check, original source/test/admission files and independent

## Authored changes and retained invariants

- Ordinary/counter preparation holds at 40–55% of their existing .60/.45s windups.
  Full alignment finishes 100ms before windup ends and holds until windup+300ms.
  Recovery then retracts through brace at +550ms, small brace at +800ms and rest
  at +1s. These remain authored behavior-clock keys, not new engine phase inputs.
- A −.40rad inward shoulder ready stance shares the active turn with the forearm.
  Active shoulder is −.80rad; active forearm is −.40rad. The measured tip is
  x−.009437, y.883616, z1.986174m in the canonical rig. The weapon approaches from
  different ordinary/counter preparations, rather than changing combat clocks.
- While aligned, only rapier recoil is smoothly suppressed, including residual
  recoil at first contact. Head/shield still react. Outside that window, and after
  a stage cancellation, arm recoil resumes. No pose history or state was added.
- Shell .008sin(1.7t) and head −.015sin(1.7t) idle terms are restored. Shield keeps
  original guard −.24/−.22rad contributions. Stage4 holds that brace after guard
  clears; stage3 starts in the same brace before unwinding toward its counter tell.
  Stage1 guard release at .6s is covered by a continuous authored brace decay.
- Exact motion3 root/leg bytecode, all-joint death poses (including deathT=0),
  geometry, bind, dimensions, lighting, AI clocks, StrikeRunner, damage, pickups,
  rewards and persistence are unchanged. Numeric admission still rejects locals,
  extra pose fields and programs exceeding128 nodes.

## Interruptions remain explicitly unaccepted

At ordinary clock.4, stage1→4 shield X is identical at equal flinch0/.5/1.
Other axes still jump: the largest zero-flinch jump is .391663rad. At counter
clock.485→rest the forearm jump is .40rad at zero recoil (original.80); maximum
axis jump is .48rad. At full recoil the forearm jump is .52rad because recoil
resumes on cancellation. At counter clock.8→rest zero-flinch maximum is .45504rad.
All nine measured interruption comparisons are retained in source-check.json.
These improvements do not establish arbitrary interrupted continuity. Changed
flinch, death interruption, actor transforms and delayed/missed renders still
need real complete-cycle capture. The existing motion3 lower-body sliding,
knockback and death-onset limitations remain. Nominal lane damage is not a swept
blade collider, and valid hits can still occur beyond visible weapon reach.

The freeze verifier now requires runner/fixed-step/pose-schema/evaluator inputs
and the exact rejected manifest in addition to candidate sources. It is still a
local hash closure, not signed provenance or a host/product/build closure. Root
must independently pin this manifest and authorize any diagnostic build/capture.

Reproduce with the focused Vitest contact/preview files and
`node --test scripts/conch-readable-admission.test.mjs`. No full suite, app build,
browser, Git write, Mac action or public-host edit was performed by this revision.
