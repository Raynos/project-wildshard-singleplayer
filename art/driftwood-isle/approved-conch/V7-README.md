# Conch Duelist V7 rebuild

Owner resumed four-enemy likeness work on 19 October 2026, with strictly above 3.5/5 required per enemy. Approved low-poly concept remains authoritative. V7 is an unaccepted actual-model candidate, not gameplay completion.

## Identity targets and real geometry

1. Asymmetric body-height shell: connected angular whorl surface, broad projecting shoulders and tapering tilted apex.
2. Deep flared mouth: excavated shell wall and thick continuous lip returning to a dark throat.
3. Small living coral face: broad brow, hooked keel and inset nostril shapes.
4. Sharp stalk eyes: thick angular leaf rims, inset amber lens and pointed dark slit.
5. Giant defensive claw: broad convex closed shell, orange flank, ivory ridge and separate opposing pincer.
6. Long narrow offensive claw: tapered closed ivory rapier with burgundy forearm and gripping thumb.
7. Crouched crab stance: asymmetric bent legs, overlapping armored thigh/shin masses and planted closed feet.
8. Facet hierarchy: coarse authored shell planes and compact anatomical surfaces, rather than dense uniform noise.

## Source and coordinates

`build_conch_lowpoly_v7.py` is self-contained Blender source; `render_conch_v7_nine.py` reconstructs it for the canonical half-dome. Blender +Z up, +Y front, foot origin at zero. Export uses glTF Y-up. Initial footprint approximately 3.2 × 2.0 m and height 3.7 m; actual metrics supersede these authoring estimates. One elite enemy per encounter is the intended scale, not tested placement.

Separate named anatomical meshes retain elbow/leg/eye/weapon partition boundaries for later pivot/rig work. No rig, motion, collisions, native admission or runtime integration is claimed. The actual triangle cost and nine-view findings must be measured before acceptance. No phone performance proof exists.

Previous sources, failed boards and exports remain unchanged. New GLB, blend scratch and renders belong under `tmp/conch-v7/` until the coordinator chooses storage-compliant delivery. Do not register the model or replace a playable asset merely because the source compiles.
