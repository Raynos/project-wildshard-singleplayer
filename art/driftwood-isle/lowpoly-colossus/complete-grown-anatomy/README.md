## Design and reference discipline

Inspected the original approved lossless hero, corrected v3 World and Explorer
sheets, and each of the retained ten full-resolution actual frames before editing.
The hero controls identity; AI hidden surfaces remain approximate.

Keep the narrow inset cyan eyes and crooked fingers embracing the heart, avoiding
the rejected refined model's broad eye panels and pendant-like exposed crystal.
Rebuild the forearm-to-palm load path and varied inward fingers as broad curved
roots rather than parallel angular hooks. Reduce the dark oval scapular cage by
rerouting the existing back roots; modestly moderate chest depth and arm spread
with the attached stone and foliage following the same whole-body mapping.
Connect the pale cheek blades closer to the nasal keel without making a continuous
beak, and widen calf/ankle root transitions into the existing planted stones.
Growth groups carry broader unequal overlapping leaves and larger rooted masses,
not more repeated triangles or a new roof cap. All 269 named components survive.

## Rig, ownership and scope

Exact retained native bones and edit pivots are preserved, not recentered to the
new geometry. Named component ownership is compared with retained mesh-map.
The established analytical weighting procedure is unchanged. It assigns weights
to the changed vertices, so weights are not claimed byte-identical or accepted
for animation. No new bodies, joints, runtime catalogue, clips, physics, gameplay,
Mac, Site, production asset or other enemy changes.

The GLB remains a static model without skins or animations. Separate editable
mesh and packed native documents permit exact rest reconstruction. Native color
conversion does not represent Blender emission. Consumer tests establish data and
rest geometry parity, not native visual quality, motion, contact or performance.

candidate, each hero plus nine full views, under the same 525x700 cameras,

## Capture inspection and remaining limitations

Individually inspected all twenty fresh original PNGs after rendering, not only
contact sheets. Front and elevated-front retain the crown, narrow eyes, pale
face and root-embraced cyan heart. Side and elevated-side show the broader palm
curvature and more closely seated leaves; back/top expose the changed mass
hierarchy. All geometry stays in frame in both ten-view sets.

Limitations: upper chest still has visibly layered plank-like ribs; the dark
lower-back oval remains only partly broken up. Broad leaf planes can still read
as attached angular caps from above. Fingers are now shorter and closer together,
which may reduce separated-digit readability. The face remains a constructed
mask rather than a fully resolved grown form. These tradeoffs need an independent
full-pair judgment; no likeness score is assigned here.
