## Volume construction

- Upper orbital ridge: a broad inclined top plane and tapered front face. The outer/high end turns back into the forehead; the inner/low end meets the nose bridge. Root vertices lie on the neighboring hull and share its indices. No free posterior corner or thin return strip.
- Lower orbital rim: shallow rounded arc immediately behind the preserved amber lobe. Outer rim flows through two or three broad cheek planes into the body; avoid a thick circular annulus or deep hanging dark pocket.
- Nose: compact peaked upper arch with a short forward hook, retaining nostril-bearing center and mouth corners as spatial guides. The side wings terminate into cheeks rather than extending over the pupils.
- Lower mouth: gently bowed rim whose lower surface turns into the pouch over a broad tapered plane. Retain dark cavity and tongue; remove the separate straight raised rail only when its connected replacement is present.
