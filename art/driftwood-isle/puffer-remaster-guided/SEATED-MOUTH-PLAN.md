# Seated mouth reconstruction

The two continuous-collar studies are rejected visual experiments. Their closed topology did not justify the nasal peaks and folded lower transition. The retained baselinehood body and orbital surfaces are restored exactly for this branch.

Build one closed mouth unit using paired inner/outer rings. Preserve the actual gape and its depth-varying inner arch. Use the original broad five-station upper arch as the visible guide; the lower outer edge is a shallow bow rather than a single low central ridge. Use a few broad quad-derived planes. There is no CDT fan connecting mouth controls to eye corners.

The outer back ring is placed below the actual retained body surface, with signed Euclidean clearance tested at vertices and edge midpoints. The inner return forms the cavity lip; it is not claimed to be buried. Both pupil rays, unchanged eye geometry, original body surface identity, closed edge orientation, nonzero triangle area and positive unit volume are hard gates. The old palate, tongue and nostrils remain as the expression guide. The three old arch/lip/tip components are replaced only after this unit passes.

This is an intentional construction change, not a requirement relaxation: a well-seated separate closed beak can satisfy the art goal. Visual hero/front/side review must establish that it is broad, blunt and attached. No acceptance follows from topology alone. Semantic color-region authoring follows a successful geometry gate.
