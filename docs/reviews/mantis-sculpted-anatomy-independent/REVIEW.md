## Candidate anatomy and remaining likeness gap

- **Head:** Smaller green eyes inside darker rims, stronger brow and a compact lower mask improve the predatory expression. The eyes still read as protruding polygonal gems in profile rather than the reference's integrated almond lenses. The broad head cap, thin projecting mouth pieces and open underside remain less unified than the approved tapered mask.
- **Chest and waist:** Overlapping sternal wedges replace the baseline's conspicuous pale throat strip. This is substantive, but the hero and front still read as a narrow vertical ladder of pieces with dark gaps, not the reference's continuous, layered bark chest. The long, comparatively thin neck and broad hip junction remain the largest proportional gap.
- **Leaf fan:** Long/medium/short leaves create a more complete organic fan. Rear and elevated rear views show useful separation and continuity, rather than only a hero-facing effect. This is closer to the reference's graduated foliage. Some overhead leaf overlap remains visually dense; the original's irregular, naturally distributed mass is not fully recovered.
- **Sickles:** Rotated cutting surfaces have genuine visible depth in left/right profiles and elevated profiles, a major improvement over the baseline's nearly edge-on blades. However, the near sickle in the hero turns its ivory teeth mostly away from the viewer. This is a real view-dependent regression, not a framing defect. The front and elevated front retain strong paired weapon readability, though the thick geometric bows remain less organic than the reference.
- **Legs and abdomen:** Taper and smaller leaf armor are coherent with the body. The narrower pointed abdomen improves the elevated rear silhouette. The front/back still collapse the four legs into near-overlapping pairs, and the hip spread reads broad compared with the approved standing figure. Static floor placement is visible, but does not demonstrate reliable animated contact.
- **Style:** Broad planar green leaves, warm wood and cream edges retain Driftwood's faceted toon language. The large angular limbs and repeated torso modules still feel more constructed than the reference's interlocking bark anatomy. Render grain and different concept lighting are not scored as geometry defects.

## Framing, limits and recommendation

All ten candidate and ten baseline frames show the complete model without clipping antennae, leaf tips, blades or feet. The paired provenance records matching cameras/settings and common union-bounds framing, and projected bounds are inside the 638×900 portrait images. Different hero versus diagnostic scales are disclosed; candidate-versus-baseline comparisons use matched pairs. No apparent improvement depends on cropping away a weak region.

The source and structural checks described by the producer do not raise this visual score. This review does not verify native rendering, articulation clearance, skin deformation, ground contact in motion, attack timing, combat readability, defeat motion or phone performance. It does not authorize replacing the live model or claim the asset is complete.

**Recommendation:** Keep complete-sculpted-anatomy as the strongest static study of this pair. The next meaningful likeness work is an integrated head-to-chest/waist silhouette, followed by a weapon orientation that preserves useful profile depth without losing the near hero cutting face. Preserve the stronger fan, abdomen taper and reduced eye scale. Do not present 3.79 as meeting a 4/5 gate.
