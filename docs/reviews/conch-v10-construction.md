# Conch Duelist V10 construction study

2026-10-19. Unaccepted static model study; no runtime registration or gameplay claim.

## Why the shell is rebuilt

The fresh V9 independent review scored 3.1375/5. Its central finding was structural: a narrow striped cone and separate aperture plate did not reproduce the approved original's broad, oblique, overlapping shoulder masses. V10 therefore replaces the whole shell construction rather than adjusting V9's radius, rim width or material values.

## Authored construction

- Eleven deliberately asymmetric polygonal control sections define a broad lower whorl, two smaller receding whorls and the tapered apex.
- Each shoulder has substantial depth and a pronounced oblique rise. Broad chalk-colored sloping shoulder planes replace the narrow regular stripes.
- One rectangular patch of the exterior cage is omitted. Its sixteen boundary vertices continue directly into the flared lip and then into the recessed throat. The shell, lip and cavity are one connected mesh.
- The mouth has actual depth and walls. It has no separate aperture plate, Boolean excavation, solidify modifier, clipped basal collar or internal decorative ribbon.
- V9's body, eyes, limbs and weapons remain unchanged to isolate the largest shape correction. Their known likeness weaknesses remain open.

## Preflight

Python syntax checks passed for the builder, nine-view renderer and board generator. A dependency-free source-mesh edge check found 244 shell vertices and 435 polygons, with zero boundary edges and zero edges having more than two incident faces. This is a source check, not a substitute for the actual exported GLB audit, rendered-view inspection, self-intersection testing or independent likeness score.
