It replaces the shell control profiles with taller sloping shoulder transitions, narrower upper whorls and nonplanar oblique shoulder planes. Coral/chalk triangular regions interrupt continuous painted belts without adding surface noise.

The cranium and cheeks taper into a narrower hooked face. Slender raised stalks carry more oblique pointed eye leaves, with slimmer housing borders and larger inset golden lens areas. The shield becomes an elongated diagonal shell-claw with a substantial convex coral back and broad off-center ivory cutting ridge. Its entire perimeter and reverse remain modeled. These coordinated silhouette, expression and shield changes are authored geometry, not render overlays.
