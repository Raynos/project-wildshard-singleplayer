# Tide-Root Colossus complete pass 3 construction

Status: unaccepted static study, 2026-10-19. No author score or runtime admission.

## Complete reconstruction

Starts from the preserved complete-pass2 editable source and retains its deeply
interwoven body, embedded stone anatomy and hooked root hands. Reconstructs the
vegetation as three-dimensional lobed bunches with offset double ridges and
unequal folded trailing shoots. These overlap across collar, shoulders, back,
elbows, lateral arms, hips, thighs, shins and inner ankles. The shoulder mantle
has depth and an uneven hanging perimeter rather than a thin roof or a row of
identical aprons. Growth remains attached to the wood anatomy from all views.

The split face is shortened below the eye line while the recessed stern eyes
and branching forehead remain intact. Crown geometry uses two unequal crooked
upright trunks and differently directed forks rather than a mirrored fan.
The chest heart has a staggered perimeter and multiple offset front planes,
with the original root clasps still reaching across its edges.

The approved catalogue figure is the visual target. The side/back panels are
corroborating references; unseen anatomy is a constructed interpretation.
These changes do not establish that the visual likeness gate has passed.

## Reproduction and evidence

Editable source and construction recipe are in
`art/driftwood-isle/lowpoly-colossus/complete-pass3/`.
`construct_source.py` reads the preserved pass2 source and deterministically
writes the pass3 source. Blender executes `build_colossus_complete.py`.
`render_matched.py` rebuilds retained pass1 and pass3 and captures an actual hero
plus nine views for each with the same fixed lighting, camera target, projection,
resolution and samples. `validate_glb.py` independently reads exported GLB
accessors, topology and dependency declarations. `package_evidence.py` creates
labelled compressed previews and exact hash receipts.

Static structural validation checks closed component topology, consistent
winding, nonzero triangle area, positive component volumes, finite attributes,
index bounds and no external resource dependencies. These checks do not measure
visual likeness, inter-component intersections, rigging, deformation, collision,
animation, runtime import, performance or phone quality.

Pass1/pass2 and the failed earlier foliage construction remain untouched.
No runtime, AI, physics, Site or plan-state files are changed.

read as a regular row of tent-shaped peaks. The complete initial build source,
GLB, validation and ten actual labelled frames are preserved in `failed-initial/`,
with raw capture hashes in its closure. It is failed construction history,
not a competing accepted version and not an independently scored candidate.

the two ridge spans and rotates each lobe independently. The mesh/triangle
counts remain unchanged. The initial full 20-view render finished in 217.25
seconds at approximately 14:24:35 UTC. The separate browser recording ended at 14:25:39 UTC. Corrected candidate-only
capture was started after the coordinator released that boundary, preserving
