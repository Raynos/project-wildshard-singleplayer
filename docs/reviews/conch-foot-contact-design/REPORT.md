# Conch authoritative foot-contact design

2026-10-20. Design only, frozen after this report. Read AGENTS.md, current DRIFTWOOD-ENEMIES plan/state, the articulated-native design and selected-complete-motion independent refusal. Coordinated read-only inspection with the native adapter owner. No engine/source edits, test execution, physics step, build, Git operation or public write. The rejected 135-channel candidate remains unchanged. This document proposes a bounded extension; none of its new contracts are implemented or admitted by this review.

## Decision

Reuse NativeCreaturePose as the single contact-state writer, with exactly two declared three-joint chains and immutable per-foot observations. Do not integrate motion.total into a supposed contact solver. The smallest defensible first admission is the actual isolated arena's static planar floor, bounded root motion and explicit loss-of-support handling. Arbitrary uneven terrain and arbitrary shoves cannot be promised by a two-foot state addition alone.

Three consequential boundaries must be resolved before implementation is called a complete solution:

- Current local-joint/root interpolation does not preserve a planted-foot IK constraint between endpoints. Endpoint contact tests alone cannot certify rendered contact.
- The living root/motor is not a leg dynamics solver. A sufficiently large shove or terrain/root-height change makes a fixed plant unreachable. Preserve the real shove and release the plant; never stretch, move the actor back, raise it or silently claim planted support.
- Height samples and ankle rays do not prove finite sole support. The current isolated adapter deliberately supplies a constant floor; wider terrain needs an explicit surface/patch contract and separate admission.

## Inspected authority, not a new owner

`src/game/practice/PracticeNativeActor.ts` constructs one native actor, borrowed decision/strike control and NativeCreaturePose. Its existing fixed.post calls clock.tick(FIXED_STEP) and stepEmbedded; the body-step after-hook calls pose.advance then publish. Existing fixed.pre and the arena retain corpse preparation and the sole world step. The adapter supplies heightAt/groundHeight as spawn.at.y and levelGround=true. This is a flat-floor fixture, not evidence of current terrain support. It registers no world-save/reward owner.

`src/engine/entities/AnimalSim.ts` applies heading, forward/strafe, stagger and impulse, then CharacterMotor correction, then its terrain-height/fall law. Grounded motor movement ignores ground and Y is restored to groundY+yOffset; smoothed ground follow owns vertical placement. AnimalMotor.move returns void. CharacterMotor has a richer result including grounded, normal-Y and collider, but that result is neither per-foot support nor exposed by this narrow port. Root terrain tilt also exists through AnimalPoseLaw/applyAnimalRoot. Contact targets must use the actual full root matrix and scale, not just actor yaw.

`src/game/plugin/animation.ts` owns previous/current locals/root, exact entry publication, motion delta XYZ, unsigned 3D distance/total and wrapped heading delta. advance updates history before the custom evaluator; applyAnimalRoot currently happens after it. prepareAppliedHit stages detached zero-time evaluation and commits only validated publication. Corpse preparation/publication is separate. resetMotionAnchor is explicit diagnostic placement, not a shove heuristic. snapshot/restore are quiet; physical pose-only restore is currently refused.

`channelPrograms.ts` snapshots declared reads before writes. Practice input admission allows frame, transition, motion, entry and strike fields, but no foot ports. Outputs are joint position/rotation/scale only, one target per program. Private locals and restoreOnly are forbidden. `clientChannels.ts` caps programs/nodes/reads/channels at 512/128/32/12; sqrt, atan2 and basic arithmetic exist, acos/quaternion operations are not primitive nodes. Do not invent a nonexistent foot input or increase caps to squeeze in a solver.

`physics/query.ts` already provides floorBelow (height only) and castRay (point, normal, collider). These are engine-owned queries, not permission to install authored terrain callbacks. A fixed number of point casts still cannot certify an entire non-planar support patch.

## Source and anatomical prerequisite

Create a separate articulated source, not a modification of the frozen failure. Bake B=Ry(pi), (x,y,z)->(-x,y,-z), consistently into source positions, normals, bind pivots, sockets, directional metadata and local rotation frames (B R B^-1). Actor yaw zero is +Z forward, +X remains its declared left direction. Do not add a display-only yaw offset. Preserve skin/FK commutation under B at nonzero yaw and scale.

Retain hip, knee and ankle on each leg. Explicitly reauthor the continuous blended hinges as closed rigid pieces with knee/ankle seams and joint collars; never relabel the old blended corners by dominant weight. Give each rigid foot a finite sole polygon, sole-to-ankle transform, authored forward tangent and support plane. Reauthor actual toes/sole geometry where necessary, recording changed vertices; do not move the root to absorb the old 43.462600 mm witness. Source bind lengths previously measured are L=.533010/.548999 m and R=.538609/.548999 m; recompute exact lengths from the new freeze, do not use rounded values in admission.

Eight components mean six leg parts, one braced torso/head/eyes/shield assembly and one shoulder-driven entire rapier arm. This sacrifices head/shield independence and the weapon elbow. The wide shield union's obstacle envelope is a real cost, not a free grouping trick. Require independent anatomy and nine-angle review, newly derived hulls within unchanged 8/128/512, all-weight coverage and visible seam continuity. These are source gates, not consequences of successful IK.

## Minimal typed declaration and continuation

Names below are proposed types, not existing APIs. Register through the existing strict practice numeric declaration and include the full declaration/source identity in the pose recipe identity. Exactly two chains, no arbitrary number of limbs or callback entry points.

- FootContactRecipeV1: owner='isolated-practice'; surface='static-plane-v1'; left/right chain joint names; exact bind transforms/segment lengths; admitted joint ranges; fixed knee-pole branch in actor frame; sole-to-ankle rigid transform; finite sole polygon/support vertices; nominal landing frame; positive fixed swing duration; clearance trajectory envelope; declared reach/turn release guards derived from admitted motion bounds; deterministic left-first tie break.
- Validate unique joints, actual parent chain and source identity; positive lengths and uniform positive actor scale; rigid joint scales one and fixed bone translations; finite, unit normals/quaternions; finite nondegenerate sole polygon. Admit only constant living ancestor transforms above the hips for this first variant. Otherwise an upper-body pose can invalidate a solve performed before numeric evaluation.
- ContactStateV1, privately owned by NativeCreaturePose: enabled/released status; fixed tick ordinal; last full root endpoint; next-foot tie break; exactly two FootStates. Each FootState stores mode ('stance'|'swing'|'released'), step ordinal, fixed-step elapsed, plant frame, swing start/end frames, and the admitted static surface identity/revision. Store prior/current contact endpoints for interpolation. No separate time source, RNG or actor.mem entries.
- A plant frame is world position plus unit quaternion. Its local +Y is the support normal and local +Z the projected forward tangent. Store a declared surface id, not a transient Rapier handle in persistent data. In static-plane-v1 the plane id/revision and coefficients are supplied by the trusted existing arena owner and validated against its floor, not authored code.
- Read-only numeric observations: contact.enabled/status/tick and foot.left/right {mode, step, phase, supported}; target ankle position/quaternion in the actual root frame; solved hip/knee/ankle local Euler XYZ in the recipe's declared orders. All are finite detached copies. Keep diagnostic world frames on a typed quiet inspection port, not a reflected mutable actor object.

Prefer one small pure two-bone solver internal to NativeCreaturePose over duplicating quaternion/FK algebra across 135 independent output programs. This is a new, reviewable bounded owner algorithm, not an arbitrary animation callback, physics constraint or service. The new numeric source reads the solved local rotations using direct-copy lanes for the six living leg joints. Strict admission rejects competing living leg position/scale/rotation formulas. Existing DAG caps stay unchanged. The trusted helper has no scene, clock, RNG, body-write or physics-step capability.

For the initial variant, living body/hip-ancestor transforms stay bind-constant; attack/hit use the braced upper assembly/weapon joints without changing that chain. If required recoil rotates/translates an ancestor, explicitly extend staged ancestor-FK evaluation before solving; do not use yesterday's root or assume that a whole-pose hit blend preserves contact.

## Fixed-step state law

1. After the real body move, obtain actual root position, yaw, terrain tilt and scale for this tick. Stage that complete transform before solving, using the same applyAnimalRoot law that will be published. This requires an intentional reorder/staging inside advance, because the existing evaluator currently precedes applyAnimalRoot. Do not publish or tick early. Compute displacement and wrapped heading from authoritative endpoints; keep motion.total unchanged for compatibility but never treat it as foot phase.
2. For each stance foot, retain its world plant frame exactly. Transform the sole frame through inverse(actual root), then through the inverse prospective hip-parent transform, and derive the ankle target using the declared sole-to-ankle transform. Scale is included exactly once. No continual projection/re-snapping of a stance plant.
3. Check geometric reach, joint limits, knee-pole conditioning and source-wide ground clearance. For hip H, target ankle A, d=|A-H| must satisfy |l1-l2|<d<l1+l2 for the nonsingular admitted domain. Equality is a singular boundary, not a default straight-leg pose. Set u=(A-H)/d, a=(l1^2-l2^2+d^2)/(2d), h=sqrt(l1^2-a^2), v=normalized(pole-u*dot(pole,u)); K=H+a*u+h*v. The fixed sign of v preserves the knee branch. Derive bone rotations from the actual bind segment axes and declared bend frames, not assumed Y-aligned bones. Resolve ankle rotation so world sole orientation equals the plant orientation. Check limits after solving; reject materially negative radicands or singular poles rather than clamping unreachable input into success.
4. Start a step when root-relative stance reach/turn/nominal-foot displacement crosses the declared release guard, including yaw-only movement and lateral/reverse displacement. Guards and allowed speed/yaw/shove envelopes are source admission inputs, not runtime parameter chasing. At most one normal swing; choose the more constrained foot by normalized reach/turn margin, exact ties by stored alternation. Capture the current foot frame, choose the next nominal frame from the current actual root and admitted static floor, then latch it. Do not continuously chase a moving target.
5. Swing advances by existing dt/FIXED_STEP, not by requested speed or unsigned path length. This lets an already lifted foot finish after the actor stops. Use S(u)=10u^3-15u^4+6u^5 for positional/orientation progress and a declared lift term 64*u^3*(1-u)^3. Both endpoint velocities and accelerations vanish; orientation uses shortest quaternion interpolation with S. A zero-velocity stance release is continuous. Choose lift from the proved sole/whole-foot clearance envelope, never arbitrary repeated tuning. Validate the entire path and the current IK reach; these formulas alone are not clearance proof.
6. Land only at phase one with a still valid surface and reachable endpoint. Set stance and copy that endpoint as the immutable plant; increment step ordinal exactly once. A stopped actor retains both plants once settled, with no phase drift. A blocked motor does not advance cyclic gait merely because speed is nonzero. A turn in place can demand alternating steps even with zero root translation.
7. If a shove/root jump exceeds the support domain before a normal release can preserve reach, mark the affected foot released. Do not pretend it remains a loaded contact. If both are invalid, publish explicit unsupported/recovery status and retain real actor motion. A controlled recovery animation/landing must be separately authored and admitted; this design does not invent a safe fallback pose or imply that 'released' is itself visually acceptable. Lack of a valid recovery is a candidate admission failure for that shove range, not permission to disable damage/pushes.

Stance is a kinematic no-slip constraint, not a simulated force-bearing leg. The current living motor does not enforce balance, center-of-mass support or ground reaction through these feet. Label observations accordingly; a single foot's planted geometry does not prove dynamic stability.

## Terrain boundary

The minimum implementation above certifies only a trusted finite static planar support region, not an infinite plane invented from a ray. The sole polygon must lie in that region and every retained foot vertex must be on/above it; swing/body/weapon geometry must also avoid the actual finite world obstacles.

A later bounded terrain contract may expose a detached point, unit normal, persistent surface id/revision and certified finite support polygon from the existing physics owner. Use the current engine cast/query machinery, exclude self/sensors, distinguish floor from overhead decks, and preserve undefined/no-floor as absence. Do not fall back to heightAt underneath a missing deck and call it contact. A planar sole cannot have full-area contact with arbitrary non-planar ground. It needs an admitted planar patch or a separately specified partial/rocker support law. Different left/right planes also require reach feasibility against the motor's actual smoothed root Y. Moving platforms require surface-local anchors and authoritative surface transforms; reject them in static-plane-v1 rather than sliding a world anchor. No authored ground callbacks or extra world steps.

## Attack, hit, death and rendering

Strike remains the borrowed exact phase/elapsed/index owner. Starting an attack does not reset plants. Finish an in-progress swing under the same contact law; any intentional attack step is a typed declared foot transition, not an inferred strike-time sine. This first contract has no intentional attack step. Weapon telegraph, active damage lane and counter strike need independent source-to-actor/FK checks.

Nonlethal prepareAppliedHit clones contact state and samples it without advancing phase or doing ground queries. Entry captures the actually published pose. Zero-time recoil must preserve the six solved living leg transforms; blending them toward an unrelated hit pose would break plants. Subsequent shove is consumed on the next ordinary actual-body tick. Same-timestamp repeated hits keep ordinal order without consuming foot time.

Lethal preparation captures the exact current local pose/root, marks contacts released only in its staged candidate and uses the existing corpse transaction. Refusal/abort leaves HP, joints, plants, ordinals and root unchanged. Successful commit releases both feet and transfers root ownership once. No support solver runs during corpse folding; pre/post corpse scheduling and one physics step remain unchanged. Do not replant a corpse, teleport it or use a foot plane to modify its physical root.

Important limitation: independent root/local quaternion interpolation in copyPublished generally moves a nominally planted foot between two exact IK endpoints. Strict all-alpha contact requires a reviewed constraint-preserving read-only sampler: interpolate the authoritative root/contact trajectory endpoints, then run the same pure IK on detached render joints. No ground query, phase advance, RNG, event or authoritative write is allowed. This is derived presentation, not a second clock, but it does change the current 'copy endpoints only' consumer contract and must be explicitly reviewed. Alternatively retain current interpolation and report its measured slip/penetration without claiming exact contact; that alternative does not meet a strict planted-foot requirement. Discrete stance/swing boundaries must be represented in the published contact endpoints so the sampler never interpolates between unrelated plants.

## Lifecycle and continuation

Initial spawn validates the actual source/plane and establishes reachable plants once at owner initialization, with previous=current and zero step elapsed. Initial solving is not advance(0). Invalid spawn fails before partial attachment.

Placement/Retry/re-entry are explicit transactions: preflight new root/plane/initial plants, then atomically reset actor/motor, contact state, motion anchors and both publications. Do not use displacement magnitude to infer a teleport. The current place sequence mutates actor before resetMotionAnchor; adding a fallible contact initialization requires preflight before that mutation. Physical placement stays refused.

Add a versioned bounded contact envelope to the existing pose continuation, retaining bit-exact floats where the owner already does. Include source/recipe/plane identity, all plants and swing endpoints, phase/tick ordinals, tie break and prior/current root/contact endpoints. Restore validates fully into detached storage before copying; no terrain query/evaluator/clock/RNG/world step. Version without contact is not silently reconstructed as a mid-stride contact continuation. Fresh restart is distinct from exact restore and may initialize anew only through explicit spawn/Retry semantics. Physical pose-only restoration remains unsupported until the existing combined physical host restore contract supports it; this report does not waive that blocker. Disposal removes no unrelated actor or shared service and leaves no detached contact owner alive.

## Exact acceptance tests to add, not tests run here

1. Declaration refusal: wrong chain/order, missing or repeated joints, malformed plane/polygon, inconsistent lengths, nonuniform/zero scale, mixed living leg output, callback/local input, unknown port, singular bind or source identity mismatch. Assert no partial publication/resource allocation. Keep all existing cap tests unchanged.
2. Analytic plane: source-derived lengths and sole points, actual scale 1 and another admitted scale, yaw 0/pi/2/pi and wrapped yaw crossing. For each supported endpoint assert FK ankle and sole frame equal the latched world frame to a documented floating-point arithmetic bound; every foot/sole vertex and all body/weapon vertices pass signed plane bounds. Bound is numerical error, not permissible penetration or added collider skin.
3. Paths: forward, reverse actual displacement, left/right strafe, arc, yaw-only turn, abrupt stop/start and motor-blocked intended motion. Verify root-local target changes while the stance world frame stays fixed; turn-only can release; blocked translation alone cannot cycle; active swing completes after stop. Assert identical result under equal fixed-tick sequences with different render cadence.
4. Reach failures: exact inner/outer singular boundaries, just-outside reach, pole parallelism, joint-limit failure, maximum admitted shove in every direction, and an above-domain shove. Preserve actual displacement/damage. Above-domain must report support loss, never stretch, root compensation or a false stance. Recovery must pass separately before gameplay admission.
5. Surface: planar ramps only after that surface mode is admitted; floor edge, absent floor, deck above another floor, surface revision/removal, toe extending beyond patch, obstacle beneath swinging toe and mixed-height feet. Point-ray success cannot override a footprint failure. Unsupported modes refuse admission explicitly.
6. Combat: both actual strike identities at each phase boundary and interior, hits during stance and swing, hit on the last active tick, two hits at one timestamp, repeated hit during recoil, lethal at every phase and after final pose tick. Assert no zero-time foot-phase advance, leg-contact preservation on nonlethal entry, exact lethal entry, one release/one corpse handoff, and transactional refusal. Run actual AI/weapons, damage timing and counter behavior separately from these pose assertions.
7. Interpolation: alpha 0/.25/.5/.75/1, plus analytic/adaptive extrema for every admitted swing/stance segment and turn; endpoints alone are insufficient. Compare current interpolation against the fixed plant witness first. Constraint sampler must retain exact endpoints, preserve stance frame, clear the entire sole and keep snapshots byte-identical after repeated/out-of-order reads. Dense samples are diagnostics, not a universal continuum proof.
8. Quiet-read instrumentation: repeated snapshot, native diagnostics, jointPoint/headWorld/bodyCapsule and copy/samplePublished must cause zero body steps, terrain queries, strike ticks, pose-time changes, RNG reads, event flushes, foot transitions or physics steps. Normal fixed tick increments contact tick once; damage publication increments hit ordinal but no contact tick. Rendering 0/1/many times cannot change continuation.
9. Save/restore/Retry: exact continuation at double stance, mid-swing, pending hit, lethal entry and supported physical restore boundary. Compare future endpoint/contact/strike/RNG bytes after replay. Malformed or mismatched contact restore must leave all owners untouched. Fresh Retry never retains prior-bout anchors; explicit placement does not inflate motion.total. Check repeated create/dispose for zero owner/resource leak.
10. Final separate gates: newly authored source strict native admission; actual isolated finite-world contact; independent likeness and animation/readability; AI/weapons/damage; loot/reward-once; player defeat/Retry/reload and no world-persistence contamination. Existing canonical or frozen failed-source tests do not substitute. Phone performance and six-shard Explorer coverage remain separate pending evidence.

## Stop condition and recommendation

Proceed only after reviewing the source anatomy concession and the three bounded architecture changes: contact state in the existing pose owner, staged actual root before solving, and constraint-preserving quiet presentation sampling. First implement/prove the flat static arena subset with explicit support-loss diagnostics and the actual admitted shove envelope. If the source cannot satisfy that envelope or required anatomy, stop that candidate rather than chase parameters. Uneven terrain, moving support, physical restore and visual quality are named pending gates, not inferred successes. Support release never licenses below-plane skin: every released/recovery endpoint and its complete interpolation must retain nonpenetration for the admitted root/shove envelope. A released pose without that proof is a failed source admission, not a runtime fallback accepted by status alone. Refuse the candidate before gameplay if its actual existing shove range cannot be covered; do not cancel or weaken the shove. General-terrain completeness does not block a valid bounded shared-Arena proof, and requires no new terrain service for the trusted static-plane subset. No Bellback multibody change, cap increase, collider padding, root lift, extra physics step or authored callback follows from this report.
