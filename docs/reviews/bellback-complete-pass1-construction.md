# Bellback complete static pass 1

19 October 2026. Construction WIP; fresh independent likeness review pending.
The current target is at least 4/5 in each separate likeness, animation and
combat-readability gate. No score is assigned by this builder.

## Evidence and audit before construction

The original approved `art/driftwood-isle/concepts/lowpoly-revision-2026-10-18/bellback-lowpoly.webp`
was inspected in both depicted views, alongside the actual V6 hero, V3–V6
editable generators, and the V5 and V6 independent reviews. The concept's
oblique view is authoritative; hidden back/top surfaces remain inferred.

V3 to V4 primarily thickened legs, shortened carrier tips and reduced teeth.
V5 added a closed cranium, broad shoulder anatomy and rigid pivots, but buried
three of four eye samples under its brow and retained a loose crown edge.
V6 corrected those defects and sole grounding, earning 3.5625/5 independently.
Its 14% head-depth compression amplified the shallow mask, while repeated
upper/lower rails, paired posts and an exposed barrel end retained a tidy cage.
The V6 review specifically identifies face, carrier density and bell profile
as outstanding. All earlier source and failed evidence remains unchanged.

## Complete geometry, facet and palette pass

New independent source lives under `art/driftwood-isle/lowpoly-bellback/complete-pass1/`.
It preserves the full V6 construction and changes its coherent silhouette:

1. The entire registered head projects 10% from its original uncompressed neck
   depth and narrows 7%; brow, eyes, cheek bones and teeth travel together.
2. Long seated forehead plates break up the broad mask. Paired ivory forehead
   horns become short low broken timber splints.
3. Ten uneven tapered teeth seat separately into the timber jaw and upper mouth.
   The continuous cream W-shaped plate and two dominant tusks are removed only
   in this new candidate. The retained V6 is untouched.
4. Kinked upper and lower wales overlap thicker diagonal wreck braces and broad
   hull strakes. The stern has split canted boards and a transverse brace.
5. The bell axis is 22 degrees rather than 30 degrees, with a straighter upper
   shoulder and less rounded crown. Actual inner wall, mouth and clapper remain.
6. Broad shoulder caps descend toward the wrists; grounded stone soles and
   quadruped articulation remain. Warm timber colors unify the larger planes.

These are construction intentions, not proof of improved likeness. Actual
matched hero and all nine views are required before independent evaluation.
No camera-dependent geometry, image generation, repaint or game capture is used.

## Scope and reproduction

The new builder is a standalone editable Blender source. The fixed capture
script derives unchanged cameras, lights, color transform and sample counts
from V6. Use the shared heavy build lane, two Blender threads, and `--shots hero`
or `--shots nine`, passing `--source build_bellback_complete.py`. The builder
exports `bellback-complete-pass1.glb` and `metrics.json`; the capture additionally
records exact source and script hashes, vertex bounds, per-component topology,
image hashes and eye-ray diagnostics.

This is static art only. No collider, physics, engine, runtime, Site or shared
State file changes. Seven semantic pivots are retained as editable hierarchy,
not a validated rig. No animation clips, full-cycle motion, contact, combat,
physical-phone performance or user acceptance is claimed. The separate
five-body/four-joint prototype remains awaiting its specific approval.

## First actual evidence

The matched hero finished successfully from source SHA256
`5cb28ba4a823291a749498c63be5b2a07a16e2915245bd4f7483e6cc2aefe464`.
The native GLB is 357,844 bytes, 4,726 triangles, 88 meshes, 96 nodes,
199 material primitives and 15 materials. Header/length, finite attributes,
index bounds and self-contained buffers pass. There are zero skins or clips.
All 88 source components have zero nonmanifold edges and zero zero-area faces;
minimum world Z is exactly zero. Both eyes have 3/4 visible sampled lens points
in the matched hero; the remaining point is blocked by its own socket edge.
These diagnostics establish neither runtime admission nor visual quality.

The builder's hero inspection flags two risks for the independent reviewer:
the straighter bell covers more of the supporting roof arch, and separate
teeth appear thinner than the approved image's chunky broken bite. The original
V6 remains the accepted historical fallback; no promotion is implied.
