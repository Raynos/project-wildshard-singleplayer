## Original-concept baseline

Both depictions in `art/driftwood-isle/concepts/lowpoly-revision-2026-10-18/conch-duelist-lowpoly.webp` were inspected. The character combines a towering asymmetrically tapering coral/cream conch, flared opening, small sharp predatory head, slender unevenly rising gold slit-eye stalks, burgundy crouched anatomy, broad pointed shell-claw shield, and slender ivory opposing weapon. Its palette is warmer and more locally varied than the new model's broad pale bands. Environment, UI and lighting are not likeness requirements. The unseen rear and top are unknown; those new views test plausible continuation and model coherence rather than fidelity to an invented authoritative rear view.

## Retained predecessor comparison

Individually inspected `tmp/conch-character-pass/color-front.png` and every `color-character-pass-{front,left,right,back,top,diag-front,diag-left,diag-right,diag-back}.png`.

- Hero/front: complete-pass1 has a narrower sharper head, slimmer eye presentation and a more elongated shield. These move toward the original's pointed predatory anatomy. The predecessor's wider orange face and eye plaques are more mask-like. Both retain the same broad cavity and armored body language.
- Left/right: complete-pass1 reduces the predecessor's abrupt shell shelves. The improvement is more credible here than in a selected hero. It does not eliminate the broad horizontal collar/shoulder shapes or high heavy rear mass.
- Back/diag-back: the predecessor has particularly broad nearly uniform orange belts and disconnected-looking shelf contours. Complete-pass1 has more irregular triangular color breakup and less abrupt turn transitions. Its rear bulb and concentric construction remain obvious.
- Top/diag-right: predecessor has a particularly broad disk-like pale crown and simple orange tip patch. Complete-pass1 adds an uneven apex/spiral presentation and local color breaks, though the pale disk remains a major shape.
- Diag-front: new slimmer facial taper and less shelf-like upper shell help, while neither candidate matches the concept's organic spiral and expressive stalk tension.
- Diag-left: the new shell's taper is less like stacked plates; side-eye flatness, segmented limbs and blunt shell-body relationship persist.
- Tradeoff: the predecessor's broad clean color blocks are very readable. Complete-pass1's added small coral triangles can look like decorative patchwork rather than natural faceted volume, so added variation does not automatically earn a large style gain. Its shield is longer, but remains flat-edged and blade-like compared with the reference claw.

**Best of these two: complete-pass1.** Preserve character-pass as the retained comparator; this review does not authorize replacing a runtime asset. Recommend another targeted likeness iteration rather than labeling this a completed replica: prioritize shell sweep and shoulder rhythm, then living eye/face expression and less mechanical shield/body transitions.
