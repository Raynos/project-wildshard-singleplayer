## Method

Overall /5 = silhouette and proportions × 35% + face/expression × 30% + defining details × 20% + facet/style × 15%. Scores are comparative expert judgments, not calibrated pixel similarity. Differences under about 0.2 should be treated as close calls. Missing deliberately deferred details count against the complete visual match.

## Limits and omissions

- All versions judged against the approved low-poly target, including older detailed and clay studies originally made for different goals.
- Deliberately deferred eyes, crown, mouth and other defining details count as missing in this overall likeness ranking. This is not a judgment of whether an anatomy test achieved its narrower purpose.
- Blender dark studio and img2threejs white-background cameras, projection and lighting differ. Color warmth/saturation is not a separate score; no color-transfer correction or new render was made.
- V24 warmed its palette before runtime comparison. That warmth earns no special credit.
- Most early Blender versions have only one hero view. Their unseen sides are unverified, not assumed sound. Available multiview defects count; confidence is consequently unequal.
- V20/V21 are the same geometry. Both version labels are listed, tied, and their combined front/hero/side images form one evidence set.
- No animation, runtime performance, gameplay acceptance or export quality is scored. All candidates remain unaccepted.
- Owner preference for Blender V9 and img2threejs V8 is consistent with this independent comparison; scores were assigned from the visual rubric, not recency.
- Blender clay V6 and low-poly V25 are source-only: no actual render exists at cutoff.
- There is no separate rendered low-poly V1–V6 sequence in this inventory: the earlier lineage is initial detailed V1 and clay V2–V6.
- Comparison boards, durable WEBP copies, reference masks/crops and duplicate exports are not separate candidates.
- Existing production gameplay audit screenshots are contextual evidence, not versions in these reconstruction experiments, and are outside this version ranking.
