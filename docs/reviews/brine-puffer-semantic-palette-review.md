# Brine Puffer: independent semantic palette review

**Status:** `unread` 2026-10-19 — Reject the current region layout; retain the flat-color painting approach.
**Link:** This report.
**Plan:** driftwood-enemies
**Made by:** Independent visual review, requested by the project coordinator.

## Verdict — 2026-10-19, 02:48 UTC

**Reject the current semantic color-region layout.** Flat colors reduce the
earlier teal/orange smears and dark under-eye streaks, and the angry eyes read
clearly. However, the orange becomes a broad mask with square stair-step temple
boundaries, especially in hero and side. A tiny isolated teal triangle within
the orange temple reads as an accidental leftover. Clean flatness alone does
not establish fidelity to the approved concept.

Retain the flat-color method and the seated-mouth geometry. Keep the short
orange brow, but taper its adjoining cheek region along one or two sloped
anatomical facet boundaries. Remove the rectangular steps and absorb the teal
island into the surrounding region. Do not use a broad horizontal mask simply
because it is easier to partition. No geometry reset is requested by this
paint-only finding.

## Direct observations

- **Hero:** Cleaner eye/hood readability, but the orange temple climbs in hard
  square steps and the isolated teal triangle is conspicuous.
- **Front:** Anger survives; the upper orange band now reads as a near-continuous
  mask. The lower white pouch remains clear, and no rejected nasal star returns.
- **Side:** The square upper temple step and isolated teal patch are strongest.
  The boundary does not follow the reference's tapered cheek/brow forms.

## Tapered orange revision — 2026-10-19, 02:52 UTC

**Orange cleanup succeeds, but reject the full layout for integration until
the cream boundary is corrected.** The isolated teal island is gone and orange
is more local to the eyes. Front keeps the angry read. Hero/side now expose a
large rectangular cream patch, with a nearly horizontal top and abrupt
vertical rear cutoff. This reads as a white bib attached to the flank rather
than the reference's cheek transition wrapping diagonally toward the fin root.

Preserve the compact orange brow/eye region. Replace the cream L-corner with a
continuous rearward-descending diagonal cheek seam. A narrow tapered ochre
accent can accompany that seam where useful; do not restore the previous broad
mask. The issue is region layout, not a need to alter the underlying volume.

## Diagonal cream boundary — 2026-10-19, 02:55 UTC

**Retain the diagonal layout as the best of these palette studies.** Hero/side
lose the rectangular cream bib; the seam wraps diagonally toward the forefin.
Compact orange and clear angry eyes remain, with no small teal island or
interpolated smear returning. The cheek seam is still a long straight edge,
and ochre is more confined to the orbit than the reference's broader cheek
accent. Those limitations remain explicit; this is not a whole-model score or
performance acceptance.
