# Colossus complete-pass1: independent static review

Status: unread, 2026-10-19. Static study only; no runtime or animation acceptance.

## Verdict

Recommend **complete-pass1 narrowly over retained V4** for static concept likeness.
Complete-pass1 scores exactly **3.765/5**; my independent V4 comparison scores
exactly **3.685/5**. This is a modest improvement, not a close-match final approval.
Keep both preserved; taste remains the owner's decision.

## Method and independence

Read AGENTS.md, GIT.md, MOCKUPS.md and the reviews README. The authoritative
reference is the original low-poly concept, especially its large catalogue
figure; its action panel corroborates the character, not a required neutral pose.
AI-derived hidden angles are approximate and cannot dictate unseen anatomy.
Inspected the original, actual complete-pass1 hero and every one of its nine
individual full-frame PNGs. Froze initial-assessment.json before inspecting V4
or reading prior scores or design explanations. Then inspected the retained V4
hero and all nine individual full-frame PNGs. No board substituted for full views.
The capture receipt was read before scoring solely for provenance.

No rounding, threshold adjustment or cross-character averaging was used.

- Silhouette: branching crown, wide shoulders, hanging arms, narrow hips and
  planted root-stone feet immediately establish the intended guardian. The
  shoulders now carry substantial leafy volume. The body still reads as an
  orderly segmented humanoid: clean parallel fingers and relatively straight
  shins replace some of the original's irregular grown-root rhythm. Side depth
  is credible but less hunched and layered than the concept.
- Face: cyan slanted eyes and aggressive brow read clearly in front and forward
  diagonals. The face is a broad continuous pointed shield/beak, with an unusually
  bright central forehead triangle, rather than the concept's narrower layered,
  split bark mask. The expression works; the specific face construction drifts.
- Details: cyan core, encircling root ribs, antlers, foliage and stone toes all
  survive full-view inspection. Added canopy mass is valuable, but repeated
  dome-shaped caps with hanging triangular leaves look like patterned accessories.
  The original has more varied moss/leaf bunches and interwoven wood/stone mass.
  Core facets remain coarse, with a large nearly uniform luminous upper surface.
- Style: flat planes and brown/lime/cyan/gray colors fit the low-poly direction.
  Repeated cap silhouettes and standardized fingers feel more manufactured than
  the original. Render grain and lighting differences are not mesh defects and
  did not earn a separate penalty.

## Actual best and preserved disagreement

My V4 scores are silhouette 3.8, face 3.7, details 3.3, style 3.9:
3.8 × 0.35 + 3.7 × 0.30 + 3.3 × 0.20 + 3.9 × 0.15 = **3.685**.
Complete-pass1's exact advantage is **0.080/5**. It wins on foliage volume,
fullness of the shoulder/back silhouette and improved distribution of foot
stone. V4 retains a slightly cleaner, less repetitive style read. Face quality
is effectively unchanged. The best candidate is therefore complete-pass1,
although neither resolves the principal face and grown-root anatomy issues.

Only after these image judgments, read the earlier V4 independent review. Its
3.65 score is retained as that reviewer's judgment, not overwritten by my 3.685.
It called V4 a passing static milestone. I do not equate either milestone or
this small improvement with final art approval. No score was raised to match
that prior verdict, and no prior design explanation supplied visual credit.
