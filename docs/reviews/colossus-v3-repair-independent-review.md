### Silhouette and proportions

The broad shoulders, long forearms, hooked hands, splayed stance and tall forked crown preserve the main read. The original's limbs have more irregular overlapping root growth, a heavier integrated shoulder/back canopy and broad grounded stone/root toes. Here the arms and legs separate into relatively regular solid segments; the feet end in small rock clusters, and the crown reads as a clean antler pair. This is recognizably the same design family, with less of the original ancient, overgrown mass.

### Face and character

Cyan eyes within dark sockets and the pointed wooden face are present and readable. The original has a longer, more sharply descending, layered root mask with a narrow aggressive eye/bridge construction. The repair has a broad flat brow, short triangular chin and comparatively broad cyan eye shapes, producing a tidy mask-like expression. Crown roots meet the head with less of the original intertwined, elongated facial structure. Face identity remains a key limitation despite its strong readability.

### Characteristic details

The chest core, surrounding roots, shoulder greenery and rock feet are genuine matches. However, the core is a very clean kite-like jewel with a simple enclosing root loop; the original has a more irregular luminous mass clasped by multiple root claws. Foliage repeatedly forms small radial umbrella caps with long downward triangular leaves at the shoulders, elbows and hips. The original's foliage is a layered, uneven growth that bridges adjacent forms. Lower-leg growth and stone integration remain sparse. The rear also exposes a comparatively regular timber framework beneath repeated leaf clusters.

### Facet style and palette

Flat low-poly planes, warm wood, gray stone and turquoise core fit the target broadly. The original varies woody planes, shadow blocks and leaf clusters with a more organic rhythm. The repair's large planar limb surfaces and repeated sharply pointed lime foliage have a more procedural, modular appearance. Lighting differs between the concept and captures, so this score does not demand a pixel-level brightness match or penalize the dark studio background. The repeated geometry and material grouping are the stronger evidence.

## Highest value remaining changes

1. Lengthen and layer the facial root mask, sharpen the downward eye/bridge relationship and integrate the crown roots into the brow. Preserve clear cyan eyes.
2. Replace repeated umbrella-cap foliage with uneven overlapping clusters and broader leaf-plane groupings, especially across the shoulders/back and between joints.
3. Break the regular plank-like limb masses with selected interwoven root planes; broaden the stone/root toes and increase lower-leg integration without changing the successful long-arm proportion.
4. Make the chest enclosure several irregular clasping roots around a less diagrammatic core silhouette. Preserve the core's central contrast.
