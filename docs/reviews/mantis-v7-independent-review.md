## Findings across the views

- **Original versus hero:** The specific character reads immediately: brown splintered mantis, large green leaf fan, leaf-tipped antennae, bright angular green eyes, cream serrated sickles and long angular legs. The hero carries enough of the concept's identity to pass. The source's graceful rising neck and irregular bark layering are simplified into broader, more solid components.
- **Front:** Both sickles frame the face convincingly and green eyes remain legible. The shoulder arms make a broad muscular zigzag, and the central cream throat projection forms a conspicuous long spike below the mask. The source has a more integrated, compact insect head and chest transition.
- **Left and right:** These expose the strongest remaining proportion issue: a long, comparatively straight armored neck/body beam, stepped dorsal plates, and thin edge-on sickles. Leaf depth is modest, making the back fan read as mounted blades. The concept's neck is more curved, with layered splinters wrapping the mass rather than a repeating spine alone.
- **Back:** Four green leaves make a coherent and recognizable fan, but the highly bilateral arrangement and large triangular color patches feel more diagrammatic than the source's overlapping organic foliage. Rear hips and legs are chunky, with less splintered taper and foot articulation.
- **Top:** The central body is continuous and the paired leaf attachments are understandable. This view reinforces the broad shoulder span, geometric hip blocks and regular fan construction; it does not justify claiming reference-accurate hidden anatomy.
- **Diagonal front:** The eyes, antennae and paired cream blades maintain character identity. The long facial wedge and horn-like brow/crown read somewhat as a wooden mask. The crisp V-shaped shoulder pose feels heavier than the original insect.
- **Diagonal left and right:** Bark projections and wing ribs provide useful depth cues, and the side-mounted eyes remain visible. The oversized cream throat sliver and repetitive angular neck plates are particularly apparent. The serrations are simplified to a few large teeth, and the lower legs end in plain bent points rather than the source's more articulated claws.
- **Diagonal back:** The leaf fan remains coherent from above and behind; it is not dependent on the hero angle. Large clean polygons on abdomen, hips and neck still lack some of the source's richer small-to-large splinter hierarchy.

## Major residuals and recommendation

1. **Face/chest integration:** Shorten and integrate the projecting cream throat wedge; compact the long mask and improve the eye/brow/chin relationship. This is the largest identity weakness.
2. **Organic proportions:** Recover a more curved, slender neck and less bulky shoulder/hip junctions without losing the readable mantis pose.
3. **Layering and detailing:** Add selective overlap/depth to the leaf fan and irregular bark scale hierarchy. Refine sickle tooth rhythm and foot/claw articulation rather than simply adding more triangles.

The brown/cream/green palette and broad faceted construction fit the intended low-poly toon style well. Remaining color facets and body modules are more regular and simplified than the original, but this is not a generic creature with only a matching palette. Keep V7 as a narrow static-likeness milestone pass; the residuals should remain explicit. No further polish is required by the stated >3.5 visual threshold alone.
