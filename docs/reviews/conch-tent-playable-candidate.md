stippling with received shadows retained at fixed rest. It does not establish
animated appearance or gameplay. Recommend a **separate public4-plus-tent
normal-clock control**, retaining the exact existing Conch4 product, canonical
body, dimensions and poses. That is the smallest isolated way to test whether the
filter behaves during actual combat. It must be labelled Conch4 filter control,
not selected-model progress or acceptance. Root must separately assign, review,

dimensions and pose adaptation before it can become the selected-model playable
candidate. Do not enable it by substituting Conch4 clips. No product factory was
created to permit such a substitution.
