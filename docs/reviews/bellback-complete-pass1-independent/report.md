# Bellback complete-pass1: independent static likeness review

Status: unread. Reviewed 2026-10-19. Scope: original approved concept against the actual complete-pass1 hero and all nine separate rendered views, then matched retained V6. No prior scores or design notes were read. First judgment was written to `first-judgment.json` before V6 inspection and is unchanged.

## Verdict and exact scores

Retain V6 as the slightly better likeness baseline. Neither candidate is concept-equivalent. Complete-pass1 improves individual construction details, but its face reads less clearly and less compactly than V6. This recommendation does not discard the complete-pass1 study or settle the user's taste decision.

## Why these scores

- Silhouette: the oversized teal bell, timber cage, low horned head and gray stone feet make the identity immediately recognizable. Both models have a straight-sided scaffold carriage and very regular quadruped stance. The concept has a denser, more organic wreck-crab mass, heavier front supports and a less evenly engineered silhouette. Complete-pass1's projecting face remains particularly long in side and overhead views.
- Face: complete-pass1 has angular amber eyes and a hostile brow, but the eye color reads flatter yellow than the concept's concentrated orange glow. Its long layered boat-prow forehead and cluster of small teeth interrupt the strong snarl. V6's broader, simpler tooth/jaw graphic and shorter face are easier to read, although its two pale forehead spikes are an unwelcome extra detail and its jaw is still too simplified.
- Defining details: complete-pass1's clearly exposed tapered bell, gold bands, dark opening, clapper, rope ties, timber layering and stony toes are strong. The less obstructed bell and removal of V6's forehead spikes improve this category. Added rail/bracing and head detail do not automatically increase likeness: several read as literal assembled parts rather than the concept's integrated creature.
- Style: both are legitimately faceted, untextured low-poly forms with the right wood / patina / rope / stone palette. Complete-pass1 adds thin repeated face ridges and tooth noise; V6 preserves broader planar readability. Render grain and studio illumination are capture conditions, not treated as texture defects in the model.

## Recommendation and limits

Use V6 as the retained likeness baseline while carrying forward complete-pass1's unobstructed bell and removal of pale forehead spikes into a future explicitly scoped iteration. First priority is a compact, forceful face with fewer clearer tooth shapes and amber/orange eyes; next is an organically dense timber-wreck silhouette rather than extra rail detail. This review makes no source changes and approves no redesign.

Static images do not validate animation, rigging, contact, collision, combat, physics, phone performance or runtime integration. The specific five-body architecture approval remains pending. No runtime admission or production replacement is granted.
