# Frozen independent initial judgment

2026-10-19. Inspected approved original `splinter-mantis-lowpoly.webp` and all ten individual full PNG candidate captures: hero, front, back, left, right, top, diag-front, diag-back, diag-left, diag-right. No construction source, prior review, or baseline image was read before this judgment was written.

Static original-fidelity verdict: below the exact 4.0 acceptance bar.

- Silhouette/anatomy: 3.8/5, weighted 35%. Strong recognizable leaf-backed mantis, bowed neck and pendant sickles. Broad upright leaves dominate back/front more than the original's layered taper; arms read as thin angular rods rather than the original's substantial folded organic limbs. Side views expose the blade's very thin edge and bulky rear abdomen. Four supporting legs read clearly in overhead and diagonals.
- Face: 4.0/5, weighted 30%. Pointed face, green faceted eyes and leaf-tipped antennae are well retained. Front is mask-like and symmetrically beady; profile has a narrow downward snout. Original's cheek/brow integration is richer.
- Details: 3.8/5, weighted 20%. Bark chips, leaf ribs, thigh foliage and cream toothed scythes are present. Scythe teeth are few, large and cutout-like; roots/limb joints and feet have a simplified block-and-rod quality. The cream neck strip reads like a separate thin slab in front and side. Surface detail must not substitute for anatomy.
- Style: 4.3/5, weighted 15%. Convincing flat-shaded low-poly wood/leaf palette, clear green eyes and cream cutting edges. No realistic texture drift; some triangulation reads busier than the clean original planes.

Exact weighted score: 3.8*0.35 + 4.0*0.30 + 3.8*0.20 + 4.3*0.15 = 3.935/5. No rounding up. These are one whole-model judgment, not an average of view scores. This score is frozen before baseline comparison and is not borrowed from any other reviewer.

The hero is attractive and coherent, but side and top inspection are part of the verdict. This is a static geometry/art comparison only, with no runtime, animation, rig, collision, or gameplay acceptance.
