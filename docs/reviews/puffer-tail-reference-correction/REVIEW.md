# Puffer tail reference correction: independent visual review

Reviewed 2026-10-20. Scope: AI target-reference consistency only, not a model-quality, gameplay, physics, or measured-camera review.

## Verdict

**The candidates genuinely improve the specific tail-orientation error and preserve the recognizable Brine Puffer identity. Recommend them as corrected AI reference candidates, with the qualifications below.** Explorer 04 BACK, 05 TOP and 09 ELEVATED BACK now show narrow, centered edge-on tail silhouettes instead of broad fan faces. World 05 TOP receives the same substantive correction. This is a visible geometry cue change, not merely relabeling.

The authoritative hero remains unchanged and authoritative. This review does not promote the candidates to canonical geometry or approve a game asset. Originals should remain available. No asset, Site, commit, or publication was changed in this review.

## Evidence inspected

- Authoritative hero: `art/driftwood-isle/concepts/lowpoly-revision-2026-10-18/brine-puffer-lowpoly.webp`, including its small rear thumbnail.
- Previous review-site assets: `dist/assets/approved-nine/pufferExplorerGen.webp` and `dist/assets/approved-nine/pufferWorldGen.webp` in the existing review-site build.
- Candidates: `art/driftwood-isle/concepts/approved-nine-2026-10-19/corrections/puffer-tail-consistency/explorer-tail-v2.png` and `world-tail-v2.png`.
- The correction directory's `provenance.json`.

All nine panels in each candidate were compared visually with their corresponding old panels and the hero. The assessment uses visible anatomy, overlaps, body-facing direction, ground/platform projection and context, not the labels as proof of camera position.

The hero's side/three-quarter views show a broad teal fan with an orange-gold rim behind the body. Its tiny rear thumbnail instead shows a narrow centerline appendage. Together these support an upright fan in the longitudinal/sagittal plane: broad when viewed from a side, narrow from directly behind or above. The thumbnail is too small to establish exact thickness, attachment, proportions or topology.

## Regressions and limits

1. **World 09 scope drift:** its tail has become more elongated and needle/blade-like despite provenance listing only World 05 as the tail target. This strengthens the edge-on cue but weakens any claim that non-target anatomy is unchanged. It is not necessarily a worse design, but it is an unrequested proportion change and not supported by the tiny hero rear thumbnail alone.
2. **Pointed rear silhouettes are not exact hero geometry:** Explorer 04/09 and World 09 now read rather like tapered blades. An edge-on fan can look narrow, but the sheets do not prove that these long pointed profiles and their thickness correspond to the rounded side-view fan. Do not derive exact mesh dimensions from them.
3. **Residual cross-view anatomy ambiguity:** spike layout, facets, limb exposure and attachment details are not exact cross-view correspondences. The front panels' bilateral rounded lateral shapes are ambiguous rather than proof of extra caudal fins. No new catastrophic identity loss is visible, but neither sheet solves hidden anatomy.
4. **AI camera caveats:** labels name intended directions only. Left/right retain strong three-quarter facial visibility; top views retain visible snouts; elevated panels vary in body/ground exposure. No projection metadata, common camera rig or measured angles exists here. Removing the World 39-degree claims improves honesty, not camera accuracy.
5. **Provenance precision:** its whole-sheet-edit warning is supported by visible out-of-scope shading/facet changes. Its `pixelExact` fields concern the PNG/display derivative pairing as recorded, not equality with the old sheets; this review did not independently validate hashes or encoding parity.

## Recommended disposition

Use these as **bounded, corrected AI references** for the intended upright sagittal tail, retaining the original hero and old sheets. Explicitly disclose World 09's shape change and keep the “AI mockup / not game capture / hidden surfaces inferred” caveat. Do not call them a measured turnaround, exact camera sheet, canonical mesh specification or model acceptance. No further generation is required merely to establish that the principal rear/top tail-orientation contradiction has improved.
