Larger shoulder/back growth bridges the root structure; smaller forearm and shin growth exposes bark between leaves.
- Extend the tapered central face and long side-cheek roots. The crown now continues through a descending brow ridge into a narrower cyan eye recess. The lower face reaches toward the core without becoming a detached beak.

- Broaden embedded stone/root feet at unequal front, outer and heel locations. Long roots clasp the stone and continue to the ground; the base is not a circular shoe rim.
- Add distinct upper/lower core clasps at different depths and vary the cyan crystal's broad outline. Its visual centre remains exposed.
