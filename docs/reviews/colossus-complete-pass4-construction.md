## One coupled reconstruction

`construct_source.py` derives the self-contained editable producer from the
SHA-256-pinned pass1 source. It selectively narrows forearm roots and heart
clasps, locally extends the leg load paths above planted feet, bows the thighs
and calves coherently with embedded stones, narrows the upper cage and staggers
shoulder heights. These are geometry changes across all views, not a new camera
or a uniform model scale. The named part structure is retained for later work,
without claiming a valid animation partition or skeleton.

The repeated three-lobe/five-blade canopy function and every invocation are
removed. Explicitly positioned growth islands have unequal sizes, oblique
ridges and depth-bearing undersides. Their shoulder/back placement follows the
scapula; elbow, forearm, hip, thigh and shin groups use different arrangements.
Selective unequal hanging shoots replace the regular perimeter skirt. This is
one shared 3D construction, not separate meshes per camera.

The original long pale facial surface remains. Its broad upper triangle is
recolored and lowered into connected brow planes, while its terminal edge has
a narrow notch. Dark sockets and cyan eyes are preserved. Main crown bends and
heights differ. Fingers are separately open, unequal paths with an opposed
thumb. The tall heart is rebuilt as one closed volume with offset front ridges
and additional triangular planes.

## Bounded construction correction and limits

The initial complete construction had a real topology error: one redundant
ridge triangle per growth island caused 31 boundary and 62 nonmanifold edges
in the exported audit. Its first hero also exposed foliage buried inside wood.
That render finished naturally. The failed editable source, recipe snapshot,
metrics, validation and labelled hero are preserved in `failed-initial/`.
All ten raw images, its GLB and execution evidence remain under the exact ignored
`tmp/colossus-complete-pass4/failed-initial/` path with hash receipts.

One correction removed that internal triangle, reseated the authored islands
and shoots at visible surfaces, enlarged selected masses, and added four
unequal joining growths. It passed the structural audit before the final
capture. This is the final candidate; there is no further micro-pass loop.

Visible tradeoffs remain for the independent review: the new greenery still
has projecting angular wedge-like planes, and its overall living coverage is
lighter than retained pass1. Back/side seating and original likeness must be
judged from the complete actual frames, not these construction intentions.
The preserved face is still a fairly continuous pointed mask. Structural
success earns no likeness credit and does not prove all these changes better.

## Reproduction, evidence and storage

From repository root, execute `construct_source.py`, then Blender 4.3.2 with
`-b -t 2 --python-exit-code 1 --python` pointing to `render_matched.py`, followed
by `-- --out tmp/colossus-complete-pass4`. Use the existing shared build lease.
The standalone GLB validator runs with the candidate GLB and `--out` JSON path.
`package_evidence.py` checks exact hashes/settings and makes the stable previews.

Final capture uses the unchanged predecessor protocol: ten actual images
(hero plus front/left/right/back/top and four elevated diagonals), 525x700,
16 Cycles samples, no denoising, Standard transform, target (0,0,2.9),
orthographic scale 7.7, fixed world lights and floor. Both protocols reset the
scene before building. The same camera positions, projection and target are
checked for all ten candidate/predecessor pairs. Candidate framing was not
changed. Retained PNGs are not duplicated in this package.

The final candidate GLB and geometry report are separate from the visual
judgment. Tests establish finite attributes, valid indices, zero welded
component boundary/nonmanifold/winding/zero-area defects and positive component
volumes. Components intentionally overlap: intersection freedom, global union
watertightness, deformation, motion/contact, collision, runtime loading and
performance remain unproven. There are no skins or animation tracks.

Ten labelled full-size compressed candidate previews and one portrait matched
hero board are retained. Raw captures and logs remain ignored. Package receipts
include source, raw-capture, baseline and final-output hashes and binary/evidence
budgets. No .blend, movie, archive, runtime/physics/AI edit, Site publication,
