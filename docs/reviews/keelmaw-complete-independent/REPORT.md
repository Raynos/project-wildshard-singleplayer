# Keelmaw complete-character independent review

2026-10-20. **Prefer the complete-character candidate, 3.07/5, over the fresh matched baseline, 2.775/5. Improvement: 0.295. Still below 4/5.** This is static source likeness only. No animation, physics/contact, native playable or combat acceptance is granted.

## Evidence and method

Read AGENTS.md, GIT.md, STORAGE.md, the active DRIFTWOOD-ENEMIES owner plan and State, the previous first-source independent review, and the builder README. Independently inspected the original approved hero and both original World/corrected Explorer sheets at 1536×1024 with `view_image(detail="original")`. Then inspected **all twenty original 600×720 raw PNGs individually**, using that same tool setting: ten final candidate views and ten freshly rendered baseline views. Images were delivered individually to the visual tool, sometimes in a batch of separate images; no contact sheet, resized preview or prose substitute was used to score them. No enlargement was treated as new detail. The final candidate's ten were inspected first; the baseline's ten followed as rendering completed. Scores were locked after all twenty were inspected, before final receipt checks. No builder likeness score was provided. The prior score and builder description were known, so this is not a blind review.

The original hero is authoritative. Supporting AI sheets disagree about top orientation, tail curl and some poses; inferred hidden surfaces are approximate. Do not mirror actual anatomy to chase contradictory panels. Raw capture and reference paths, hashes, dimensions and individual inspection attestations are in `verification.json`.

## Why the candidate is better, and why it is still short

The wider snout, more substantial ivory cheek/jaw, thicker limbs and broader palms establish more of the reference's weight. The larger, raised tail has more presence and the muted green grouping is less checkerboard-like. Directionally these are useful integrated changes. All four legs, the open mouth, amber eyes, gold keel, ropes, barnacles and armored tail remain recognizable across the orbit.

However, the reference's low, hulking shoulder-to-head flow is still replaced by a horizontal barrel with four similar bent appendages. Forelimbs are more substantial but read as a short chain of blunt polygon blocks; the torso remains uniform rather than convincingly shoulder-dominant. The tail has gained thickness and lift but is still a sharp segmented hook with broad bare bands rather than a heavy, armored, continuously tapering curl. Its underbelly color transition is much less convincing than the hero's pale band.

The face is wider, but the upper head still resembles a flat rectangular lid with repeated raised tiles. Eyes remain round, exposed yellow beads rather than deep, predatory apertures beneath an integrated slanted brow. The ivory cheek is a larger slab, not yet the hero's deep interlocking cheek/jaw volume. Teeth remain a visually repetitive pointed fence despite size variation. The gold keel is especially conspicuous straight-on: a narrow flat bar bridging the opening. The approved hero also has a central keel; the problem is its simplified shape and lack of integration, not its mere presence.

The dorsal grid has changed from square tiles to overlapping shield-like pieces, but top and elevated-back still show three extremely regular gold rails and repeated roof-shaped plates. Golden spikes are large, bright, nearly repeated wedges; they dominate over a body that has too few purposeful armor planes. Rope twists are present but small in the render, and barnacles still read as similar repeated small bolt clusters at regularly spaced attachment stations. This is a modest detail gain, not a wholesale match to the organic nautical layering of the hero. Low-poly simplification is appropriate; adding noise is not the fix. The missing hierarchy is broad anatomical planes, medium body-following armor and selected asymmetric accents.

## Individual raw-view observations

Each entry applies to separately inspected baseline and candidate PNGs.

- **Hero:** Both are head-right three-quarter views. Candidate has a visibly heavier snout, cheek, forefeet and tail; baseline is narrower and more striped. Candidate still reads as a blocky crocodile toy with flat upper-face tiles and bright repeated dorsal wedges. Entire silhouette fits in both; candidate tail has a tight left margin. The authoritative hero has a much closer, looming face/forequarters presentation, so this is a category comparison, not a pixel-aligned match.
- **Front:** Correctly front-facing. Candidate's head and forelimb spread are wider and its ivory jaw is more substantial. Both are dominated by the straight gold center bar and rectangular opening. Candidate's eye/brow expression remains weak. Tail rises on screen-left consistently with the rest of this actual orbit. Four-limb presence is not fully inspectable from this occluded angle alone.
- **Left:** Snout points screen-left as labeled. Candidate has a deeper pale cheek/jaw and stronger limb mass, but side silhouette still exposes the horizontal barrel, similarly bent front/rear legs and sparse triangular combs. Baseline has a notably thin lower jaw and thinner tail. Full models fit.
- **Right:** Snout points screen-right; not swapped with left. Candidate's thicker tail and larger lower face are clear, but open tooth fence, smooth limb bands and hard segment junctions persist. Both preserve the same coherent anatomy under the opposite view. Full models fit.
- **Back:** Correct rear view. Candidate has a much heavier raised tail crossing the foreground; baseline tail is thinner and lower. The candidate's rear legs still read as broad oblique blocks with little dorsal-to-hip integration. No clipping observed.
- **Top:** Head points screen-left, matching corrected Explorer but opposite World top. Candidate's wider head and feet are improvements. The extremely regular three gold dorsal strips and repeated paired head tiles remain exposed. Baseline has an even more literal square panel grid and alternating lime patches. Both show four complete limb/foot groups and a coherent curl.
- **Elevated front:** True elevated frontal view; Explorer's analogous panel is more oblique. Candidate's broader face/limbs improve weight; the snout surface remains repetitive, its gold bar splits the mouth and the dorsal rows remain mechanically regular. Baseline is slimmer and its smaller jaw less substantial. Full silhouette fits.
- **Elevated left:** Head-left oblique category is correct. Candidate has the clearer bulky crocodilian silhouette and thick upward tail, but its forelimb/torso transitions and bare tail facets remain schematic. Baseline has smaller paws, narrow pale cheeks and a thin curled end. Both are complete in frame.
- **Elevated right:** Correct head-right oblique category. Candidate improves face width and palms, with larger shell clusters visible along the side, but regular spikes and broad plain limb polygons still dominate. Both tail ends now fit, unlike the historical clipped evidence; candidate left tail margin is tight.
- **Elevated back:** Correct elevated rear category. Candidate's heavier curl and wider feet help; three regular gold rails, paired ropes and repetitive armor deck remain the strongest mismatch. Baseline's checkerboard and narrow feet are worse. Both actual curls are consistent with their other views; the AI references disagree, which is not a defect in the actual model.

No obvious duplicate or missing limb is visible across the ten views. Static occlusion and rigid source assembly do not establish natural deformation or articulation.

## Framing, parity and provenance

Builder confirmed a freeze after both sets completed. Independently verified all 20 raw hashes and all three original-reference hashes against the final capture receipt. Final producer SHA-256 is `0a09992f4fe843707edea34284e68c70894c583bc7efd09ca3d1e827fe2d1eed`. The ordinary candidate model's native JSON, packed payload and GLB are byte-identical to the rendered final output. Fresh baseline native JSON and packed payload are byte-identical to retained original v1.

Both sets use identical explicit camera position/target, orthographic scale 8.3, 600×720 resolution, 32 samples, and identical render-setup source, including lights and ground. Hero azimuth/elevation is −48°/18°; front/left/right/back are 0°/90°/−90°/180° at 7° elevation; top is 89.9° elevation with 90° roll; elevated-front/back are 39°; elevated-left/right are ±55° azimuth at 30°. This is fresh matched framing, not unchanged historical framing. Camera equality does not imply exact camera equivalence to an AI target panel.

**Raw `cameras.json` framingNDC is invalid evidence:** pre-render Blender transforms were stale. The builder disclosed and corrected this without changing rendered PNGs. This review independently recomputed all twenty orthographic projections from native mesh vertices and explicit eye/target/up/roll/aspect parameters. Results agree with the corrected receipt within 2.23e−16. All vertices fit. Minimum candidate edge margin is 14.5545 pixels; baseline 39.1059 pixels. Visual inspection also confirms whole-animal framing. Low side/front/back renders have an obvious horizontal ground/background boundary near the lower frame and visible render grain. These are presentation defects; neither is falsely scored as anatomy or hidden clipping.

Independent native counts: candidate 6,798 triangles, 19 bones, 19 parts; baseline 7,080 triangles, 19 bones, 19 parts. Builder source-audit file pins were independently checked. Builder audit reports native/GLB position/color/skin and oriented-triangle parity, single-sided materials, zero degenerate triangles and bind closure. That structural algorithm and consumer test were **not independently rerun by this reviewer**; these remain attributed builder evidence. They do not establish welded anatomy, posed skin quality, collision coverage, corpse contact, gameplay or performance.

## Recommendation

Keep the candidate as preferred **static WIP** and preserve the old baseline/history. The next complete-character revision should resolve the shoulder-to-cheek-to-forelimb mass together, integrate a recessed brow and less slab-like mouth architecture, then replace the three-rail dorsal repetition with body-following armor hierarchy. Carry that rhythm through the heavy tail and selected rope/barnacle groupings. Do not use another successful structural test or a favorable single hero view as visual acceptance.

Only `REPORT.md`, `scores.json` and `verification.json` in this review directory were written. No source model, Git, Site, Mac, browser or heavy build action was performed.
