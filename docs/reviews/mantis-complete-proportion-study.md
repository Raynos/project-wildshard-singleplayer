polygon, position and material, checks directed edge incidence, positive signed
volume, nondegenerate area, source-polygon normals versus exported normals, and
exported normals versus actual facets. It reports nonadjacent within-part
intersections and neck-incident cross-object intersections against the preserved
repair. Shared-vertex pairs are excluded; layered intersections are not contact
approval. Full results are in `geometry-audit.json`.
