# Matched-view comparison, frozen before author material

Prefer pass4, narrowly, as the static model to keep developing. Both remain recognizably the same construction family, well short of the original's irregular root anatomy.

Pass4's irregularly placed foliage breaks up the predecessor's repeated pointed green collars on elbows, hips and knees. Its shoulder mantle is broader, less like identical hanging epaulettes; exposed wood is easier to read in side and rear views. The head/eye presentation reads slightly cleaner in front and hero. These are visible wins, not credit for any unseen topology.

Tradeoffs: pass1 has denser cascading greenery closer to the original's abundant vegetation, especially across the back. Pass4 exposes a conspicuous cyan sliver at the upper back and its hands are more separated, elongated hook fingers, farther from the concept's massive looped root palms. Pass4's wider antler span reads a little more like a deer crown than upright tree forks. Neither fixes the smooth beak face, orderly rib enclosure, or regular extruded wood silhouette. The recommendation is incremental, not a likeness pass or animation/gameplay acceptance.
