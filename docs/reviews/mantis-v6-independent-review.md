### Silhouette and proportions: 3.75

The tall leaf fan, rising narrow thorax, long leaf-tipped antennae, four walking
legs and two toothed raptorial limbs make this recognizably the approved creature.
Side and rear views retain that anatomy. The main deviations are thick, somewhat
stocky lower legs, the wide blunt head, and raptor weapons that read as broad
manufactured blades rather than the reference's long tapering organic sickles.
The hero is closer than the side views: side and diagonal-side frames expose
nearly constant-thickness, square-edged blade extrusions.

### Face and identity: 2.75

The pointed center and green eyes preserve the basic motif, but the expression and
construction differ substantially. Front and diagonal-front show a broad, flat
diamond shield with protruding pale hexagonal eye plugs. The concept has narrower,
more integrated vivid green eyes under strong angled brow/temple armor, a more
layered splinter crown and a compact predatory mouth. The model's small separated
ivory mouth prongs do not recover that jaw structure. Side views expose the simple
pointed head wedge and eye attachment instead of resolving the missing brow planes.

### Character details: 3.5

Leaf tips on antennae, woody wing ribs, cream cutting edges, serrations, green limb
leaves and dorsal splinters are all present. Their placement establishes a useful
translation of the concept. Remaining differences are the simplified shin/foot
segmentation, large regular blade teeth, repetitive plate shapes along the thorax,
and the loss of the reference's interlocking bark splinters around the chest and
hips. Top and diagonal-back views make the broad, relatively plain central body
especially apparent. Rear surfaces are coherent but are not evidence of exact
concept matching because the original does not show them directly.

### Faceted low-poly style: 3.25

The model is angular and uses the right wood/leaf/ivory color families. Its leaf
backs have convincing triangular color breakup, visible particularly from the back.
However, the dominant front leaf faces contain large pale unbroken fields separated
by a few broad planes; they do not match the original's varied triangular greens.
Wood similarly relies on long uniform faces and repeated bevels where the concept
has irregular amber/dark-brown facets integrated with splintered geometry. This is
a spatial/material-design gap, not a request to add indiscriminate polygon count.

## Highest-value corrections

1. Rebuild the brow/eye socket/cheek silhouette as layered pointed wood planes.
   Narrow and seat the eyes, strengthen their green, and make the mouth a compact
   articulated-looking jaw rather than two dangling pale pins. Check front and
   three-quarter views together.
2. Taper the raptor blade cross-section and pointed ends; break the straight square
   edge-on slab profile without losing the cream edge and clear tooth silhouettes.
3. Carry irregular triangular green breakup onto the visible wing fronts and vary
   the woody planes around neck, hips and shins. Preserve the strong tall leaf fan
   and long-neck silhouette while doing so.
