# Complete puffer: fresh independent review

## Verdict

**Retain V71 as the stronger of these two static likeness candidates. Do not promote the complete remaster as an overall likeness improvement.** Complete has a better thick, cream-edged scalloped tail; V71's separate angular brows and broader wedge-tipped forefins carry more of the original's identity. This recommendation chooses an existing candidate, not an unrendered hypothetical hybrid.

The complete candidate's frozen first-pass result is **3.65/5 (73/100)**. See `initial-assessment.md` for every view and the pre-comparison score. All ten complete frames and all ten paired V71 frames were directly inspected as pixels. The approved original was inspected first. No prior scores, assembly justification, or source audit were read before freezing the complete assessment.

Independent paired V71 assessment, using the unchanged rubric:

- Silhouette/proportions: 3.75/5 × 35% = 1.3125
- Face/expression: 4/5 × 30% = 1.2
- Defining details: 3.75/5 × 20% = 0.75

These subjective scores are not precision measurements or proof of an acceptance threshold. Historical V71 3.60 is preserved as a different assessment, not overwritten, averaged, or retroactively relabelled. This reviewer disagrees with an implication that individually retained components necessarily make the strongest assembled likeness. The frozen complete result was not raised to match a target or changed after seeing prior scores.

## Matched-view comparison

- Hero: V71's substantial separate brow wedges and wider low forefins better echo the original's menace and planted silhouette. Complete's brows flatten into a ribbon; its rounded small toe edge is less like the concept's chunky irregular wedges. Complete's thick cream/orange tail border is a clear gain over V71's simpler orange ring.
- Front: V71's raised outer brow wedges and wider foot spread better match the original front miniature. Complete remains angry, but has a more level band and a smaller front footprint. Both retain the broad mouth/cream belly identity and both are less inflated than the original main image.
- Left: both reveal the upper-front shelf, barrel profile, narrow appendage roots and projecting cream bib. V71's orange cheek patch is too large and uniform; complete reduces that problem. Complete's tail thickness/cream edge improves detail; V71's forward low fin reads more convincingly as a broad flipper.
- Right: confirms those advantages and weaknesses on the other side. Neither solves the body-band geometry or the sharp face-to-body transition. V71's fin is still too elongated, but its angular toe language is closer than complete's narrow rounded fan.
- Back: no decisive overall winner. Complete visibly thickens the edge-on tail; both have separated feet and a coherent teal rump. Sparse original rear evidence does not justify exact tail-angle or rear-anatomy claims.
- Top: V71's forefins project further forward and end in exaggerated teeth; complete is more compact but reads as small side lobes. Neither view alone settles the concept's intended hidden anatomy. Both show the same broad planar back organization.
- Elevated front: V71 preserves distinct brow masses and angular white toe wedges. Complete's eyes are hooded beneath a continuous orange band and its smaller scalloped toes look less like the original. Both are recognizable.
- Elevated left: V71's cheek band is excessive; complete is cleaner there. V71's long front fin is imperfect but preserves the broad wedge shape. Complete's tapered support looks more leg-like. Complete tail border is superior.
- Elevated right: corroborates the left comparison; neither hides the faceted horizontal body bands. Original's side spikes are bold silhouette accents; both candidates' small side plaques fall short, especially the reduced complete wedges.
- Elevated back: complete has a thicker tail edge, while V71 retains broad pointed front appendage silhouettes. Both show regular diamond-like spike bases and a manufactured planar body. Exact hidden back anatomy remains uncertain.

## Evidence and source limits

The capture script was inspected after visual scoring. It builds geometry from native positions/colors, uses the same materials/lights and camera per pair, swaps visibility only, and performs no per-camera geometry edits. Receipts declare 600×740 Cycles renders, 24 samples, no denoising, AgX, native bind pose. Hero uses scale 3.2; the nine standard views use scale 4.2. Thus hero is not interchangeable with a standard view and cannot substitute for the other nine.

The source audit reports exact unchanged bones/bind pivots and semantic composition scope PASS. Tail root samples and both forefin root samples are inside the measured main hull, as are the six changed subordinate-spike roots. These technical checks do not add likeness credit.

Important unresolved inherited defects:

- Cream crown spine 1 maximum sampled root exposure: **0.006194892669808704** source units (about 6.195 mm if units are metres).
- Cream crown spine 7 maximum sampled root exposure: **0.03442319213531382** source units (about 34.423 mm if units are metres).
- The original exact-edge topology preflight remains **FAIL**. Four inherited open head components contain 7, 2, 2 and 2 triangles. The producer diagnosis identifies palate, tongue and two nostril inlays as single-sided surfaces. This review inspected the recorded topology diagnostic and its generation code, not a newly rerun geometric audit.
- A visually plausible mouth in these static images does not prove backface behaviour or correctness of those inlays in the runtime material/culling setup. The Blender script does not establish equivalent game culling.
- Finite root sampling is not continuous attachment/intersection proof. A visible stalk or narrow root is a shape observation; it is not by itself evidence of a detached mesh. The inherited exposure diagnosis should not be exaggerated into claiming every changed attachment floats.

The original's main render and visible design cues remain authoritative; AI-generated small hidden-angle views are approximate references. Neither candidate is accepted as exact anatomy. No animation, combat, collision, runtime, physical-phone performance or fun claim follows from these captures. Preserve the tested playable fallback and unchanged admission gates.
