**Made by:** Independent visual review, requested by the project coordinator.

## Fitted mouth verdict — 2026-10-19, 02:22 UTC

**Reject the current fitted-mouth shape.** The new connected junction creates
sharp inner-brow/nasal peaks and a star-like folded rim in hero/front. Side adds
an upturned projecting beak plate. These are visible geometry regressions from
the preceding mouth and the approved reference's broad, blunt bill; color
cleanup cannot remove them.

The mouth opening and lower pouch remain recognizable, but the long radial
planes around the opening draw attention to a spiky construction rather than
an inflated fish face. Passing connected-hull, attachment or visibility guards
does not compensate for this shape regression. No whole-model score is given.

## One bounded correction

Rebuild the connected outer mouth collar inside the prior beak/arch silhouette,
with a broad blunt upper bridge and short convex cheek transitions. Remove the
raised nasal corners and long radial triangles that terminate near the inner
brows or below the lip. Preserve the useful gape, eye positions and pouch
fullness. Retain the earlier mouth as the visual fallback while this connected
collar is revised; do not promote the folded shape merely to finish integration.

The reference has a shaped upper bill and substantial lower lip, but its
illustrations do not establish an exact hidden cavity or unique profile. The
rejection rests on directly visible new peaks and folded planes.

## Actual-depth correction — 2026-10-19, 02:27 UTC

**Still reject the connected mouth shape; change construction approach.** The
side's upturned plate is reduced, but hero/front retain paired sharp inner-eye
peaks and a central V-shaped fold below the lip. Relative to the baselinehood
mouth it still reads as a folded star collar rather than a broad bill. The
visible issue is the ridge/triangle arrangement joining eye corners, mouth apex
and lower lip, not only the forward depth of the authored controls.

Prefer the retained old beak silhouette with a valid, well-seated separate
mouth submesh over forcing this continuous cage. Baselinehood with the previous
mouth is the better current face. Refine its broad shaped arch and lower lip,
with a hidden/sealed attachment at the pouch and all closed-volume, clearance
and validity checks retained. Single-cage construction has no independent
art-fidelity value. A further depth-only adjustment or palette pass cannot
remove the persistent peaks and folded joins.

Inspected all three new actual images in
`tmp/puffer-remaster-guided/integrated-mouth-depthfit-clay/`:
`clay-hero.png`, `clay-front.png`, `clay-side.png`. Reopened baselinehood
hero/front and rejected fitted-mouth hero alongside them; all baseline side
and fitted views had already been inspected. The render receipt records the
completed three-view study. Reported original-surface depth restoration and
the maximum 2.145 mm forward deviation do not override this visual finding.
No new whole-model score, runtime claim or owner acceptance is made.
