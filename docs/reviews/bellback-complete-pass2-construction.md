# Bellback complete pass 2: static construction

Status: frozen for independent likeness review. No builder score.

This whole-creature study starts from retained V6, following the independent
complete-pass1 review. It preserves the compact face, two substantial upper
fangs and broad broken lower bite. It removes the paired pale brow spikes and
uses orange amber eye material with restrained emission. Head depth is slightly
shorter and its width slightly broader; every facial component shares that
change, preserving eye and tooth registration.

The bell is less tilted and its crown broader. A new segmented roof arch rises
behind its crown instead of crossing the main teal field. The carrier has
thicker, sweeping lower and upper ribs and overlapping haunch strakes. Unequal
fore/hind stance, broader front shoulder caps, and lowered rear legs shift the
weight toward the front while keeping real stone soles on the ground.

These are modeled solid meshes, not generated or composited likeness images.
The original approved concept remains authoritative. Hidden views establish
coherence only; they cannot prove exact unseen anatomy.

making portrait paired boards. No historical source or capture is overwritten.

Only static model/evidence and this review note are changed. No runtime,
physics, AI, collider, Site, shared State or production asset edits. Existing
semantic pivots are editable organization, not a rig or approved ragdoll.
The separate five-body expansion remains unapproved. No animation, contact,
combat, phone-performance, runtime admission or user acceptance is claimed.

(51.61 s and 66.21 s respectively). Both exported byte-identical native GLBs:
324,112 bytes, 4,378 triangles, 73 meshes, 81 nodes and 170 material primitives.

self-contained buffers pass; there are no skins or animation clips.

point blocked by its own socket edge. These are diagnostics, not likeness scores.
