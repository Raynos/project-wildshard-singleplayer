# Tide-Root Colossus complete pass 1

19 October 2026. Unaccepted complete static study; independent review pending.
No author-assigned score. The current target is at least 4/5 independently in

## Audit and composition

Read the approved original, V1 construction notes, V2 failure and its full-nine
review, V3 failed-mask/repair history and independent review, V4 construction and
independent full-nine review. Inspected the original pixels and V4 actual board.
V1's modular pale construction, V2's orderly robot and shoe rims, V3's radial
umbrella patches/short face, and V4's thin side and isolated paper leaves are
explicit failure evidence. No previous source or evidence was replaced.

This pass retains V4's named root anatomy, narrow cyan eyes, elongated mask,
antler branching, turquoise heart and hanging hands. It recomposes foliage as
unequal closed five-sided lobes with differently inclined broad facets and a
staggered pointed apron. These patches bridge the flank and back roots and drape
across arm and calf overlaps, rather than repeating either balls or flat collars.

The upper cage is deepened continuously through all connected growth regions,
with a forward upper-body lean. New flank load roots and forearm splits break
long plain surfaces. Dark stone is embedded at upper arm, forearm, thigh and
shin heights. The broad foot stones are split into smaller offset grounded toes,
and a wood root passes between them to the ground. The forehead loses its pale
central triangle; the successful eye apertures remain. Lower-body depth varies
by side to reduce mechanical bilateral rhythm without changing root attachment.

## Reproduction

Sources: art/driftwood-isle/lowpoly-colossus/complete-pass1/.
Run build_colossus_complete.py with Blender -b -t 2, --python-exit-code 1,
--out tmp/colossus-complete-pass1 --render for the matched hero.
Run render_nine.py with --out tmp/colossus-complete-pass1-nine for actual nine.
Both use the prior fixed world lights, Standard transform, full bounds and
unchanged view directions. Hero is 900x1200/32 samples; nine are 525x700/16.
Geometry is identical across every camera. No view-facing anatomy or overlays
alter the subject. Denoising is disabled. Source and GLB stay editable.
