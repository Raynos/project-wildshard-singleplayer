## Recommended next complete-character correction

Keep the improved smaller teeth, coarse rope facets and ribbed underside. Rework head, chest and leg transition together: shorten and break the uninterrupted forehead wedge, make amber eyes compact inset accents rather than broad strips, integrate the jaw into the cheek/chest volume, and overlap irregular shoulder/haunch timber into narrower wrists. Preserve bell aperture, separate clapper and readable wreck skyline. Give bindings believable local contact/knots and less uniformly horizontal routing without increasing decorative clutter. Judge the integrated result at all ten unchanged cameras against this retained candidate and the original hero. No isolated structural-test pass should raise likeness scores.
