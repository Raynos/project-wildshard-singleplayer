## Corrections needed as one complete-character pass

1. Rework head, brow and cervical/chest transition together: smaller inset green eyes with more shaped sockets, a compact predatory forehead/cheek mask and a throat integrated into layered bark chest plates. Avoid simply shortening a neck parameter until the head sits directly on the hips; the upright rising Mantis posture is the improvement to preserve.
2. Build a coherent taper from narrow upper thorax through chest and pelvis into a purposeful segmented abdomen. Break up the exposed smooth throat strip and hip hub with restrained, overlapping bark silhouettes. Preserve the new tapered abdomen while removing the boxy cross-section and very repetitive stepped plating.
3. Art-direct the whole wing fan in 3D: stronger long/medium/short hierarchy, varied attachment/tilt and faceted thickness, keeping leaf green dominant and ribs subordinate. The hero suggests a fuller layered fan; do not blindly copy a generated sheet's exact count. Verify front, side, rear and overhead together so fixing the hero does not turn the back into opaque kites.
4. Reorient and shape both raptorial forelimbs as volumes so the cream cutting face remains legible in profiles and elevated obliques. Refine contiguous cutting edge, tooth size rhythm and arm/elbow integration; do not solve with camera changes or oversized floating teeth. Current side-view needles are a substantial identity loss.
5. Refine legs and feet with the whole body's proportions: reduce abrupt thigh/shin contrast, distribute bark/leaf armor coherently and preserve readable negative spaces. Exact front occlusion can be partly pose, so distinguish it from missing anatomy. Static stance is not evidence of reliable ground contact.
6. Keep broad low-poly flat facets and warm wood/green/cream palette, but give head/chest smaller intentional planes than wings. Current candidate is cleaner than baseline, still closer to assembled wedges than the reference's sculpted bark creature. Review these changes as a complete model in the same ten views before any score claim.
