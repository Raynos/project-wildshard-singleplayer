# Conch character pass: independent full-nine likeness review

Reviewed 2026-10-19 UTC. Verdict: **3.57/5; strictly greater than 3.5: YES.**
This is a narrow static visual milestone, not owner acceptance or arena readiness.

## Evidence and scope

Inspected actual pixels of the authoritative original
`art/driftwood-isle/concepts/lowpoly-revision-2026-10-18/conch-duelist-lowpoly.webp`,
the actual hero `tmp/conch-character-pass/color-front.png`, the comparison board,
and all nine individual full-frame `color-character-pass-*.png` images in that
directory: front, left, right, back, top, diag-front, diag-left, diag-right,
diag-back. Also inspected `nine-view-character-pass-board.webp`; the board did
not substitute for individual frames. Both original character depictions were
considered; their UI and environment were not scored against studio renders.

Required repository instructions and coordinator STATE were read. The STATE
contains historical scores; no earlier review report was used to establish this
score, and no requested change was treated as evidence of improved likeness.
Rear and hidden anatomy in the single original remain approximate: the other
views test coherent continuation of the visible design, not an invented rear
reference.

## What earns the score

- The tall peach-and-cream shell, broad flared coral opening, stalked golden
  slit eyes, plum armored body, large shield and opposing pale blade establish
  the correct character immediately in hero, front and diag-front views.
- Shell height and the wide crouched stance preserve the principal design
  hierarchy. The model has substantial shell depth in both side views and the
  back, rather than relying on the favorable hero projection alone.
- Eye rims and stalks, pointed orange facial plate, dark segmented abdomen,
  angular shoulder armor, contrasting weapon tips and small clawed feet provide
  meaningful identifying detail rather than color-only resemblance.
- Flat angular planes and the restrained cream/coral/plum palette fit the
  target's low-poly language. Broad regions remain legible at board scale.

## Concrete remaining gaps

1. **Shell shape is the largest silhouette discrepancy.** The original has a
   more irregular, organically tapering conical spiral. Here broad nearly
   horizontal bands and overhanging shelves read as stacked tiers. The effect
   is especially apparent in right, diag-left and diag-back, while top exposes
   a broad pale disk-like crown. Front is more convincing because the lip
   conceals part of this regularity. The original also has more varied local
   coral and cream facets rather than large clean band boundaries.
2. **Face is recognizable but less predatory.** The model's wide bulbous
   orange upper face and long central triangular beak feel more mask-like than
   the original's narrow, angular facial taper. Golden eyes and slits are
   effective, but their broad frames, relatively even presentation and reduced
   aggressive tilt soften the expression. The original's slender, rising
   stalks give the face more alert tension.
3. **Shield mass and presentation differ.** The target is an oblique, elongated
   faceted shell-claw form that integrates into the forward arm. The model
   reads as a thick upright orange wedge with a narrow pale blade-like front
   edge. Hero/front retain the asymmetry, but top and diag-right reveal its
   simpler blocky section. The opposite weapon reads correctly as a stabbing
   blade but is flatter and cleaner than the organic claw transition.
4. **Body is more regular armor than reef anatomy.** Side and rear frames show
   simplified segmented limbs, flat dark joint patches and a symmetrical
   plated pelvis. The original's shoulder, knee and foot shapes have more
   irregular biological transitions. The compact frontal body also loses some
   of the original's lankier duelist reach.

These are material likeness gaps, not disqualifying omissions of the character's
core identity. The result clears this particular bar narrowly; it is not a
near-final replica.
