## Individual-view findings

- Hero: pass2's taller crown and thicker cream-rimmed tail are closer to the original. Its cream pouch is wider but its broad squared front and flattened lower plane remain less organic than the reference hanging bowl. V71 has better broken-up crown facets but a lower, flatter head roof and a plain orange tail border.
- Front: pass2 gains inflated width and crown height, but the squared cream mass crowds the narrow facial band. Both preserve convincing angry orange brows and dark open beak; neither achieves the original's deeply sheltered eye/descending roof relationship.
- Left and right: pass2 improves crown-to-face descent and reduces the large orange cheek patch. Persistent horizontal teal courses remain obvious on both sides. V71's pouch is smaller and more tapered; pass2 improves volume at the cost of a more block-like forward projection.
- Back: pass2's fuller body and tail thickness read coherently; V71's rear has more visually articulated facets. Exact backside fidelity cannot be inferred from tiny original thumbnails.
- Top: pass2 expands the body envelope and improves tail border, but smoother long crown panels and orderly sparse diamonds still feel unlike the reference interlocking irregular facets. V71 has stronger varied crown-plane contrast.
- Elevated front: same strong angry expression; pass2 gains bulk but broad low-detail crown and squared pouch remain visible. V71's smaller cheek mass leaves slightly more breathing room around fins.
- Elevated left and right: pass2's raised roof, thicker tail, and restrained orange cheeks improve the concept's overall palette hierarchy. Both expose uniform flank courses and relatively cylindrical rear fin roots rather than the reference broad sculpted fins.
- Elevated back: pass2 is fuller with stronger spike/tail presence; V71 has more varied plane rhythm. No exact unseen-anatomy score is invented.
