## All ten views: candidate versus retained

- **Hero:** The candidate's inward, shorter hands and narrower reach give a more compact whole-character read. Pale cheek pieces sit more convincingly beside the central ridge. Retained has clearer long separated digits; candidate fingers gather into a club-like curl. Both have the antler crown, narrow cyan eyes, embraced crystal and planted stone feet, but the chest is a layered wooden breastplate rather than the hero's irregular root anatomy.
- **Front:** Candidate slightly reduces the wide hanging-arm U and joins the palm to the forearm better. It keeps inset eyes rather than introducing broad cyan panels. The dark divided jaw and parallel cheek edges remain manufactured-mask cues. Retained's hands have more air between digits; candidate sacrifices this useful articulation. Repeated inward chest strips remain too regular in both.
- **Left:** Candidate's rear mass protrudes less and the palm is less a set of parallel extended hooks. Both still have a large dark bowed back enclosure, a chest-forward ledge and an angular thin-looking ankle stack. Candidate's closer grouping makes the hand read as a solid hanging paddle at this angle; no decisive finger-resolution win.
- **Right:** The candidate is marginally less barrel-deep and carries the arm into the palm more continuously. Both show bright shoulder panels with foliage perched on top and an exposed dark hollow beneath the back leaves. The concept has more interwoven, layered growth joining those areas. Rock/root continuity improves only modestly.
- **Back:** Candidate leaves cover more of the scapular area and the rerouted back roots somewhat reduce the oval cage impression. A broad dark paired arch still dominates the lower back, rather than the reference's tapered overlapping roots. Retained fingers are more individually legible from behind; candidate clenched silhouettes are blunter. This is inferred back coherence, not verification of an approved hidden surface.
- **Top:** Candidate looks slightly tighter front-to-back, but broad green plates read as attached caps; bare wood channels remain visible through the center. Retained has similar shortcomings, with somewhat more separated small hanging pieces. Neither develops the reference sheets' dense but hierarchical grown-over crown/shoulder mass. More leaf area is not automatically better style.
- **Elevated front:** Candidate compactness and cheek seating improve the central read without losing the slit-eye expression or heart clasps. Retained has more expressive open root fingers. Both present an oversized, regular rib-ring around a simple cyan polyhedron and a thin pelvis bridge instead of the more organically interlocked original.
- **Elevated left:** Candidate hand curvature is broader and its torso depth less intrusive; the foliage remains a stack of angular independent plates. The near hand loses some curled-digit negative space relative to retained. Face/core overlap and stacked chest boards persist in both.
- **Elevated right:** Candidate keeps the crown/face/core hierarchy and gains a modestly more joined palm/forearm contour. Retained's longer root fingers have stronger gestural separation. Both still have an underresolved continuous flow from calf through ankle into stone: merely widening roots does not make the scattered foot stones one convincing weight-bearing base.
- **Elevated back:** Candidate gives the shoulder growth slightly broader coverage and a less protruding rear silhouette, but the paired dark back roots and canopy cap construction are still conspicuous. Retained's longer fingers and more scattered leaf ends are not uniformly worse. No view demonstrates a transformed, finished back.

## Regressions and what to carry forward

The main regression is **digit separation and gesture**: shortened, closely grouped fingers improve bulk but can become a hanging mitten or club in front, side and elevated views. Do not declare the hands solved. Larger leaf planes also create stronger angular-cap readings from above; the foliage improvement is coverage, not a clean style win. The numerical tie on defining details/style deliberately reflects these tradeoffs.

Keep the candidate's narrower overall spread, modestly reduced torso depth, closer cheek seating and broad palm continuity. Preserve narrow inset eyes and the crooked roots embracing the cyan heart. Next work should resolve the complete load-bearing root system: irregular interwoven chest roots instead of parallel plank ribs; expressive separated fingers emerging from palms; a tapered back rather than a dark oval backpack; and coherent calf/root/stone transitions. Build foliage in unequal overlapping rooted groups, not another layer of uniform hanging triangles or larger roof plates. Rejudge the entire character in the same ten views rather than scoring isolated patches.
