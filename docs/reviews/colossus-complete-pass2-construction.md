## Reference and substantive correction

The approved original catalogue figure is authoritative. The action figure
corroborates the design; small hidden-angle concept panels remain approximate.
Read the complete-pass1 independent review, and retained its source and evidence.

The previous continuous pointed face plate is removed. Two converging crown-root
plates form a narrower split bark mask over a recessed dark backing; separate
outer cheek splinters integrate that mask into the crown and shoulder roots.
The cyan slanted eyes remain. The center no longer has a large bright triangle.

Foliage is rebuilt rather than simply increased. Unequal angular closed volumes
with offset peaks replace repeated dome-and-apron constructions. Different
scales, lean and selective shoots establish large shoulder/back masses, smaller
elbow/hip bunches and exposed forearm/ankle wood. Left/right group sizes differ.
The foliage is fully volumetric, including the rear mantle.

Parallel middle/inner/thumb rows are replaced by broad curling root hooks and
one unequal split prong. The outer claw remains continuous with the forearm.
A coherent shape-space adjustment deepens the hunch, widens the upper cage and
bows/staggers lower-body paths without detaching the existing root intersections.
The crown, chest core, stone-root feet and long arm silhouette remain the identity.

## Reproduction and boundaries

Editable build source and matched render script live alongside the GLB.
Run render_matched.py through the shared heavy build lease with Blender -b -t 2
--python-exit-code 1 and --out tmp/colossus-complete-pass2. It builds unchanged
complete-pass1 and complete-pass2 in separate reset scenes, then renders both
with identical world lights, target, orthographic scale, exposure, dimensions
and sample count. Each receives an actual hero and nine full-frame directions.
No denoising, composited anatomy, viewpoint-dependent mesh edits or mockups.

Validation checks finite GLB attributes, indices, welded closed meshes,
consistent winding and nonzero positive mesh volume. Intentional component
intersections remain; this is not deformation, intersection or runtime proof.
No runtime, AI, physics, Site, remote, Git or shared State changes were made.

## Preserved construction attempt

The first pass2 geometry produced single-apex foliage volumes that read as small
pyramids in its actual hero. It completed all ten captures with a clean geometry
audit, then was retained in attempts/01-pointed-foliage with sources, GLB,
compact views and exact raw-capture receipts. Its replacement changes the
foliage peaks to broken offset ridges and selective wider shoots. The corrected
candidate is captured with the same settings and predecessor evidence. The
first attempt is construction history, not an independently reviewed score.

Potential remaining visual weaknesses belong to the independent review: the
core still has relatively broad facets and the smaller foliage masses expose
more limb wood. Geometry validation does not settle likeness or taste.
