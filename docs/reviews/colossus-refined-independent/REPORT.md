## Findings

- Refined's larger cyan eye panels are easier to see but more robotic. The authoritative hero has narrow, sharply inset cyan slits and a pale tapered wood face. Retained next is closer on expression. Both retain an overly mechanical mask.
- Removing the short core clasps and inward ribs simplifies the chest but weakens an important identity: the original's crooked root fingers visibly embrace its crystal. Refined reads more as an exposed pendant inside a broad oval chest. Deeper seating helps side projection but does not replace the missing visual rhythm.
- Refined's larger canopy islands, tighter arm spread, fuller ankle roots and more continuous bark color are modest genuine improvements. They do not outweigh face and chest regressions.
- Both still have long angular plank-like forearms and segmented hook hands, thin lower-leg anchoring, sparse repeated leaf wedges and a broad dark oval rear torso. The original reads as overlapping interwoven root mass with denser leaf growth and more integrated stone bases.
- Prefer the older model overall. A future whole-character pass should restore slit-eye expression and embracing chest roots while addressing side/back root mass, hand curvature, canopy layering and stone-root anchoring together. Do not merely increase detail count.
