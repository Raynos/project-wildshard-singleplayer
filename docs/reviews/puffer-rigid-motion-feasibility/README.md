2026-10-19. **No feasible integrated alternate animation established. No promotion.**

## Useful result

A direct exact-extreme, positive-skin-membership compound recipe fits the existing
one-body contract's structural budget: seven unique named bones and344 points.
Per-bone counts are belly82, tail53, finL42, finR42, rearL42, rearR42, head41.
Every retained point is an actual source vertex, expressed in its bone's bind
frame. All exact duplicate source positions are consolidated without rounding;
no extreme is dropped, no support padding is introduced. Provenance retains
source-global and part-local indices for every point.

But the hull union fails even before a new death or attack animation is authored:
192 source vertex records (all mixed0.8-fin/0.2-belly caps) lie outside all seven
hulls at an unchanged steady locomotion input. This is a unit-scale input of the
existing gait: phase0, stage0, no hit/death, full gait amplitude, idle sine1.
No transition adapter is active in this steady-state example. A common body bob
cancels from membership; fin translations use the exact existing gait formula.

Worst record: connected rearR local37, global10099, bind position
(-0.6134300827980042,0.147599995136261,-0.2231999933719635).
Its weighted position in the common body frame is
(-0.6134300827980042,0.12359999513626099,-0.1271999933719635).
Its largest violated hull halfspace is positive for every hull; the smallest
of those separating values is17.3197288121mm at rearR. The belly value is
20.4248693080mm. These are separating-halfspace certificates, **not Euclidean
surface distances or measured floor penetration**. The differences are many
orders above the1e-9m numerical classification tolerance.

This disproves all-state containment for this complete direct construction. It
also disproves the tempting inference that a convex combination of points in
two hulls necessarily remains in their union. Convex combinations remain in the
convex hull of the union, which can contain empty space between the two hulls.

## Convexification and membership limits

Even the bind hulls are not exact copies of source surfaces. Their volumes in
m³ are belly1.7069020, tail0.0695161, finL/R0.0346976 each,
rearL/R0.0143944 each and head0.0612049. They overlap, so summing these volumes
would not give union volume. Largest sampled hull-facet-centroid distances to
any actual source triangle are respectively160.883,102.424,67.063,75.584,
43.802,43.802 and83.746mm. The receipt records exact diagnostic centroids and
facet provenance. These flag substantial bridging/cavity filling to inspect;
they are not a complete concavity-volume calculation or proof all added volume
is outside the original solid. The recessed palate/head is not a solid-head
coverage proof.

Assigning vertices to every bone with positive skin influence is sufficient to
contain each influence's unblended position, but not the final blended position.
The four cap regions are not rigid to a single fin. All pure-weight records pass
this example. Also, even vertex containment would not by itself prove complete
triangle containment across a nonconvex hull union.

An eighth hull, different grouping, or a new motion restricting relative fin
movement might change feasibility. This pass does **not** prove all legal
recipes impossible. A whole-body convex hull could bridge major gaps and change
contact, so a cheap global enclosure cannot be claimed as exact anatomy. No such
substitute is authored or accepted here. The direct construction is retained as
a failed, reproducible artifact rather than padded or parameter-chased.
