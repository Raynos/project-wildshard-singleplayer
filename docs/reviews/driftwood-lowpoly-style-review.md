## Reference language

The original crab and monkey mockups, palm/boulder/shrine references and
historical real-world captures use broad purposeful facets, clean matte color
regions, sparse accents and readable silhouette detail. Low-poly is an art
language, not permission for primitive assemblies, broken anatomy or random
triangle noise. The five detailed concept pairs drifted toward sculpted
surface density: fine chips, layered scales, rope grain and many tiny roots.

## Relative style-fit order before revision

1. Splinter Mantis: closest. Retain leaf wings, hooked forearms and twig antennae;
   consolidate tiny bark splinters into broad wood planes and simple leaf forms.
2. Conch Duelist: retain broad spiral, genuinely open coral aperture, stalk eyes
   and asymmetric shield/thrust claws; remove chipped-edge and plate noise.
3. Brine Puffer: retain squat teal/ivory silhouette, inflation, broad fins and a
   few crown spines. Replace dense shingles, fine grooves and glossy iris detail.
4. Tide-Root Colossus: retain trunk silhouette, branching crown, large hands and
   turquoise heart; use fewer meaningful root masses and sparse foliage.
5. Bellback: retain actual hollow bell/clapper and timber quadruped construction;
   simplify dense corrosion, rope grain, barnacle carpets and surface clutter.

This order is a comparative art judgment, not measured gameplay quality or
completion. No current implementation meets the owner's visual target.

## Habitat presentation

Use the actual island, not five identical beach settings: Puffer tidal pools,
Conch sandy/rocky cove, Mantis fern-rich inland shrine jungle/palm fringe,
Bellback wreck timber area, Colossus mossy inland ring-shrine clearing.
The landscape and cover generators plus world/places.ts support inland grass,
crags, jungle/shrine, gully, wreck and cave habitats. This presentation guidance
is not a claim that new enemy placements have been implemented.

## Construction and evidence gate

The owner requested construction after this audit, aiming for 5/5 visual quality.
Five revised image pairs are being prepared against these references. They keep
approved identities but must not inherit a false human approval for the new
image revision. Preserve previous images and failed model studies.
Compare actual full-3D exports, Model Explorer and first-person arena captures
against the style revision. Test animation, attack tells, AI, hits, death,
loot/retry and world-state isolation. Distinguish judged visual quality,
measured tests, untested device performance and human fun. Technical passes or
model counts never establish final quality acceptance.
