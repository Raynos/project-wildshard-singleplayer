# Nine Dragon labs, clean room, and asset parity

**Audit date:** 2026-09-26. **Scope:** the playable *partial fragment* at Lantern Square, the Well rim/crossings and stair street, compared with the nine P1–P9 look labs and the former clean-room page. The planned 500 × 500 × 500 m nine-stratum shard is a separate production scope; parity with a shot lab does not mean that city is finished. This audit is a preservation inventory, not permission to delete anything.

## What blocks a safe lab/archive closeout

1. **Finish and verify P9:** commit the in-flight hook/rope/FX/miss/reel work; compare the hit, miss and landing sequences to `round-9-lab-grapple/` in portrait. The grapple owner maintains the detailed [grapple parity audit](nine-dragon-grapple-parity.md). Enemy yank is a new gameplay feature, not existing lab parity.
2. **Close the P7/P4 choices:** decide which candidate organic GLBs are worth placing, and review the trunk, leaf wind, and crowd rigging against the approved nine-angle domes. Register each *placed* GLB with the shared Model Explorer; remove intentionally rejected alternatives only after the review record names them.
3. **Move live assets out of `lab/` in small sets:** textures; crowd; canopy/scroll/lion; conditional guard; grapple hook. Update `def.ts:FILES`, all loader URLs, and generated byte/version manifests together. Verify a cold offline iOS PWA load after each set—the current asset paths are part of its cache manifest.
4. **Prove the playable envelope:** only the square, stair, rim and crossing bridges have physical floors; gallery decks/stairs and visual nets still need collision/traversal work before players are sent there. See [fragment safety audit](nine-dragon-fragment-safety.md).
5. **Keep history separate from full-shard work:** source labs that Git already removed remain recoverable by commit. Do not delete the remaining grapple lab or live assets until the above gates pass. The eight other strata, streaming, navmesh, per-stratum music and traversal are full-shard plan work, not lost lab code.
