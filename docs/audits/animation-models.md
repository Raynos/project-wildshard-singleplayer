# Model Explorer animation and rig audit

**Date:** 2026-09-30 · E335 · working-tree build served at port 4400.

All 276 cards and 515 variants in the actual four shard registries were constructed and inspected. There are 264 distinct card ids; shared cards appear in several shards. This is a structural baseline, not a verdict on animation quality. The tree was shared and included ongoing model migrations. Subsequent main changes may add/remove cards (including E333’s AR-15 roster correction). The initial run used fixed waits for asynchronous specimens and did not capture a dirty-tree manifest or served-build identity; a failed/slow asset could leave a placeholder. Its positive skins were measured, but any “no skin” candidate must be checked against a ready specimen before rigging. Re-run the committed audit script with readiness and build provenance before each implementation wave.

Evidence: [machine-readable inspection](animation-models.json), produced by `scripts/browser-lane.sh --max 20 node scripts/audit-animation-models.mjs <served-build-url> docs/audits/animation-models.json`. Every inspected skin had normalized, nonnegative active weights and valid active indices within the script’s 0.015 sum tolerance. This does not prove good deformation, bind parity, foot contacts or playback.

## Interpretation and source cross-check

- Existing creature rigs use procedural gait/hit/death motion, not embedded generated clips. A skeleton is not evidence of a finished animation library.
- The three shared training dummies each have 18 ordered joints, valid skins, no embedded clips, and spring-driven reactions. E336 is the separately authorized UniMate pilot.
- Wendell and the Driftwood trader are rigid articulated groups. A deforming humanoid rig is still needed for full-body motion.
- Pine’s three NPCs have 11-joint skins in this snapshot. E322’s richer NPC rig is already in progress elsewhere; reconcile its landing before creating another rig.
- Nalati’s camp specimens each have only three joints. UniMate v2 was trained on rigs with 5–70 actual joints; padding does not add articulation. The combined camp’s 15-joint skin is five people, not one usable humanoid.
- The Ghost Rider Explorer specimen contains only a 24-joint horse; its rigid rider and ghost appearance are attached by `src/nalati/ghostRiders.ts` in-world. This is a specimen parity gap. The mounted shepherd has a horse skin and rigid rider attachment. Kokpar riders are rigid instanced silhouettes. Audit horse and rider separately.
- Nine Dragon’s first-person arms contain two seven-joint skeletons, 10 skinned mesh parts and 16 authored clips. Protect their exact combat contacts, grapple timing and cloth simulation.
- Fish, gulls, Pine birds/hare and Nalati sheep/marmots use instanced or shader motion. No skin is not a defect for these distant populations; test a close-range rig with the existing distant representation retained.
- Storm Titan is articulated procedural scenery; moving transit and drones are rigid kinematics. Foliage, cloth, water and ordinary props need their existing simulation or wind/transform animation, not automatically a skeleton.
- Explorer’s creature clip UI routes through Animal gait controls. Generic people/gear metadata alone does not guarantee clip playback. A general clip player, seek/speed/loop controls and bone/weight overlays belong in the draft.
