## What the live partial shard now does

`src/chunks/nine-dragon-stack/grapple/Traversal.ts` claims LOCK on a visible hook, checks line of sight and a safe floor with the shared Rapier queries, uses JUMP to fire, plays the left-arm grapple clips, moves the capsule across the Well with the CharacterMotor, and closes the temporary upper safety guard. The lab's rope, filament, sparks and flashes now run in the live traversal, with phone-sized geometry and fixed substeps. The bite, miss/reel/dock, and motor-driven vault/settle phases are wired. A miss returns normal jumping and hides the line; a successful crossing lands on the lower gallery. The square-to-street and Well-rim crossings were exercised in portrait browser QA. The 19-leg fragment walk gate passed with zero stuck and zero boundary escapes. The final portrait trailer review cut predates the new FX and should be recaptured for final approval.
