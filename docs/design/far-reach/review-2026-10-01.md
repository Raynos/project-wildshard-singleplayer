# Sky Reach (`far-reach`): the look and feel review, 2026-10-01

**Feeds:** [SKY-REACH](../../plans/SKY-REACH.md) P1 (this file), then P2 to P6. **Ask:** E374.
**Build captured:** a clean export of HEAD `597c3b7b` (`scripts/serve-build.sh --head`). Phone tier, touch HUD, iPhone UA,
390×844 @3, portrait. The viewmodel is left as the game draws it. God shots hide the camera's children, as LOOK-LOOP says.
**Evidence:** `art/far-reach/round-11-review/` (boards 1 to 7, mockups A to D). The capture scripts are in the session
scratchpad, `cap.mjs` (FP poses by `player.spawn` + yaw / pitch, god poses in `game.onLate`) and `cap2.mjs` (the fan in
motion, the lock probe).

Jake, after playing it on his iPhone: "they look absolutely nothing like the mock-up … an incredibly half-assed
implementation … it's fine that it's thin on content, but it should be a polished slice … look at the Driftwood quest …
we need to do 10x, 100x better."

## The verdict

He is right, and the cause is not one bug. Four things stack up:

1. **The world is lit from behind with no fill.** It reads as dark violet cut-outs on a lavender card.
2. **There is no sky.** It is a 3-stop gradient with clouds turned off, plus flat purple puffs.
3. **Every surface is one flat vertex colour per facet.** There is no grass, rock, moss or roots.
4. **Nothing tells you what to do.**

The content (8 islands, 4 creatures, a 4-step quest, a boss, 7 models) exists. The *presentation* of that content was
never built. Measured against the mockup Jake picked, the gap is in light, sky and surface, not in layout. The camera
pose, the bridge, the windmill island ahead and the HUD in `B-sky-reach.jpg` all exist in the game today
(`board-1-vs-mockup.jpg` A vs B).

The mockups in this round prove the point. **Mockup A** (`mockup-A-spawn-look.jpg`) is the live spawn capture repainted
with nothing moved: same camera, same island, same bridge, same HUD. It reaches the mockup's quality. **Mockup B**
(`mockup-B-quest-start.jpg`) is the live first frame with a quest-giver, the chip and markers added. It reads like
Driftwood's start.

What the numbers say:

- The shadows are crushed to near black: p10 is 6, against 25 in the mockup and 40 to 47 in the good shards.
- The sky is about 20 L* too dark.
- The palette is narrow: hue spread is 51° to 55°, against 83° to 94° in the good shards. Everything is lavender,
  violet or olive.

Chroma matches the mockup, so the fix is **light and value**, not saturation.

## Ranked: why it looks bad and feels half-done

Each item gives the evidence, the cause in the code, and the fix.

### 1. Lighting and palette: backlit silhouettes, no fill, crushed shadows (worst)

**Evidence:**
- `board-1` B: the spawn view.
- `board-3` A: the windmill isle. The tower is a near-black violet slab.
- `board-4` D: the bridge looking back.
- The numbers above.

**Cause:**
- The sun sits at azimuth 300°, elevation 15° (`manifest.sky.sun`, `look/render.ts` `SUN_DIR`). The spawn and the main
  route look north into it, so every face the player sees is in shadow.
- The fill does not lift those faces:
  - `hemiIntensity` is 0.85, with a cold lavender sky (`0xb7a6cf`) and a mud ground (`0x8a6a58`).
  - `envIntensity` is 0.55.
- The grade's `shadowTint [0.92, 0.88, 1.05]` pushes the shadows toward violet.
- There is no bounce and no rim light.

The mockup is also backlit, but it gives every camera-facing surface warm golden bounce, plus a rim on the silhouettes.

**Fix (shard: `look/render.ts`, manifest `sky` / `grade`):**
- A warm ground bounce: hemi ground near `#c9935c`, intensity of about 1.4 or more.
- A rim term on the island lips, the windmill and the pines.
- A floor on shadow value: p10 L* of 25 or more.
- Shadow tint toward warm plum, not cold violet.
- Then the LOOK-LOOP's ΔE00 per region and a fitted LUT.

The key light stays low and warm. Do not move the sun overhead: golden-hour backlight is the mockup's whole mood.

### 2. Sky, fog and clouds: no sky to speak of

**Evidence:**
- `board-1` B.
- `board-4` A / B: the islands sit in empty lavender over a flat pale plane.
- The spawn frame: the storm clouds are 9 flat, grey-violet, hexagonal puffs. From the spawn they read as hovering over
  the windmill, not over the far crown.

**Cause:**
- `sky: { clouds: false }` (`look/render.ts`).
- The dome is a 3-stop gradient with a sun spot.
- The engine's `cloudSea` is a single flat plane.
- The fog is a uniform rose mix (`far.rose-fog` patch, 85 % at 460 m).
- The storm clouds are flat-shaded low-poly blobs (`world/shapes.ts`).

**What the mockup has:**
- A luminous, gold-to-pink cumulus sky that fills half the frame.
- A sea of lit cloud tops with depth.
- A dozen distant floating islands fading into haze.
- A sun bloom.

Nalati's look pass ranks this third of eight ("grasslands live on the sky"). Sky Reach lives on its sky even more: the
sky is most of every frame.

**Fix:**
- Shard:
  - A painted golden-hour cumulus panorama **at infinity only**: one seamless matte, using the `scripts/horizon-matte/`
    pipeline and ND TECHNIQUES §5.2. Jake's no-screenshot-cheats rule holds: nothing painted in the playable space.
  - Distant island silhouettes in that matte.
  - The cloud sea as 2 or 3 layered shader sheets with lit tops and a soft parallax.
  - Storm clouds over the crown as soft lit volumes (billboard clusters with a lit-edge shader), not faceted blobs.
- Lead: whether the cloud-sea layers belong in the engine `horizon.cloudSea` rather than in the shard. Sunscar may want
  them too.

### 3. Materials: one flat colour per facet, everywhere

**Evidence:**
- `board-1` B and `board-3`. The island tops are flat olive with the 12-gon's radial fan facets visible: the fan of
  triangles from the island centre shows in every ground shot.
- The keels are violet-black facets.
- `board-4` A: from above, the islands are smooth inverted cones.

**Cause:**
- `islandMesh` uses `PALETTE.grass` / `rock` / `rockDark` as vertex colours, flat-shaded, with no texture, scatter or
  per-vertex variation.
- The generated models are flattened to faceted vertex colour with AO at 60 % (`world/meshes.ts`).
- `creatures.oneMaterial: true`.

**Fix (shard):**
- Island tops:
  - Grass clumps: instanced cards, wind-bent, olive root to gold tip.
  - Flower drifts (white and yellow), stones, and macro colour noise.
  - A mossy lip at the rim.
- Keels:
  - Warm brown-grey layered strata as a vertical gradient band, with cool shadow and warm edge light.
  - Hanging roots and vines.
  - One or two waterfalls spilling into the cloud sea, as in the mockup.
- Hide the radial facets: subdivide the top, or jitter its normals.

This is "toon" in name only: `kitLook: 'toon'` today means flat, while Driftwood's toon has bright colour variety, sand
gradients, foam and water. The mockup Jake picked is soft painted stylized, close to Nalati's painterly. The P2 builder
should chase the mockup and ask Jake once whether Sky Reach is "painterly" (Nalati's family) rather than "flat toon".
Do not decide that alone: the shards keep their own styles (Driftwood toon, Nalati painterly, Pine Hollow photoreal),
and taste is Jake's call.

### 4. Silhouettes and composition: identical cones at one height

**Evidence:**
- `board-4` A / B: eight islands, all 12-gon inverted cones.
- Six of the eight decks sit at exactly y 30 (`layout.ts` `DECK`), so the horizon line is one flat shelf.
- `board-3` B: the hover bridge isle is a perfect triangle in silhouette.

**What the mockup has:**
- Irregular rims and jagged hanging bottoms.
- Varied sizes.
- Small islands scattered high and low.
- The bridge sagging toward the next island.

**Fix (shard, presentation only, no new walkable content):**
- An irregular rim per island: noise the 12-gon radius and add 2 or 3 outcrops, keeping the colliders.
- Jagged keels with 2 or 3 hanging spires.
- 10 to 15 decorative, non-walkable islands at varied heights and distances: instanced, LOD, in the matte past 400 m.
- A gentle sag on the rope bridges' rails.

### 5. Model quality and density: sparse and dark

**Evidence:**
- `board-3` A: the windmill tower is near-black and its sails are flat code planks.
- The pines are dark cone silhouettes, about 5 per island.
- No props at all: no fences, lanterns, crates, rope coils or prayer flags.
- `board-3` C: the keeper's lectern sits beside a gale wisp, a grey ring and a crystal, that fills the frame.

**Cause:** the C6 Hunyuan3D models are good shapes (`board-7` C1 shows the posts). They are lit badly (item 1) and
flattened (item 3).

**What the mockup has:** a white stone tower with a slate cap and torn cloth sails, and something to look at every few
metres.

**Fix (shard):**
- The windmill to the mockup: white stone, slate cap, cloth sails that turn and flutter.
- Wind-bent painted pines.
- A small prop kit: fence, lantern, rope coil, pennant line, rocks.
- A density pass per hero scene.

### 6. First-person hands and the fan: no hands, and the fan is a navy triangle

**Evidence:**
- `board-5`: idle, SWING +90 ms and GUST +120 ms all show only a dark navy wedge at the right edge. The swing barely
  moves it.
- Every other shard shows hands:
  - Driftwood: skinned castaway arms.
  - Nalati: gloves on the bow.
  - Nine Dragon: gold gauntlets.
  - Pine Hollow: hunter gloves.
- The mockup shows a teal-and-bronze fan held open in a hand.

**Cause:**
- `fanModel.ts`: 7 navy (`0x2b3f78`) panels, 8 iron box ribs and a box grip.
- `WarFan.ts:41` sets position (0.33, −0.34, −0.62), rotation (−0.35, −0.25, −0.5) and **scale 0.5**. Rolled −0.5 rad
  and pitched −0.35 rad, the fan is seen almost edge-on and mostly off screen.
- No hands are mounted.

**Fix (shard, with the public kit):**
- Hands: `WeaponHands`, `HANDS_MATERIAL`, `gripPose` from `#kit`, and `gloveFist` / `ARM_PAL` from `#engine`.
- A fan model to the brief below.
- Four poses (idle, swing, heavy, GUST), plus a wind-ribbon trail.

### 7. The quest flow: no start, no guidance, no reward beat (the worst *feel* problem)

**Evidence:**
- `board-2` A: the first frame has no objective anywhere on screen.
- `board-2` C: the MAP tab has no quest card.
- Driftwood: B shows "◆ WHO LIT THE FIRE? | CASTAWAY 123 M ▲" from frame one, and D shows its MAP card.
- Nalati: E shows "TULPAR | BAQYT ATA 87 M".
- `board-3` C: the world pins are raw white text ("NOTES", "VANE") with no diamond and no distance.

**What Sky Reach does** (`quest/install.ts`, `plugin.ts`):
- It builds a `QuestState` with four steps.
- Nothing reads `quest.chip()` or `quest.markers()`.
- The steps have no `markers` and no `chip`.
- The quest has no `startWhen` and no `intro`: it is silently active at spawn.
- `fullMap.setQuest` is never called.
- `quest.onStep` is unset: no "Objective · …" toast and no sting.
- Step 2 ("clear the roost") has no marker at all.
- The winch pin stays hidden until step 4 unlocks.
- The only feedback is toasts and a 10-coin burst.
- The first step's notes sit on the keeper's isle. That is two bridges away, and the second is hover-only, but nothing
  tells you a hover bridge needs the board. On foot you fall through it.

**What Driftwood does** (`src/shards/driftwood-isle/quest/Spine.ts`, `questLine.ts`):
- Start:
  - `startWhen: talked:castaway`.
  - An `intro` objective ("Find out who lit the fire on the plateau") with a CASTAWAY marker.
  - Wendell at his campfire waves when you come within 16 m.
  - "Talk to Wendell" (`NpcTalk`) opens a `DialogueBox`, and the sword stows while you talk.
- Guidance:
  - Every step has `markers` with short names.
  - The `QuestChip` under the minimap shows the chip, plus the nearest marker's distance and bearing.
  - The MAP tab shows a card with chapter, objective and hint.
  - Each step fires a toast ("Objective · …") and a music sting.
- Reward: the finale is a staged beat ("Stand in the ring", the golden-hour reward view), then "Quest complete".

Nalati (`adventure.ts`) and Pine Hollow (`quest/index.ts`) wire the same pieces.

**Fix (shard, all public API):**
- `QuestChip`, `NpcTalk`, `DialogueBox` and `QuestState` with `startWhen` / `intro` / `markers` / `chip` / `hint`, all
  from `#game`.
- `ctx.game.runtime.play.fullMap.setQuest(...)`.
- `minimap.setMarks(...)` for the minimap diamonds.

What the quest needs:
- A **bridge-keeper NPC on Sunrest at the spawn**: mockup B. He names the goal and points at his notes. The notes become
  his dialogue, so step 1 becomes "talk to the keeper".
- Markers on every step: roost, the three vanes, the winch.
- The chip and the MAP card.
- A first-hover-bridge hint ("Glowing bridges hold only a board · HOVER").
- A reward beat:
  - The crown bridge rising as a held, framed moment: camera hold, wind, sting.
  - After the Roc, a sunset view from the crown, like Driftwood's ring.

**Engine gap (lead):** the quest presentation is re-wired by hand in each shard: Driftwood's Spine, Nalati's adventure,
Pine Hollow's quest. One `installQuestPresentation(ctx, quest, { npc?, markers })` that wires the chip, the map card,
the minimap marks and the world pins would make a polished start the default. A world-space quest pin style (◆ + short
label + metres) is also missing: `hud.pin` takes a bare element, so every shard styles its own, and Sky Reach styled
none.

### 8. HUD use: the baseline HUD with nothing of the shard in it

**Evidence:** `board-1`, `board-2`.

**What it lacks:**
- No quest chip (item 7).
- No weapon panel. The mockup has "GALE FAN 3 / 3", a GUST charge count, as Nalati has "BOW ARROWS 24 / 24".
- No first hints: Driftwood shows "DRAG TO MOVE" on frame one, and Sky Reach showed none in the same harness.
- The world pins are unstyled.

**Possible bug:** in `board-3` F and `board-4` D, the Storm Roc BossBar stays on screen after leaving the crown. The
script has `seal: () => undefined`. This was observed after a teleport away; confirm it by walking back over the bridge.
If it holds, the boss needs a leash (a shard `seal`, or a BossBrain disengage, which would be an engine gap).

**Fix:**
- Shard: the weapon panel (GUST charges, if GUST gets charges), the hints and the pin style.
- Any change to what a HUD control is goes through the E332 herdr notice, plus a board for Jake (AGENTS.md).

### 9. Creatures: the first one you meet clips through your face

**Evidence:**
- `board-1` B and `board-5` C: a drift ray's wing passes through the near plane at the spawn and fills the frame as a
  purple slab.
- `board-3` C: the wisp is a grey ring around a crystal.
- The Roc reads as a dark lump from below (round-6 / 7 boards).
- The goats are fine after G26.

**Cause:**
- `RAY_HOMES` circles (0, −24) at r 22, which is right over the spawn bridge. The ray dives at the player within
  seconds of the first frame.
- There is no camera keep-out.
- The ray is dark dusk violet. The mockup's manta is pale blue and translucent, with a glowing tail.

**Fix:**
- Shard:
  - Move the free ray's home off the spawn: let the first ray *show* itself gliding past, as in the mockup, and attack
    later.
  - Recolour or remodel the ray to the mockup: pale translucent fins, emissive tail.
  - The wisp as a visible wind knot: streak ribbons, not a ring.
  - The Roc's underside lit (item 1) and banded pale.
- Engine gap: a near-plane keep-out or fade for creatures. Any shard with diving flyers needs it.

### 10. Effects: the wind level has no wind

**Evidence and cause:**
- No grass sway, no wind streaks, no drifting cloud wisps, no birds, no dust motes, no waterfalls, no sail flutter.
- GUST is one faint white ring (`board-5` C).
- The updraft is a stack of white rings that fill the frame while you ride it (round-6 board C).
- The gale wall is a pale box.

**Fix (shard):**
- Wind streak ribbons (ND TECHNIQUES §5.3) along the gusts, the updraft and the island rims.
- GUST as a cone of streaks with leaves and petals.
- The updraft as a soft spiral of streaks and rising leaves, not rings.
- Grass and sail motion.

### 11. Lock-on (Jake: flyers lockable, a more generous range; the engine builder sol-lock owns it)

**Code facts:**
- The fan row has `ui.lockOn: true`.
- Flyers qualify by `flying` (`LockOnTarget.ts` `scan`). Their range is `FLY_ACQUIRE` 24 m, eye to body, unless
  `species.flight.lockRange` is set. **No Sky Reach species sets `lockRange`.**
- The rays circle at r 22, 14 m over the deck, so they spend much of their time 25 to 40 m away. The Roc circles 16 m
  out and 16 m up, about 23 m: right at the edge.
- **Sky goats can never lock.** Ground creatures need their kind in the engine's hard-coded `HOSTILE` set
  (`LockOnTarget.ts:58`), and `skyGoat` is not in it.

**Probe (`cap2.mjs`, `cap3.mjs`):** the LOCK disc never showed in 60 unaimed and 80 aimed samples at the spawn, the
roost, the keeper's isle and the crown, with flyers 0.4 to 39 m away. But my aim-probe could not confirm that the view
pitched onto the target, so treat this as **unverified**. sol-lock should reproduce it with its own harness.

**Route:**
- Engine:
  - Lockability as a species-row field, not a kind list.
  - A generous flyer range.
- Shard: set `flight.lockRange` per species (ray 32, Roc 40, wisp 20) once the engine's semantics land.

### 12. Title and Explore cards: one inline SVG for everything

**Evidence:** `board-6`.
- The title card and all four Explore cards (Models, Sets, World, Practice) are the same 390×844 SVG of three trapezoids
  (`explore/art.ts`, `thumbs/card.ts`).
- Driftwood ships live-captured WebP / JPEG per card.

**Fix (shard, P5):** capture the cards from the finished hero scenes: thumb 640×360, portrait 1024×1536, landscape
1600×900, and Explore cards 600×400 (practice 772×400) in `explore/` and `thumbs/`. Use the round-10 handoff's
`still()` method.

## Hands and weapon: the mockup brief (P3)

The reference is `mockup-C-hands-fan.jpg`, a live capture with only the viewmodel replaced. The P3 builder makes the
pose sheet (idle, SWING, HEAVY, GUST, stowed) in the Practice Arena from this brief, codex image_gen edits of
Practice Arena captures, one image per pose.

**Hand and forearm:** a right hand in a fingerless dark-brown leather glove, a brown leather bracer with a stitched
edge, and a wrapped cream linen sleeve. Build it with the kit's `WeaponHands` and `gloveFist`, in a Sky Reach palette.
Optionally the left hand appears on HEAVY.

**The fan:**
- Nine teal silk panels (≈ `#2f8a8c` to `#58b3ad`) with a pale cloud-swirl pattern.
- Bronze ribs with rivets at the pivot, a heavier iron guard rib on each side, a short wrapped grip and a red silk
  tassel.
- About 0.55 m across when open.

**Framing:**
- At idle the fan is open in a wide arc: its top edge near 55 % of screen height, its left edge near the centre, its
  face turned about 35° toward the camera so the panels read.
- It never covers the GUST / DODGE / JUMP cluster or the bottom bar.
- A warm rim light on the top edge from the low sun.

**Motion:**

| Move | What it does | Telegraph |

| Idle | a slow breath, the tassel swinging | — |
| SWING | the fan snaps half-closed on the backswing, then opens through a horizontal slash right to left, with a short wind ribbon | ≈ 0.25 s |
