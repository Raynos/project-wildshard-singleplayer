## No-shortcut check (ledger 5)

- **Staged state: reachable.** `stage()` at a9e50413 (`plugin.ts`) is unchanged from round 2: `logbook` sets only
  `SCOUT_FLAG` (Sefa's first talk), `waymarks-lit` runs the logbook interact, the well pull and jar, then each brazier's
  `spot.onInteract()` and `light()`. The dusk snaps to `duskOf` (0.25 for B, 0.86 for C and D), which play reaches by
  easing at 0.02/s (`look/dusk.ts`). All three are listed in `meta.json` `staged`.
- **A staged frame is a real play frame.** No frozen pose: B, C and D stage quest state only; the viewmodel is the idle
  hold; C carries its toasts, flame, embers and plume. No breach.
- **The caravan turned 180°** (6a4321dd7) so mock-B looks at the tailboard into the afterglow. That moves real geometry
  for every player, not a per-shot trick; h2 still frames it and the navmesh was re-baked. Not a breach. Parity (walk 0
  stuck, combat, leak, pause/resume) and the GPU ceiling are recorded at b69c79d16 (96c647c50), four sunscar commits
  before the capture; I did not re-measure them.
- **Painted stand-ins:** none in the playable area. The far range is at infinity. The dark rectangle on the far ground
  left of the tower (A, dusk-fire, `aerial-spawn` x 0.3–0.5, y 0.14–0.24) is a rendering defect, not a painted card.
- **Device and HUD:** 390×844 phone, stored 780 wide, baseline HUD, 30 fps chip in every frame, 0 page errors.

No breach found. The README's re-aim list is incomplete again (round 2's R2B-10).
