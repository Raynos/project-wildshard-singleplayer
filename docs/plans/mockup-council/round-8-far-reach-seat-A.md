2. **Make C's fan and hand the intended hero subject** — C x 0.3–1, y 0.49–0.82, then check A/B/D/proposal B for occlusion. 3. **Finish the Roc's visible eagle silhouette during actual flight** — D x 0–0.95, y 0.23–0.41. Retain the implemented bank and soar. Rework visible underwing colour/feather layering toward slate and white under crown lighting, and give the head, breast and talons readable volume. Adjust ordinary flight/animation so a sustained banked approach exposes broad wings and forward claws instead of a flattened side view. Capture an unstaged post-strike lap with actor position, altitude, yaw, bank, phase/state and timing; validate the stage against that observed trajectory rather than assuming the nominal circle is the actor path.

4. **Finish meadow diversity rather than adding more uniform straw** — A/B/C foreground, y 0.6–0.85; D and proposal B crest. Keep the restored strand detail and spread. Interlock varied blade heights/lean and dark green roots with restrained gold tips; place small daisies within the sward and expose articulated grey/lichen rock faces. Reduce B's slab dominance toward the existing target outcrops. Judge material/shape and the same clean luminance crop together; a matched fine-detail statistic cannot replace flower/rock distribution.

5. **Restore the separate sky moods without a blanket gamma change** — C x 0–1, y 0.05–0.5; proposal B x 0–1, y 0.05–0.56; D x 0–1, y 0.05–0.58. Concentrate the sun and distribute highlights along cloud/object edges instead of broadening the smooth yellow field. Recheck foreground Y after each change.

6. **Make crown stone and pennants weathered objects** — D x 0–1, y 0.51–0.85. Retain the chipped rim and compass pattern; add irregular surface relief, fractures and restrained lichen to the existing slabs/paving. Replace proud spiral tubes with recessed carved relief or an equivalent cut-stone surface treatment that holds up when approached. Vary flag wear, sag and stone-to-grass boundaries.
