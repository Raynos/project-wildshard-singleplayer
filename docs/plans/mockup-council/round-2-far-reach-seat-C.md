## Findings, ranked by score gained

1. **The bridge deck and rails: the crudest surface, centre-frame, in three views** (A, proposal B, C; x 0.15–0.8,
   y 0.45–0.65). Next to the new textured posts, the deck's flat-shaded planks with sawtooth facet edges, and the pale
   square dowel rails, look like a different game. Fix: give the planks the posts' painted wood (grain, wear, dark gaps,
   nails), and vary the plank ends and heights a little. Make the rails sagging rope hand-lines with rope texture, tied to
   the posts as the mockups show, with the stakes or netting of A and proposal B.
2. **Light the spawn into the sun** (A, B, C, proposal B; whole frame). `look/light.ts` adds a warm bounce from the
   anti-sun side strong enough to make every camera-facing face read front-lit. The disc sits high above the mill, while
   A has it behind the mill at the isle decks' height and proposal B at the horizon. Fix: lower the painted sun toward the
   horizon behind the mill (`PANO_SUN` and the panorama together, one sun). Cut the bounce so camera-facing sides fall into
   warm shade, and let the rim do the work (gold edges on the mill, the pines, the posts and the isle lips). Add aerial haze
   so the isles step back in depth instead of sitting crisp.
3. **Open the air under and around the bridge** (A, proposal B, C; x 0.2–0.8, y 0.5–0.6). Where the mockups show cloud
   sea and a chasm, the game shows a flat sage-green wall with root strips (the windmill isle's near cliff). The aerials show
   why: a short bridge to a wide isle. Fix: a longer bridge, or the mill on the narrower, higher rock spur that mockups
   B and C show, so the cloud sea is visible at eye level past the deck. Shade that cliff face like the other isle keels,
   not as a flat green plane.
4. **The fan's and hand's finish** (all five; centre-right and lower right). The leaf is good. The rest isn't: flat matte
   ribs, flat ochre guard plates, a bare pink grip and a plain cylinder sleeve. Fix: as mockup C shows, metal guard plates
   with studs and engraved corners, a worn edge and sheen on the lacquer, creases in the leaf, and a leather glove over the
   palm and knuckles (fingertips bare) with stitching. Add a cuffed bracer, and a cloth sleeve with folds. Then fix X1, so
   C shows this fan in a pose the player actually sees.
5. **D: the Roc clear of the bar, the sun tamed, the arena dressed** (D; y 0.2–0.85). Capture the Roc lower on its
   real stalk line (the stalk dives at the player, so a later frame of the same run has it below the bar, as the mockup
   does). Drop `sunGlow`'s new `wide` halo and the bloom at this view, so the sun is a gold disc low behind the stones, not
   a white blob under the Roc. Give the crown the spawn meadow's density with rocks and flowers, and no bare soil. Make the
   stones rough and lichen-blotched, with carved, unlit runes. Lower or trim the arena's far edge so the cloud sea and the
   isles show between the stones, as the mockup does.
6. **C's missing stone arch** (C; x 0–0.9, y 0.42–0.5). Mockup C has a stone span crossing above the bridge. It is in
   the mockup, and nothing in the game stands in for it. Build it, as real geometry with a collider, or say in the round
   notes why the layout can't have it.
7. **The sky isles** (A, B, C; y 0.07–0.4). The overhead cluster now sits in all three spawn views at the same high spot.
   Mockup A hangs it lower and right behind the mill, backlit, with long roots and moss. B and C show open cumulus there
   instead. Fix: move the cluster lower, behind the mill (A's position, which also takes it off the top of B and C), and
   give it long trailing roots. Take off the cotton-ball puffs stuck to the keels, which read as decals.
8. **The keeper's stand and the meadow** (B; x 0.4–0.6, y 0.5–0.68; A, B, proposal B foreground). Replace the
   square-bar lectern with a carved stand, an open book that reads, and the lantern hung from it. The spawn sward is
   better than round 1 but still one repeated tuft. Add the mockups' daisy clusters and the three mossy boulders at
   A's lower left, and swap C's lozenge boulders for the irregular, rough stone the mockups use.

SCORE sky-reach: 5.6
