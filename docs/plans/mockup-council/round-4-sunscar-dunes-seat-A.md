## Ledger §5: no-shortcut and reachable-state audit

- **Real geometry:** the captured source and wider frames show real terrain, tower, wagon, cargo, horse, tent, braziers and weapon meshes. No painted stand-in for a walkable subject was found. Sky/ranges are distant scenery; flame, smoke and glow are effects on real structures. The rectangular ground strip is an unresolved rendering defect, not evidence of a screenshot overlay.
- **Phone tier and baseline HUD:** the captured recorder requests 390×844 @3, phone tier and touch, then exports 780×1688 JPEGs. It restores HUD/viewmodel after aerials. All five scored frames retain the normal tracker, pins and controls. Keep those differences from old mockup UI; do not fabricate vitals/ammo, a distant READ prompt or a special toast layout to score better.
- **B `logbook`: reachable by source inspection.** `plugin.ts:stage()` sets only SCOUT_FLAG, Sefa's first-dialogue effect, leaving the logbook unread. The normal quest names it next. Dusk 0.25 is reached by ordinary easing. The camera is outside the logbook's 2.4 m interaction radius; the missing target prompt is not justification for a capture-only prompt.
- **C/D `waymarks-lit`: reachable by source inspection.** The stage invokes the actual logbook handler, well pull, jar interaction, then each brazier's oil interaction and light action. These supply the read label, raised bucket, removed jar, visible poured oil, fire/glow, labels and flags. C settles three seconds, beyond the bucket's 1.2-second lift; D settles eleven. Their dusk 0.86 is the ordinary three-lit-braziers target. The quest permits lighting them in any order, so the photographed waymark can be the player's final one. Both shots and B are listed in `meta.json.staged`. This is source verification, not a fresh normal-play walkthrough or an assertion of every final side effect from telemetry.
- **Real held pose and effects:** no staged weapon transform, attack freeze or dropped fire effects was found. These are ordinary idle frames; C retains flame, plume, embers, ground glow and completion notices. The recorder calms creatures, but before Sefa the ray is already held in its normal home glide by the real quest code. It remains animated; its absence in dusk-fire is a composition/state miss, not a frozen-pose shortcut.
- **Dusk-fire:** unstaged, with the Sefa objective and unlit tower. This is a reachable initial state, but not the target's burning-tower image. The lower score reflects that missing conjunction; do not claim the required milestone has been captured.
- **Views:** the README's three camera-change entries match the recorded blobs. B and D move toward their target relationships; dusk-fire's pitch moves the horizon toward the target. A/C are unchanged in the camera file. Neither capture records actual camera height (`camAt`), so terrain-driven height shifts cannot be established from the blobs alone. No deliberate dodge is evidenced, but the viewpoint audit remains incomplete.
- **No narrowing, budgets and shipping:** cairn removal is global and no quest/combat deletion was found in the inspected shard changes. Wider views retain the locations and creatures, but do not prove normal-play regression coverage. Commit `87b0c8daa` reports parity and GPU-ceiling compliance at nearby `4174b8d99`, before the latest sand change. The round surface does not supply exact `8221a34a` frame-time distributions, total loading/Explorer process memory or deployment identity. The recorder takes a caller-supplied SHA without checking the served build. Zero page errors, a 30 fps chip and a 30 fps encoded clip cannot close that proof gap. This is incomplete evidence, not a finding that a limit was exceeded.

No confirmed screenshot shortcut warrants voiding this numerical comparison. The listed quest stages are supported as ordinary play milestones. Exact-build compliance remains incompletely evidenced, and the images themselves remain below 8/10.

SCORE signal-dunes: 5.9
