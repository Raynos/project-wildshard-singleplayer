# E8c: the night elites (Sir Nibbles, Hot Mess, Riveting Rita) and the Night Shift quest

Slice rule: [README.md](README.md) · east index: [INDEX-east.md](INDEX-east.md). Absorbs **EB7** (night elites), **LW6**
(their finishes and trophies), their contract rows and the tier-3 quest **Night Shift** (L15: hunt a night elite).
Names are Jake's (19k). Opus (G848).

## 1. The showcase piece

`rule: night` (so storm hour too, E7b); each respawns at the next dusk and drops a weapon finish and a trophy:

| Elite | Band, L | Look | P1 → P2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sir Nibbles** | 1, L4 | a 2 m Crate Mite in a cardboard crown | hides as a crate stack, pounces (0.8 s rattle) → splits off four Mites and rolls (1.0 s lane) |
| **Hot Mess** | 4, L13 | a Welder with two torches, leaking glowing slag | twin flame cones (0.8 s) → a slag trail that lights the dark, a 360° flare (1.2 s ring) |
| **Riveting Rita** | 5, L16 | a Riveter with a lamp-plate and a nail-gun arm | lamp sweep (seen = a rivet volley, 0.7 s laser) → a plate charge, then a slam ring (1.0 s) |

Each plays the `night` music slot at full tension plus the elite host's sting (audio plan §2).

## 5. Risks

- A 4-minute night is short for an elite hunt: an elite engaged at dawn must not vanish mid-fight (the rule gates the
  spawn, never despawns an engaged elite).

## 6. Questions for Jake

1. **Do night elites stay through dawn once found?** (a) an engaged or aware elite stays until it leashes, then leaves;
   (b) it vanishes at dawn. **Recommended (a):** nothing is worse than losing a 60 s fight to the clock.
