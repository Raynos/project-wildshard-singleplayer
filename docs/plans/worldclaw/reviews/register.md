# WORLDCLAW-SHARD · the council's findings register

One row per finding, across every round. The ID stays the same forever. Statuses:
- **fixed**: the commit that fixed it;
- **rejected**: the reason, with evidence;
- **settled**: it re-argues the ledger ([WORLDCLAW-SHARD §0.2 D1–D56 and §0.3 R1–R33](../../WORLDCLAW-SHARD.md)) without
  new evidence;
- **escalated**: it went to Jake as a decision.

**Rules for reviewers** (in every round's brief):
1. **Don't re-raise a closed row** without new evidence (a file:line, a quote, or a fix that broke something).
2. **The bar:** would an executing agent fail, do the wrong thing, or have to guess? A finding needs a location,
   evidence and a concrete fix. Wording, style and "I would design it differently" are **nits**; nits never block.
3. **After round 1**, review only (a) the diff since the last round and (b) the battery. A finding outside the diff
   counts only if it's must-fix with evidence.

**Rounds:** 4 at most (Jake); the loop stops early after a clean round (zero accepted must + should). What is still
open after the last round goes to Jake as decisions.
