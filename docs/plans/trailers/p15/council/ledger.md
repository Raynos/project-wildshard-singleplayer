# P15 council — the frozen ledger (E472)

1. **Two trailers, two audiences** (Jake, 2026-10-10): one for builders (T15's V1 / V2 are its candidates), one for
   **players** of the user-generated worlds. This council is about the **player** trailer only.
2. **The model is WoW 2004's cinematic trailer:** *"Think of something like the classical trailer for World of
   Warcraft. The original World of Warcraft from 2004, the one that's not using in-game footage. Cinematic trailer. I
   want that for the gameplay."* No in-engine footage. 15 seconds.
3. **Rank by wow:** *"rank them by how amazing wow factor the trailer is going to be, how exciting would people be to
   see a trailer like this."* Then **make the top 3** and show Jake.
4. **Rejected before** (Jake, 2026-10-10): the 67 s CT2 montage — *"insanely AI … AI software clips put together"*;
   T15 V4 (a camera push over one painting) — *"not a moving trailer"*; T15 V3 (cards on a map) — *"just images"*.
5. **What Wildshard is:** an endless grid of shards (floating square lands), each built by a player in its own art style
   (Driftwood Isle toon pirates and sea; Nalati painterly steppe, horse riders, eagles, snow leopards; Pine Hollow
   photoreal forest, herds, the Antler King; Signal Dunes desert, signal tower, skitterers; Sky Reach golden floating
   islands, a roc; Nine Dragon Stack neon vertical city in rain), joined by a two-lane **highway** with roundabouts (not
   a glowing lattice). Players ride, sail, glide and fight across them.
6. **Labelled concept** on every frame and the end card.
7. **Tools:** OpenAI Image 2.5 (keyframes, character sheets as references), LTX-2.5 image-to-video (~8 min of model
   lock per 4–5 s shot; first-frame conditioning; fast limbs and close faces smear, wide / silhouette / strong poses
   hold), MiniMax Music 3, Stable Audio 3, ffmpeg cutting. Three trailers made in one session: ~12 shots in total plus
   re-rolls.
