# P15 — ten ideas for a 15-second cinematic trailer for players (TRAILERS Part D, E472)

Jake, 2026-10-10: *"none of this is a trailer for the gameplay … Think of something like the classical trailer for
World of Warcraft. The original World of Warcraft from 2004 … Cinematic trailer. I want that for the gameplay … One
trailer is for the building experience … The other trailer is for the MMO players who are playing the user-generated
content … 10 ideas … rank them by how amazing wow factor the trailer is going to be. Use a council on 10 ideas, and
then make the top 3 and show me."*

**The audience:** players, not builders. **The promise:** an endless world where every land you ride into is a
different game, in its own art style, made by someone else, and you cross between them. **The model:** WoW 2004's
cinematic: heroes, motion, scale, one big reveal, an epic score; no UI, no gameplay footage.

**What we learnt the hard way (T15, CT2):** a montage of unrelated pretty clips reads as *"AI clips put together"*;
a camera move over a still or cards on a map *"is not a moving trailer"*. So every idea below has **one hero or one
party carried through every shot**, **real action** (riding, falling, fighting), **cuts on motion**, and one reveal.
Every idea is made the same way (no in-engine footage): hero keyframes from OpenAI Image 2.5 with one character sheet
used as the reference for every shot (the same hero in every frame), animated with LTX-2.5 image-to-video (4–5 s a
shot, ~8 min of model lock each), cut to a MiniMax Music 3 cue with Stable Audio 3 hits; corner tag *Concept trailer ·
not gameplay* and the end card. LTX's known limits (CT1): fast limbs and close faces smear, so the action is staged in
silhouette, mid-shot or wide, with strong poses; big rigid things hold.

Ranked by the lead's guess at wow factor (the council re-ranks):

### 1. The Crossing
**Hook:** a rider at full gallop crosses the border between two shards, and **the rider and horse change art style
mid-stride.** **15 s:** a painterly Nalati steppe, a lone rider galloping toward camera (4 s) → low tracking shot
beside the hooves as they hit the highway's white line (3 s) → the same rider bursts out the far side **as a faceted
toon rider** onto Driftwood's beach, the horse rearing, surf exploding (4 s) → pull up: the highway runs on, a dozen
worlds in a dozen styles beyond (4 s, into the end card). **Wow:** the transformation is a new image nobody has seen;
it *is* the game's promise. **Risk:** the style change inside one take — made as a cut on the hooves crossing the line
(two takes, matched action), not a morph.

### 2. Every World a Different Game (one swing, six worlds)
**Hook:** one hero swings one sword, and **every frame of the swing is in a different shard.** **15 s:** a hero
draws a blade in a painterly steppe; the swing begins — cut — continues in a toon pirate ship's deck — cut — through
Nine Dragon's neon rain — cut — through Sky Reach's floating cliffs — cut — lands on a Pine Hollow beast; each cut on
the motion, the arc continuous across styles, faster and faster; the last hit lands in silence → title. **Wow:**
match-cut action; a legend-of-styles in one gesture. **Risk:** matching the sword's arc across six takes.

### 3. The Party
**Hook:** four adventurers from four different worlds, each still in their own art style, walk down the highway
together at dusk. **15 s:** feet on asphalt (2 s) → a slow low push across the line-up: a toon pirate, a painterly
steppe horsewoman, a photoreal pine ranger, a neon samurai, side by side (5 s) → they stop; a giant shadow falls
across them (2 s) → reverse: a colossal beast rising out of the next shard (Pine Hollow's antler king) (4 s) → they
draw weapons; cut to black, title. **Wow:** four styles in one frame; the hero walk; the monster reveal.
**Risk:** keeping four styles distinct in one generated frame.

### 4. Leap of Faith
**Hook:** a hero runs off the edge of a floating shard and falls through the sky past other worlds. **15 s:** a
runner sprints to Sky Reach's cliff edge and leaps (3 s) → freefall, camera falling with her, floating islands and
other shards whipping past, each a different style (6 s) → she opens a glider just above a neon city's rooftops and
swoops in (4 s) → title. **Wow:** vertigo and scale; the world as a sky full of lands. **Risk:** the falling figure.

### 5. The Charge
**Hook:** WoW's charge beat: a line of riders thunders out of one world into another. **15 s:** silence, a horn,
fifty riders on a ridge in painterly Nalati (3 s) → the charge, dust and hooves (4 s) → they cross the highway into
Signal Dunes at dusk, the signal tower blazing ahead (4 s) → a skitterer swarm erupts from the sand to meet them
(3 s) → title. **Wow:** mass and sound. **Risk:** crowds smear in LTX; staged wide and in silhouette.

### 6. The Dragon of Nine Dragon Stack
**Hook:** a creature reveal: something vast uncoils out of a neon vertical city. **15 s:** rain, a lone rider on the
highway approaching Nine Dragon's gate (4 s) → the lanterns sway; a rumble (2 s) → a dragon of neon and tile rises
between the towers, coiling up into the clouds (6 s) → it looks down at the rider; title. **Wow:** the classic
monster money-shot. **Risk:** one shard's world only, so "every world is different" is not said.

### 7. Border Duel
**Hook:** two fighters from two worlds, each in their own style, duel on the dashed line of the highway. **15 s:**
a toon pirate and a painterly nomad circle each other on the centre line, each half of the frame its world (5 s) →
they clash; on each hit sparks in both styles (6 s) → freeze on the clash; title. **Wow:** style clash as combat.
**Risk:** close fighting is LTX's weakest motion.

### 8. The Map Comes Alive
**Hook:** a hand-drawn map of the shard grid (V2's ink) and the camera dives into one ink plot, which becomes a real
painted world with a rider in it. **15 s:** ink map (3 s) → dive (3 s) → the ink turns into a living painterly
steppe, a rider galloping (5 s) → pull out over the highway to other worlds (4 s). **Wow:** the drawn-to-real dive.
**Risk:** reuses the builder trailer's look; the reveal is a transition, not action.

### 9. Night Convoy
**Hook:** players travel together: lantern-lit wagons and riders on the highway at night pass through world after
world. **15 s:** a column of lanterns on the road (3 s) → through a toon village, a neon gate, a pine forest, each a
cut as the convoy passes (8 s) → dawn over the grid; title. **Wow:** warmth, journey. **Risk:** gentle — less wow.

### 10. The Watchers
**Hook:** creatures of each world turn to watch a lone traveller pass on the road: a toon crab, a painterly eagle, a
neon koi, a stone giant. **15 s:** five heads turning in five styles (8 s) → the traveller, small, walks on → the
biggest watcher stands; title. **Wow:** charm and mystery. **Risk:** low action.
