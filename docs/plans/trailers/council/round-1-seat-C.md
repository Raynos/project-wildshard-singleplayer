## Battery

1. **Shot 6 end to end: fail.** The doc names no blockout geometry, frame count, look-still prompt, LTX settings or "done" criteria. CT1's #9 (`ltx_comfy.py layout`, 145 frames, seed 42) is the nearest recipe and is never cited. The time of day (b06 sunset vs lights on) and the shard's identity are unresolved (R1C-2, R1C-8).
2. **Shot 10 Nalati: fail.** No in-engine capture is named, nothing says how the painterly style is kept (R1C-14), "gallop" breaks the slow rule, and there is no plan for melted legs (R1C-11, R1C-15).
3. **Ledger on every shot: fail in part.** Highway, not lattice: pass. Each shard in its own style: fail (b09; R1C-14). Small, slow figures: fail on 10 and 12. No generated text: fail on b02's monitor. Label: start and end only (R1C-4).
4. **Lock busy: fail.** The doc gives no order and no list of what runs outside the lock. The score must come first because the timings hang on its hits (R1C-10, R1C-16).
5. **Bad take: fail.** Only "new seed"; no cap, fallback or give-up point (R1C-15).
6. **Continuity: fail.** Four towers, two authors, time of day out of order, no neighbour map (R1C-2, R1C-8, R1C-21).
7. **Muted phone: fail.** Two text moments in 67 s, a typed line too small to read, and unlabelled mid-loop frames beside the alpha trailer (R1C-3, R1C-4, R1C-18).
8. **Jake's first 5 s: fail.** A slow push on a still map, possibly behind a title card, with nothing happening (R1C-7).

Verdict: The world, the highway look and the method are right, but the cut lies to itself (the shard is on the grid before it goes live and has a different tower in every shot), the pitch is invisible muted and unlabelled mid-loop, and the build and pull-out shots can't be made the way they're written; fix the six must-fixes before CT3 starts.
