# T15 council, round 1 — pick four of twenty (E471)

You are one of three independent seats. You have not seen the conversation that wrote the ideas; that is on purpose.

**Read:** `docs/plans/trailers/t15/ideas.md` (the twenty ideas and the diagnosis of why the last draft looked like AI),
`docs/plans/trailers/t15/council/ledger.md` (settled facts — do not re-argue them without new evidence), and look at
`art/trailers/round-3-draft1/board-draft1.jpg` (the draft Jake rejected) and `art/trailers/round-2-highway/` (the look
references and the six shard paintings). Skim `docs/plans/MARKETING-SITE.md` and `docs/design/mmo/VISION.md` for what
Wildshard is.

**Your job:** pick the **four** ideas to make, as four variants of one 15-second trailer for the marketing site.

Score every one of the twenty, 1–5, on:
- **Concept** — does it sell Wildshard's one big idea (a world of shards, each built by a player with Claude Code) in
  15 s, to a stranger watching muted on a phone?
- **Not AI** — would a viewer who just called the last draft "AI clips put together" call this one made by a person?
  Be hard: generated motion, generic fantasy maximalism and portal / montage gags all count against.
- **Buildable** — can one agent make it to a high finish in ~2–4 hours with the ledger's tools? Name the riskiest step.
- **Memorable** — is there one image or gag someone would remember?

Then pick four that are **different from each other** (in idea and in technique), so Jake compares four real
alternatives, not four versions of one. For each pick: why it wins, the riskiest step, and one concrete change that
would make it better (you may merge or sharpen ideas; say so). Name any idea you think is a trap and why.

**Write** your answer to `docs/plans/trailers/t15/council/pick-seat-<X>.md` (your seat letter is given below), at most
120 lines: a table of all twenty with the four scores and a one-line note, then your four picks. Do not edit any other
file. Do not commit. Do not run `set-label.sh`. Do not start browsers, models or renders.
