# T15 council, round 2 — review the four made trailers (E471)

You are one of three independent seats; you did not see how these were made. Round 1 (`selection.md`) picked four of
twenty ideas; they have now been made as four 15-second trailers. Your job: judge the **results**, not the ideas.

**Read:** `docs/plans/trailers/t15/council/ledger.md` (settled facts; Jake's own words), `selection.md` (what each
variant was meant to be), `docs/plans/trailers/t15/ideas.md` §"What made draft 1 look like AI". **Look at** each
variant's frame sheet in `art/trailers/round-4-t15/review/` — `<variant>-sheet.jpg` is one frame per second (0.5 s,
1.5 s, … 14.5 s, labelled with the time), so you can follow the motion; `<variant>-notes.md` says what happens between
frames and what the sound is. You cannot play the videos; judge motion from the sheets and notes, and say so where it
matters. Also glance at `art/trailers/round-3-draft1/board-draft1.jpg`, the draft Jake rejected as *"insanely AI"*.

For **each** of the four, score 1–5 and justify in one line each:
- **Reads muted on a phone** — does a stranger understand "a world of shards, each built by a player with Claude Code"
  in 15 s with the sound off, on a 6-inch screen?
- **Not AI** — would the person who called draft 1 "AI clips put together" call this made by a person? Point at the
  exact frames that give it away, if any.
- **Finish** — timing, type, readability, the end card, the corner tag; anything that looks unfinished or broken.
- **Memorable** — the one image someone would remember.

Then: **rank the four**, say which **one** you would put on wildshard.io beside the alpha trailer and why, and list
up to **three concrete fixes per variant** (a frame or time, what is wrong, what to change), most important first.
A fix must be buildable in under an hour with the same technique; no new concepts.

**Write** your answer to `docs/plans/trailers/t15/council/review-seat-<X>.md` (your seat letter is given to you), at
most 120 lines. Edit no other file, commit nothing, run no `set-label.sh`, start no browsers, models or renders.
