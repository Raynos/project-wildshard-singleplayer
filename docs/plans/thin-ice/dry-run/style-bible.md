# Thin Ice · style bible (fjord dry run, P3; throwaway, E359 D61)

Jake's pick: **Aurora, kept simple** (direction 2, at Ink's cost). Anchors: `out/p3-glass-*.png`.

- **Palette:** night navy `#0b1626`, ice teal `#2fb7a8`, aurora green `#4cf2a0`, aurora magenta `#c04cf2` (accents
  only), snow `#e8f1f5`, timber brown `#5b3a26`, window amber `#f2a640`. Warm = only lamps and windows.
- **Light:** one moon light (cool), a hemisphere light tinted by the aurora, ≤ 6 real lamps; windows and lamps are
  emissive textures. Bloom only. No volumetric fog; a cheap height fog over the ice.
- **Sky:** an animated aurora shader on the sky dome; the ice reflects the sky through one environment map.
- **Ice:** one see-through surface (fresnel), cracks as a decal layer; under it the drowned village as dim, low-detail,
  fog-tinted geometry with no shadows. No planar reflection, no refraction.
- **Materials:** matte painted timber and stone, crisp snow caps, glossy ice. No photo textures.
- **Shape language:** clean faceted stylized shapes, chunky silhouettes, readable at 50 m; slightly exaggerated roofs and
  spires.
- **Do:** strong silhouettes against the aurora · warm windows as the only warm colour · cracks that read as paths ·
  the drowned roofs always faintly visible.
- **Don't:** photoreal scans · many real lights · busy textures · grey-brown mud tones · pure black shadows.
- **Kit look (E6):** the kit sword / bear / hoverboard take the palette (timber + iron, teal glints).
- **Asset route:** stylized: code + Blender models; generated models only for mid / far props, restyled.
