# Fjord dry run · P2 · three pitches (throwaway, E359 D61)

Vision (P1, Jake): "a frozen fjord where you are the last ferryman". Nothing added.
All three use the same 500 m geography (`../fjord-spec.json`: 11 places, 7 routes). Base cost ~34–43 agent-days.

## A · The Last Crossing
- **Sentence:** The fjord is freezing shut. Crank the last rope ferry across it, shore by shore, and bring three
  stranded settlements home before the ice closes.
- **Pillars:** moment: riding the ferry under the glacier as it calves · session: reopen one shore's landing and clear
  what came out of the ice · return (note): the ice line moves each visit.
- **Verb:** **new**: crank the rope ferry, a moving platform you drive along a rope line (+3–5 days).
- **Weapon:** custom harpoon (+2–4).
- **Enemies:** ice bear (kit bear, restyled); the drowned (new, +1–2).
- **Boss / antagonist:** the Ice Warden, a whale-sized thing under the ice that rises at the Glacier Mouth / the closing
  ice itself.
- **Places:** South Landing (spawn) · Ferryman's Village (hub) · the Lighthouse (landmark) · the Old Quarry (arena) ·
  Glacier Mouth (boss) · Ice Cave (secret) · Cliff Overlook (vista) · the Rope Ferry (traversal) · Signal Tower
  (objective) · the Frozen Wreck (objective) · Hot Spring (camp).
- **Happenings:** the glacier calves (an ice plume and a roar; seen from the village and the ferry) · a stranded boat's
  red flare (seen from the lighthouse and the overlook).
- **Critical path:** landing → village → ferry → wreck → signal tower → lighthouse → ferry → glacier mouth → village.
- **Cost:** base + 8–14 days.

## B · Lantern Line
- **Sentence:** Fog has swallowed the fjord. Zipline and rope-bridge along the cliffs to relight the lantern chain so
  the last boats can find their way home.
- **Pillars:** moment: the lantern chain lighting up across the fjord at dusk, seen from the lighthouse gallery ·
  session: relight one cliff's lanterns · return (note): the fog banks shift.
- **Verb:** **existing**: the kit zipline and rope bridge (after E9) (+0).
- **Weapon:** kit sword, a lantern in the off hand (+0).
- **Enemies:** ice bear (kit); fog wraiths that only fear light (new, +1–2).
- **Boss / antagonist:** the Fog Mother, fought on the lighthouse gallery / the fog.
- **Places:** as A, with the Signal Tower as the first lantern station and the Rope Ferry as the cliff line.
- **Happenings:** a fog bank rolls in from the sea (seen from the overlook and the tower) · a far lantern relights
  itself (seen from the village).
- **Critical path:** landing → village → cliff line → signal tower → overlook → lighthouse gallery → village.
- **Cost:** base + 3–5 days (the cheapest).

## C · Thin Ice
- **Sentence:** The ferry is gone and the fjord has frozen. Race the ice on a sled-board, fast and fragile, and find
  the village that drowned beneath it.
- **Pillars:** moment: skating over clear black ice and seeing the drowned roofs below · session: chart a new route
  across the ice before it cracks · return (note): new cracks each visit.
- **Verb:** **existing, restyled**: the kit hoverboard as an ice sled-board (+1).
- **Weapon:** custom harpoon (+2–4).
- **Enemies:** ice bear (kit); ice crawlers (new, +1–2).
- **Boss / antagonist:** the Bellkeeper, a long eel coiled around the drowned church's spire / the cracking ice.
- **Places:** as A, with the Frozen Wreck as the drowned church spire and the Rope Ferry as the broken ferry line.
- **Happenings:** the drowned bell tolls under the ice (ripples and a ring of cracks; seen from the overlook and the
  hot spring) · a crack races across the bay (seen from the village).
- **Critical path:** landing → village → bay run → wreck spire → ice cave → glacier mouth → village.
- **Cost:** base + 6–10 days.

**Jake's pick (2026-10-01): C · Thin Ice.**
