## 2. The battery

1. **Cold pickup of F0: fail.** SF1 passes: files, fixtures and done-when are named. SF4 fails: "all simulation code" and "the shared sim" are unbounded, no maths library is chosen (write one or port fdlibm), and physics determinism (Rapier SIMD) is missing (#3, #19).
2. **Build M1: fail.** You stall at SF8, whose done-when needs SF11 and SF15 (#8). You stall again at SF15/SF16 on the template's weapons, sky shader, day clock, playground and DOM pin (#1) and its function-valued rows (#2). Then you guess the SDK channel and injector toolchain (#10) and every budget number (#6).
3. **A fresh outside author: partly.** At M1 these exist: interactables (data), quests (SF12), one brain (SF13), AS scripts (SF11), the ledger (SF14). Missing: creature visuals (rigs and commons are L; SpeciesLook has 9 function fields), any item or reward kind (SF36 is L), markers / UI (SF28 is L), audio. The plan doesn't list these expected gaps, so the gap log can't tell expected from surprising (#1).
4. **The drive: fail.** SF17/18/20/22 cover the shape, but the car doesn't exist and lives in L (#17). The neighbour-sim policy is unset (#14), shard frames and determinism across the border are unset (#5), there's no tile cache for the stall (#35), and the caps the gate measures against are undefined (#6).
5. **Pine Hollow onto the grid: fail.** SF46 is one line, prescribes compression it already partly has, and has no target or order (#21).
6. **A future MMO server team: partly.** They can rely on the schema, the headless step, the hash test, the server/client split and the ledger interface. They'd find no declared replicated state (#11), no named sim runtime artifact (#30), no time or command contract (#29), and a world-frame and Rapier determinism story that only holds at the origin (#3, #5).
7. **Modes and toggles: fail.** DEVSERVER is undefined (#23). Dev mode exists as a live toggle, but its effect on the grid is unspecified. The default-on grid conflicts with ledger 5 (#7). The new first-run UX (grid vs title deck) has no Jake pick in any row.
8. **A look under the one-frame rule: partly.** SF19 keeps the old look as a Debug variant and sends Jake a board. But the per-pixel grade mechanism is undefined, no painterly family exists (#22), and it doesn't say whether the old full-screen look survives in explore mode (one shard at a time), the natural home for the variant.
9. **A hostile script: partly.** Fuel stops the loop, the memory cap traps the hoarder, the validated effects reject NaN, and the ledger rejects the replay. What the player sees, spawn floods and the fact-id derivation are unspecified (#13, #25).
10. **An API bump in alpha: fail.** It doesn't say how many API versions the client runs, how format / API / ABI versions relate, or what happens to the outside author's v0 shardfile after 72 h (#24). SF43 has no done-when (#18).

Verdict: The plan rightly puts compatibility proofs ahead of 80/20 percentages, but its M1 can't pass with the rows it has, its determinism story leaves out physics and the grid's coordinate frames, its budgets have no numbers, and its L lane has no exit, so fold in #1–#9 and size or split the secretly huge rows before it goes `ready`.
