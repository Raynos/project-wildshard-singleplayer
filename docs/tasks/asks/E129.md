## What changed (Driftwood only: no new flags or Settings variants)

1. **Wendell faces you** (`src/entities/npc/Castaway.ts`). The campfire used to be merged into his body mesh, so only
   his head could turn (±63°). The fire is now its own mesh, and body + head + arm hang off one `figure` pivot at his
   feet. Inside 6 m, or while the dialogue is open, the figure eases round toward you (rate 3/s, capped at 2.2 rad/s,
   so 180° takes ~1.5 s), the head leading. Walk off and he eases back to his idle facing. The feet don't step; at that
   rate the turn reads as a shuffle, not a moonwalk. One more draw call (the fire mesh).
2. **The sword rests lower** (`src/player/SwordMoves.ts` REST). Was hands `0.27, -0.33, -0.52`, blade dir
   `-0.34, 0.76, -0.55`. Now hands `0.31, -0.35, -0.52`, blade dir `-0.20, 0.58, -0.79` (tipped forward). On the phone,
   the tip went from ~60 px under the crosshair to ~110 px under it and further right. The fists sit just above the
   DODGE / JUMP discs. On desktop, the tip went from well above the crosshair to level with it, right of centre.
   Swings, charge, sprint and dodge keys are unchanged; only the rest pose they start from and settle back to moved.
   The audit's `0.33, -0.42` hid the fists behind the touch discs on the phone, so I dropped it.
3. **The sword is put away while you talk** (`src/player/Weapons.ts` `stowed`, wired by `src/game/quest/Spine.ts` →
   `AdventureWorld.stowWeapon` → `src/main.ts`). When the talk opens, the held weapon drops out of the frame over
   0.25 s on the existing swap `holster` blend, and its input is off: no swings, heavy or weapon swap. It comes back
   up over 0.25 s when the talk ends, or when you walk away. Before this, attacks were not blocked during dialogue.
