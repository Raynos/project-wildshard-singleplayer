## Physical isolation and default fix

Safari build `e5e8b8e-muloh54x`, `nineFacade=instanced`, lanterns on: Nine Dragon loaded, entered World Explorer, and rendered for over 90 seconds without another native kill. Inspector observed load peak ~746 MiB (0.782 GB), Explorer peak ~1005 MiB (1.054 GB), then garbage collection reduced it to ~0.69 GB. This succeeds at reproducing a working path but does NOT meet the strict 1 GB Explorer cap.

Chrome's default facade path independently failed on the connected phone: 15:13:17 WebContent PID79224 killed at 3,969,601 KB; retry PID79272 killed at 15:13:30 at 2,256,961 KB with ActiveHard2048MB. Inspector sampling only observed ~684 MiB before the native spike. Source-level root allocation remains unproven; evidence isolates the facade multi-draw path, not a JS leak or a measured VRAM total.

Wrap-up change forces the retained instanced facade path on MOBILE_DEVICE (actual mobile UA or phone tier), even with stored Auto or forced desktop quality. Desktop Auto retains multi-draw. Red paper lanterns remain enabled by default. Clean-export validation, deployment, then repeated default-setting physical acceptance are required.

The mobile guard shipped as `135652ba`, live `135652b-muloyex9`, Actions `36478136455` successful. E272 subsequently broadens policy to every platform and shard: remove facade multi-draw code, shader branch and toggle entirely. Local real-scene regression rejects the historical batching implementation and passes desktop, phone tier and iPhone-with-desktop-quality after removal. Physical hard-budget acceptance remains open.

## 2026-09-30 Simulator check (E283 / model migration era)

**A regression check, not phone evidence.** The Simulator runs on the Mac's memory and GPU. The phone killed WebContent at
multi-GB while the Simulator stayed under 1 GB (E271 / E272). Nothing here says whether the phone now meets the caps.
Only a physical reading can say that.

Setup: iPhone 17 Pro Simulator (`wildshard-iphone`, iOS 26.5, Safari; DPR 3, canvas 804 × 1428), through
`scripts/sim-lane.sh run`, and shut down after. Each run had a cold Safari relaunch. The builds were clean `git archive`
exports served with `vite preview`. Nine Dragon was entered the way ENTER WORLD on the start title enters it (the
one-shot arrival, `?chunk=nine-dragon-stack&mute=1`). The phases:
- **loading:** until the loading screen is gone, about 17 s from localhost;
- **play:** 60 s at the spawn, the player turning a full circle;
- **explorer:** pause ▸ EXIT TO MAIN ▸ EXPLORE WORLD ▸ World explorer, then 60 s of flying at Normal speed (out-and-back
  legs with a finger turning the view). The camera stayed over the city: ALT ~160 m, 89 calls, 1.1 M tris on the
  readout.

Two meters were read:
- **Native:** the kernel's WebContent physical footprint, as `ios-memory-watchdog.py` reads it (`proc_pid_rusage` v4). The
  interval high-water counter is reset at each phase start, so a burst between the 100 ms samples still counts. The table
  gives the game tab's process. The sum over every WebContent process adds a ~0.04 GB prewarmed process.
- **Inspector:** Web Inspector `Memory.trackingUpdate`, the categories summed. This is the meter behind the physical
  1.054 GB Explorer peak.

GB are decimal. Two alternating runs per build.

| Build | Loading, native / Inspector (cap 1.8) | Play at spawn, native / Inspector | World Explorer, native / Inspector (cap 1.0) | GPU process, native |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HEAD `d82c6cc9`, run 1 | 0.741 / 0.780 | 0.481 / 0.789 | 0.484 / 0.745 | 0.21–0.23 |
| HEAD `d82c6cc9`, run 2 | 0.750 / 0.831 | 0.505 / 0.785 | 0.502 / 0.751 | 0.21–0.24 |
| pre-E283 `f9ca6490`, run 1 | 0.720 / 0.748 | 0.493 / 0.855 | 0.493 / 0.741 | 0.24 |
| pre-E283 `f9ca6490`, run 2 | 0.693 / 0.746 | 0.476 / 0.837 | 0.520 / 0.736 | 0.24 |

- **Both builds are under both caps in the Simulator, with room:**
  - native loading ~0.75 of 1.8;
  - native Explorer ~0.50 of 1.0;
  - Inspector Explorer ~0.75 of 1.0.
  No WebContent process died or restarted in any run.
- **What moved since `f9ca6490`** (the E283 per-LOD instance buffers, `place()`, the E281 pass):
  - loading is slightly higher: +0.04 GB native (0.745 against 0.707 on average) and +0.06 GB Inspector;
  - play is lower on the Inspector: −0.06 GB (0.787 against 0.846);
  - play and Explorer native are flat, within the run-to-run noise (±0.03);
  - the GPU process is ~0.02 GB lower.
  Nothing moved by more than 0.06 GB.
- **Closest to a cap:** the Inspector Explorer figure, 0.75 GB against 1.0. That is the meter on which the phone read
  1.054 GB. At that time the Simulator's own peaks (E261, `e5e8b8e`) were the same size as today's, so the gap
  between the Simulator and the phone is large and unknown. The phone may still be over 1.0 GB. Physical hard-budget
  acceptance remains open.
- The raw JSONL (samples per 100 ms, per PID) and the driver (`nine-sim-drive.mjs`, `sim_mem_phases.py`) are in that session's scratchpad, not in the
  repo.
- Caveat: another session's `serve-build.sh stop all-mine` stopped the preview server for `f9ca6490` run 2, 44 s into
  that run (during play). By then the load was done, the Explorer entered and rendered textured (its end screenshot),
  but anything streamed after that point would have failed. Its numbers agree with run 1's.
