flickering black square. The stylized cumulus' silver-lining fresnel was `pow(1 − |N·V|, 2.5)`: a flat facet square to the
eye rounds |N·V| a hair past 1 (iOS runs mediump), `pow(negative)` is NaN — in front of the clouds, as the user saw. The
same trap sat in the gas giant's limb haze (`pow(1 − mu)`) and the ocean fresnel. All three clamp to [0, 1] now
(StylizedSky.ts, Sky.ts, Ocean.ts); no visual change otherwise. Headless Chrome runs highp, so it never showed there:
the proof is the user's iPhone — look at the sky over the spawn cove for a minute.
