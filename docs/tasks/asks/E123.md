# E123

**Status:** done (2026-09-24) — commit 31d34c8, live build `31d34c8-mughr39k`. Phone shadows on Driftwood are 2048² by default; A/B on the device with `?pshadow=old` (before) · `?pshadow=2c` (runner-up) · `?pedge=0.35` (fainter warm rim)
**Ask:** (in world, on the sailboat's deck, phone) The shadows are really messing up something. There's a whole bunch of diagonal stripes on top of this boat. I have no idea what's going on.

## What caused it

- **The diagonal stripes were the old boat.** The first boat (now only `?boat=v1`) had zero-thickness two-sided
  lofted strakes. Every strake shadowed the deck and itself along its triangles, so the deck showed diagonal bands. The
  E113 rebuild (31a5400) made the hull a closed, front-sided mesh, so the shadow pass draws its back faces. Reproduced at
  phone tier: `?boat=v1` still stripes at morning and golden hour, and the current boat has none at morning, golden hour
  or a 10° dawn sun. See `progress/238-…` (left).
- **The "blocky / blobby / pixelated / bleeding" look came from shadow resolution.** On a portrait phone the camera's
  vertical FOV is 94°. The one 80 m cascade's square is therefore **189 m** across, not the ~130 m assumed. At 1024² that
  is **18.5 cm per shadow texel**. three r186's PCF (5 Vogel taps, rotated per pixel by interleaved gradient noise) and
  the toon band `smoothstep(0.25, 0.75, shadow)` made each 18 cm stair a soft blob with a halftone-dot fringe. The warm
  terminator band rode that 20–30 cm penumbra as an orange halo.
- **The shimmer was the light step, not the camera.** Moving the camera does not make the shadows swim. After a refit
  for a camera yawed 0.5° / 2° or stepped 3 cm / 20 cm, then drawn from the original pose, ≤ 0.003 % of pixels changed:
  three's CSM keeps a fixed-size, texel-snapped square. The pops come from the day clock's 0.25° shadow-direction step
  (E89, about every 1.5 s). Each step re-rasterises every edge, and at 18.5 cm texels **10.6 % of the boat frame changed**
  per step.

## Options measured (phone tier, 390×844 @3×, play mode, this Mac)

GPU time comes from `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` around one frame (`composer.render`), taken as the median of 80
frames. Shadow cost is the frame with shadows on minus the frame with `shadowMap.autoUpdate` off; the game loop was held
during the timing. Pop is the % of pixels changing (> 24/765) on one 0.25° light step. Views: boat deck, hut deck, sand
by palms, pier.

| rig (`?pshadow=`) | texel near | pop per step (boat / hut) | shadow pass GPU ms (boat / hut / sand / pier) | frame GPU ms (hut / sand) | shadow tris (hut) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `old`: 1×1024 to 80 m (before) | 18.5 cm | 11.2 % / 7.3 % | 0.10 / 0.39 / 0.37 / 0.08 | 5.35 / 5.35 | 532 k |
| **`2k`: 1×2048 to 80 m (default now)** | **9.2 cm** | **6.4 % / 4.6 %** | 0.07–0.12 / 0.35–0.40 / 0.40–0.45 / 0.13–0.14 | 5.35 / 5.42 | 532 k |
| `2c`: 2×1024, near cascade to 14 m | 3.5 cm (to 14 m), then 18.5 cm | 1.9 % / 2.7 % | 0.23 / 0.54 / 0.50 / 0.23 | 5.78 / 5.94 | 786 k (+48 %) |
| `near`: 1×1024 to 55 m | 12.7 cm | 5.7 % / 4.2 % | ≈ old | ≈ old | 518 k |
| `2048x2@80/14` | 1.7 cm near | 1.0 % / 2.2 % | 0.27 / 0.61 / 0.56 / 0.30 | 5.81 / 5.86 | 858 k |

- **2k:** the phone's shadow pass is bound by geometry, not fill, so 4× the depth texels cost about nothing: ≤ +0.07 ms
  on this GPU (within noise), no extra draws or triangles. Shadows are half as wide per texel, the dot-fringe is gone and
  a step pops half as much. It does use **+12 MB of GPU memory** (a 32-bit 2048² depth map is 16 MB, against 4 MB).
- **2c:** the sharpest near the player, but it re-draws every caster that meets the near square (+48 % shadow triangles,
  +16–32 draws). It costs +0.13–0.15 ms of shadow pass and +0.4–0.6 ms of frame here, several times that on an iPhone.
  Its PCF radius of 0.6 texel is small at 3.5 cm, so edges read as crisp stair-steps, and a seam shows at 14 m where it
  hands over to the coarse far cascade.
- **near** (shorter reach): a small gain, and shadows stop 25 m sooner. Not worth it on its own.
- **Normal bias:** it is now held at 0.76 shadow texel (0.14 m on the old map, 0.07 m on 2k, 0.025 m in 2c's near
  cascade), read each frame from the cascade's live width. No acne at a 10° dawn sun, golden hour or morning on the boat,
  hut deck, pier or sand. Contact shadows sit closer to their casters.
- **Orange toon edge band (f):** it narrows with the penumbra on 2k. The shader now weighs the facet's turn and the cast
  shadow's edge apart (`uToonEdge`, default 1, which looks as before). `?pedge=0.35` dims the warm rim on cast-shadow edges
  on the device. Its default is Jake's taste call, so it was not changed.
- `bench.budget.json` / `scripts/bench-load.mjs` measure load (bytes, time to play), not frame time. They are
  unaffected: no new assets, and the map is allocated at boot as before.

## What shipped

- **Default on the phone, Driftwood only:** one 2048² cascade to 80 m (`2k`), with the texel-relative normal bias
  (src/world/Sky.ts `shadowRig`, `fitNormalBias`). Desktop keeps 3×2048 to 220 m. Pine Hollow keeps 1×1024 and Nalati
  its 512²; both were checked at phone tier, rig unchanged, no errors.
- **Switches for the on-device A/B:**
  - `?pshadow=old`: before E123.
  - `?pshadow=2c`: the runner-up, 2×1024 with a 14 m near cascade.
  - `near`, or any `<size>x<cascades>@<far>[/<split>]`, e.g. `2048x2@80/14`.
  - `?pedge=0.35`: a fainter warm rim round cast shadows.
  - `?pshadow=` also works on the other shards' phone tier.
- Evidence: `progress/237-e123-phone-shadows-before-after.jpg` shows the same camera BEFORE | AFTER | `2c` on the boat
  deck, the hut deck and the sand. `progress/238-e123-boat-stripes-cause-and-golden-crop.jpg` shows the old boat's
  stripes against the current boat, and a full-res golden-hour crop of the hut deck before and after.
