## Jake's decisions (grill, 2026-10-03, AskUserQuestion)

1. **An ask is a receipt.** It is only Jake's words and closes when the reply or work lands. Follow-up work is a plan row.
   One queue: `docs/plans/`.
2. **Leftovers:** build them in the session that found them. If one truly can't land, it becomes an open row of a live
   plan with an acceptance line. Never a new ask. A plan can't archive while it has open rows.
3. **Bulk-close the 86** (33 done, 20 dead, 33 folded), each with the audits' evidence.
4. **Orphan small bugs** (E354 trails, Pine's black pond, E106 stipple, E338 serve-build, …): build them now, in this
   cleanup. A big one becomes a row in its plan.
5. **Phone checks: drop them all.** Agents stop filing iPhone checks; Jake's playtests and FEEDBACK notes are the check.
6. **Exception:** a risky rendering or memory change (batching, multi-draw, texture or memory policy) still needs a
   physical-device reading. It ships default-off behind a Debug row until it has one.
7. **Old needs-picks:** one decision page now, then a **7-day expiry**. An unanswered pick is dropped as "Jake approved
   none".
8. **Handoffs:** one live `## Handoff (<lane>)` section per lane, overwritten in place, deleted when the work closes.
   E357 / E374 history moves to `project/archive/`.
9. **Claims: a 71 h lease.** The brief shows a claim with no commit naming its ID in 71 h as expired, and anyone may take
   it. `folded` / `superseded` / `answered` count as closed.
10. **The brief prints** the live plans' State lines, open reviews, unanswered asks, expired claims and picks past
    7 days. Nothing else.
11. **Archive** `docs/tasks/ASKS.md` and the finished council / review round files to `project/archive/` (links fixed).
    New rule: a council's rounds are archived the day it ends. Closed ask files stay in place.
12. **Enforce** with `scripts/check-asks.mjs` in the pre-commit guards: the Status enum, a fold must name its target,
    one Handoff per lane, new asks only via `ask-new.sh`.
