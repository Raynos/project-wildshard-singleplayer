## Concepts (round 1)

Three portrait phone mockups (local Qwen-Image-2.1 turbo, `scripts/mockup-local.sh`), each an edit of the real phone
capture `progress/normalization/x1-board/pine-hollow-verbs-a.jpg` (390 × 844, baseline HUD). The HUD keeps the
baseline layout; only the weapon strip, the quest pill and the weapon's own verb labels change. Prompts sit beside the
images (`*-prompt.txt`).

- **A — Frozen Reach** (ice shelf at the edge of the world). Faceted blue glacier blocks, crevasses, a black sea of
  broken floes, a low sun under a green aurora. **Weapon:** a bone-and-iron harpoon on a rope: THROW hurls it, REEL
  pulls a hooked creature in (or you to an ice ledge). **Creature:** the tusker, a walrus-beast that swims under the
  ice and bursts up through a crack where you last stood (telegraphed by a spreading crack ring). **Quest step:** light
  the beacon cairn on the far ridge.
  `art/far-reach/round-1-proposals/A-frozen-reach.jpg`
- **B — Sky Reach** (floating islands above a sea of cloud at golden hour). Grassy rock islands joined by rope
  bridges, a ruined windmill, waterfalls spilling into the clouds; HOVER is the way between islands. **Weapon:** a
  gale fan: SWING slashes, GUST blows a cone of wind that knocks things back (off edges). **Creature:** the drift ray,
  a manta that circles in the air and dives along a lane at you, then glides back up out of reach. **Quest step:** raise
  the fallen rope bridge to the windmill island.
  `art/far-reach/round-1-proposals/B-sky-reach.jpg`
- **C — Glow Reach** (bioluminescent mangrove swamp at night). Plank boardwalks over black water, arching roots,
  teal and violet glowing mushrooms, drifting spores, a big moon. **Weapon:** a spore sling: FIRE lobs a glowing pod in
  an arc that bursts into a poison cloud; PODS swaps the pod. **Creature:** the lantern toad, which puffs its glowing
  throat sac (a ring telegraph) and bursts a stun pulse, then hops away dark. **Quest step:** find the lost light (carry
  a glow pod back to a dead lantern shrine).
  `art/far-reach/round-1-proposals/C-glow-reach.jpg`

  1. *Is the player on the hoverboard?* Needed by the hover bridges' collider gate and glow (`plugin.ts`
     `isHovering`). ENGINE.md has no verb, event or field for it (§6 lists `hover` as a mechanism, §21 the
     `Hoverboard` tool, nothing readable). Used the undocumented `ctx.game.runtime.world.player.hover` on the Player
     port. Wanted: a documented `app.player` movement mode (`'foot' | 'board' | 'swim'`) or a `player.mode` event.
  2. *A collider for one movement mode.* Covered: a registry piece's `active()` (ENGINE §14, §22) re-evaluated every
     step does it, gated on gap 1. No gap beyond gap 1.
  3. *A flying creature.* Nothing in §19 says how a creature leaves the ground (`SpeciesRow` / `CreatureBrain` /
     `StrikeRunner` move it in xz only; `Animal` ground-follows). Used the undocumented `Animal.driven` flag and wrote
     `position` / `yaw` from the brain (`species/manta.ts`), as Nalati's ridden horse does. Wanted: a documented
     altitude / flight body for a brain (Nalati's eagle uses `yOffset` + `mem`, also undocumented).
  4. *Push a creature (GUST) and know it fell off an edge.* `DamageRequest.knockback` exists but ENGINE.md doesn't say

     `Animal.position` itself and kills anything over the void with `app.combat.hit({ amount: 1e4 })`. Wanted: a
     documented creature impulse and an out-of-world / fall death cause.
  5. *A new shard's own `style` word.* `ShardManifest.style` is a closed union (`toon · painterly · pbr · jiehua ·
     greybox`); Sky Reach says `toon` though its look is its own. Wanted: an open string or a new member.
  6. *Relabel the attack disc SWING.* §10 shows `touch.relabel` but not which `DiscSpot` is the attack disc for
     `mode: 'melee'`; the fan keeps the default attack label and adds GUST as `verb.1`.
  7. *Doc nit:* SHARDS.md §3 says the manifest imports `buildTerrain` from `#engine/data`; the template's manifest imports it
     from `#engine`. Sky Reach uses `#engine/data`.
