# E90

**Status:** done (2026-09-23) — 29c9e69, live build 29c9e69-mueuyxwm; the iPhone check (walk the cove) is Jake's
**Ask:** As I move around the trees and shadows pop in — fix that permanently on Safari iOS

**Cause (Driftwood, the Blender cove — the default island):** `src/world/BlenderIsland.ts` swapped each 6×6 tile of
palms / rocks / logs to its 227-tri far copy at **24 m** on the phone, and on the phone the far copies cast **no
shadow** — so walking around, whole clusters of palms changed shape and their shadows switched off and on. The ground
cover tiles (ferns, hibiscus, bushes, flowers) blinked on and off at 32 m. Animals' shadows switched on at 30 m.

**Fix:**
- palms keep their full model to 110 m on the phone too (the desktop's distance) and every far copy casts a shadow, so
  no palm swaps inside the 80 m shadow range and no shadow comes or goes at a swap;
- ground cover rises out of the ground over the last 10 m before its tile's cut (vertex shader, like GroundCover's
  grow-in): it is sunk under the terrain when the tile switches;
- phone animal shadows to 80 m (the cascade's reach), `src/core/tier.ts`.

**Cost (phone tier, headless, four cove poses, before → after):** pier 0.80 → 0.98 M tris, beach 0.89 → 1.16 M,
inland 1.12 → 1.34 M, east cove 1.16 → 1.37 M (+18–31 %); draws 116–130 → 148–160. If the iPhone drops frames in
normal power mode, `LOD_D` is the knob.
