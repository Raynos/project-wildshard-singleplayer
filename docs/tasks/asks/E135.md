**Step 1 (this commit): the phone reports what happened.** `src/core/lifeTrace.ts` writes a ring of lifecycle lines in
localStorage `wsLifeTrace`: boot (with the navigation type: `reload` means iOS relaunched it), world ready (with the
`paint` entries: none means the DOM never painted), hide, show (away time and which path), lost, restore, reload.
Each line carries the layout: window, visualViewport scale and offset, the canvas box.
- It is sent home through the E133 error reporter as system `lifecycle`: no chip, no modal. `pnpm inbox:pull` shows it
  as category `error`, `[lifecycle] …`.
- A report goes when the page comes back after more than 60 s away (6 s of frames counted first).
- A report also goes when a boot follows a page that ended hidden or on a reload (20 s + 6 s after boot).
- `GpuRecovery.show()` also forces a DOM repaint (a two-frame opacity 0.999 on `<html>`). This is a cheap guess at the
  unpainted DOM layers. The report will say whether it was enough.
