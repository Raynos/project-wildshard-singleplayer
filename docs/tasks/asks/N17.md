**Ask:** The horse riding is very non-intuitive: as I move the horse I expect the camera to follow; a horse doesn't strafe — a full new 8-directional movement system intuitive for horses; the horse is moving and I'm still looking the wrong way; the horse's head turns so fast, nothing about it is real. Find examples of first-person horse riding in big polished AAA games, see how it works there, then improve it. + the DISMOUNT button is too big — a small toggle on the right edge like the other things


**Evidence:**
- fa2ba06: the reins steer in the horse's frame with 8 sectors and no strafe; the turn rate falls with speed and eases in;
  speed has inertia; the view follows the horse's heading, and a free look drifts back after ~1 s (held while you draw or
  lock on); the neck leads each turn; the camera bob is smaller; the eye stays over the saddle on slopes. The saddle owns
  the camera again: since the Rapier merge, Player.step/update had put it at the on-foot eye, inside the neck. The HORSE
  tab now reads DISMOUNT in the saddle, and the USE band's DISMOUNT hides. GALLOP sits in JUMP's spot; HOVER hides while
  you ride; LEAN L/R and OFFER are placed.
- ca80143: the aim plate and arrows no longer hit your own horse (CreatureBodies.cast skips hidden creatures); the rider's
  capsule stays out of the world while riding.
- Research and the chosen design with its numbers: docs/design/nalati/riding-research.md. The user picked "stick steers,
  camera follows".
