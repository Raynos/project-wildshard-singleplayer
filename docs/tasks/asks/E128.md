## What was left, and what was done (2026-09-25, E128 subagent)

The phone audit (art/driftwood-audit/round-1-phone/README.md) listed five bugs. E125 fixed bugs 1–4. E123 (the user's pick
`2c2k`, shipped in 813cab2 after the audit) had already fixed most of bug 5, the coarse phone shadows.

- **Bug 5, phone shadows: the part still broken.** E123 made the near cascade 1.7 cm a texel, so the blobs and the orange
  halftone halo are gone. Its PCF radius stayed at 0.6 texel, though, so every palm, stair and railing shadow edge near
  the player drew as a serrated saw. At full device resolution that is 4–6 px teeth along each diagonal (E123 called them
  "crisp stair-steps").
  - **Fix:** src/world/Sky.ts `PHONE_NEAR_RADIUS`. The phone rig's near cascade filters at 1.2 texels, a ~2 cm
    penumbra. The edges come out clean and still sharp. The far cascade (9 cm texels) keeps 0.6.
  - **Why 1.2:** 2.0 was tried too. It brings back the dotted IGN fringe on thin fronds and widens the warm rim.
  - **What it does not touch:** shadow direction, shadow colour, the toon terminator band (T4) and the rig.
  - **Scope:** Driftwood phone only (`stylized && rig.phone && cascades === 2`). Desktop, Pine Hollow and Nalati don't
    reach this line: their rig has `phone: false` and `stylized` is null.
  - **A/B switch:** `?pradius=0.6` gives the look before this fix.
- **Cost:** none measured. three r186's PCF is 5 hardware-filtered taps at any radius, so only a uniform changes.
  - **Method:** GPU time from `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` around `composer.render`, on the iPhone 16 Pro viewport
    (402×874 @3×) at the phone tier, in play mode. Radius 0.6 and 1.2 were measured in alternating 20-frame blocks in
    the same page, 160 frames each, on a quiet round.
  - **Median frame (0.6 → 1.2):** boat 1.169 → 1.159 ms, hut deck 1.734 → 1.783 ms, sand by the palms 1.598 → 1.595
    ms, pier 1.174 → 1.174 ms. The p25 values match to ±0.1 ms, and a second, busier round was noise both ways. The
    Explore HUD reads 60 fps at every spot.
- **E125 re-checked on live `188fc54-muguhb85`**, iPhone 16 Pro, `?explore=world&touch&tier=phone&mute=1`. All four
  still hold:

  - **Gully under the rope bridge:** brown and grey walls down to the waterline, no blue faces above it.
  - **Horizon at 110 m:** the islands stand on the hazy far sea, with no straight cut.
