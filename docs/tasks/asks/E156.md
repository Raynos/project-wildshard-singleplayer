## Why distant foliage isn't there (Driftwood, the phone tier; the desktop reaches 1.25× further)

It is not a depth or z-order bug. The game has no occlusion culling. Frustum culling uses a padded frustum. Driftwood's
cover is solid low-poly geometry with no alpha textures, so no mip-alpha loss. **The plants past a set distance are
never drawn at all.** Every foliage system draws a bubble round the camera:

| system | what | drawn to (phone) | how it hides further out |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GroundCover.ts`, near tier | tufts, ferns, hibiscus, bushes, daisies, pebbles | full size to 8–16 m; each plant's own edge in 14–38 m | shader: past `near` it takes the terrain's colour (`uBlend`), then shrinks to 0 at its edge |
| `GroundCover.ts`, far tier (E117) | 3–8-tri stand-ins | 16–76 m | shrinks and turns ground-coloured at a second edge |
| `BlenderIsland.ts` cover tiles | the Blender cove's small cover | 16–40 m | shader sinks it 3 m into the ground; the tile turns off at 41 m |
| everything past ~76 m | — | — | **nothing: bare flat-shaded terrain** |

Each kind also has a fixed instance cap (e.g. far tufts 7 920). That is why the bubble can't simply be made bigger:
a test with every reach ×4 filled the caps with far plants and the near ones vanished.

**Why nothing has fixed it so far:** every fix (E90, E117 per-plant edges, grow-in, blend to ground, the far tier) changed
*how* plants enter the bubble: pop, then grow, then recolour, then a cheaper model. None of them gave the distance
anything to look like. The terrain past the bubble never carries the cover's look (its colour, its lumps, its red
hibiscus), so the edge is always bare ground turning into dressed ground, and you see that as "appearing". At eye height the
whole 14–100 m range sits in a strip about 5 % of the screen tall just under the horizon (rows 1337 → 1206 of 2532). On
flat ground that hides it. On any slope facing you the band is large on screen, and bare.

Evidence: `progress/261-e156-driftwood-foliage-bubble.jpg`, the same pose from the hut side toward the shrine, phone
390×844 @3×, HEAD 12e9d79. The four frames: as shipped · blend off · 10 m in · 20 m in. The probes are scratch-only (a
HEAD export with debug hooks). Their scripts are in the session scratchpad. **Correction (the build's grid probe):** the
bright knoll top in that image (110–130 m out) is the shrine's clearing, GroundCover's 9.5 m avoid disc: nothing grows
there at any distance, so it was the wrong example. The ground in front of it (10–110 m) does carry dense cover (≈ 77 %
of it from above), and that is the part drawn bare.

Pine Hollow is the same by design: `Grass.ts` 40 m (shrinks over the last 10 m), `Undergrowth.ts` 60 m (18 m band), cards past 60 m.
It also has one real shader loss: its grass, fern and needle cards are alpha-tested with mipmaps, so thin blades lose
coverage at far mips. The fwidth sharpening helps but does not preserve coverage.

## Fix directions (Jake picks, none built)

- **A. The ground wears the cover.** Bake each spot's cover mix into the terrain's colour: fern / tuft / bush density
  as darker, mottled greens, hibiscus as red flecks. The far ground then already reads as the plants' colour, and the 3D
  plants grow out of a matching base, not bare ground. This is the standard fix and costs almost no GPU.
- **B. A horizon tier.** A third, cheaper stand-in (clump impostors or 1–2-tri tufts, merged per tile) out to the
  island's edge, so hills keep a silhouette.
- **C. Reach by screen size, not metres.** Keep cover longer where it covers the most screen (slopes facing the camera,
  the zoomed bow AIM), and cut it sooner on flat ground where it is a sliver.
- A + C is the likely pair. B only if silhouettes on far hills still read bare after A.

## Built (2026-09-25): A + C, on by default

**A — the ground wears the cover** (`src/world/coverTint.ts`, new):
- `CoverGrid` is a chunk-wide 4 m grid. Per cell it holds the cover's mean colour and two optical depths: its ground
  footprint (`top`) and its upright cross-section (`side`). GroundCover fills it at boot from the same density rules its
  cells place by (≈ 10 ms on the M5). Each kind's colour and areas come from its own triangles.
- The Blender cove overwrites its own area from the cover it actually placed (its tiles' triangles, splatted).
- Both terrains take them per vertex (`aCover`, `aCoverSide`, `flat` like their facet colour, with the facet jitter).
- The terrain shader mixes each facet toward the cover colour by how much ground the plants hide from where you look:
  τ = top + side·cot θ. From above you still see the ground between the plants. At the grazing angle far ground is seen at,
  it reads as the plants.
- The plants fade into that same colour (`coverSeen`, shared GLSL), so the fade band has no edge. Before, they faded
  into the bare facet colour.

**C — longer reach on slopes** (GroundCover.ts):
- Plants on sloping ground keep their reach × up to 1.7 (8° → 23°); flat ground is unchanged. The factor comes from the
  ground's own tilt, not the angle it is seen at.
- A view-angle version was built and measured first. The refill has to include every plant the next 4–8 m could bring
  in, or it inserts plants part-grown. That was +68 % instances and +19 % triangles on flat ground, so it was dropped.
- C fades out as the camera climbs (5 → 14 m over the ground), so Explore's high views are unchanged.

**Also:**
- The near refill takes cells nearest first, so a kind at its cap drops its furthest plants, not a corner.
- Once the Blender island loads, GroundCover stops placing plants in its area. They were clipped in the vertex shader
  anyway, but each one took an instance and its vertices.
- The WebGPU port (`?gpu=webgpu`, opt-in) is not updated: the WebGL path is the only default.

## Jake's video (2026-09-25 12:34, iPhone, the old build): "pop in and pop out"

**Where:** running at the dune fringe by the tidal creek and the rope-fence path under the NE headland. That is procedural
ground, so GroundCover's cover, not the Blender cove's. The fringe of ferns, red hibiscus and bushes grew in at ~25–30 m
running up and dropped out backing off.

**Cause:** the near → far handover (26–38 m on the phone). The far models E117 added were hand-made and far thinner than
the plants they stand for. Side-on (how a plant 30 m away is seen), as a share of the near model's area: fern 15 %,
hibiscus 25 %, bush 28 % (and 0.55 m tall against 0.84), daisy 10 %, tuft 73 %. So every fringe plant grew 4–7× in view
inside a 5 m band as you crossed its near edge. A + C (above) don't touch this, and the video predates them anyway.

**Fix:**
- The fern, hibiscus and bush far models are now built from the near models' own leaves (`kites()`): each 4-triangle leaf
  becomes the 2 triangles of its outline, at the same place, yaw, pitch and length, with the hibiscus's flower chips kept.
- Colours ×0.82 stand in for the crease's shade that a flat kite loses.
- The far tuft's blades are 0.08 → 0.09 wide.

**Measured** (phone, the creek fringe from 35 m, frozen time; every plant forced to its near model, then to its far one):
- The 26–45 m band differs by 8.5 % between the two in the old build and 6.9 % in the new one.
- Mean luminance: near 127, far 132. Before this fix, a far model was a dark spiky star with the ground showing through.
- Image: `progress/265-e156-far-models-before-after.jpg`, rows OLD · FULL / OLD · FAR / NEW · FULL / NEW · FAR.

**Cost** (triangles, phone tier, against A + C): in the ferns 1.076 M → 1.165 M (+8.3 %), plateau +3.3 %, Explore 27 m
+7.2 %. Draws unchanged. Far models: fern 6 → 18 tris, hibiscus 7 → 21, bush 8 → 14.

## Jake's second video (2026-09-25 12:59, iPhone): "they still pop in"

**Where:** the spawn cove, ~70 m from the castaway at the hut, in the palm grove. That is the Blender island's area, where the
cover is the island's own placements, not GroundCover.

**Cause:** the cove's big cover (the bush and flowering-bush models, 1–1.5 m, and the hibiscus and ferns) was on the phone's
short cover reach. Each plant sank 3 m into the ground between 16 and 40 m out, and its tile switched off at 41 m. The
desktop draws the same cover to 100–148 m. The cove had no far models at all, so a bush rose out of the dirt as you ran at it.

**Fix (BlenderIsland.ts):**
- The big cover (`bush*`, `flowerbush*`, `hibiscus*`, `fern*`: on the phone 614 bushes, 244 flowering bushes, 542 hibiscus,
  1 779 ferns) gets its own tiles. Each plant stands to 50 m and sinks at its own edge in 60–80 m, over ground the cover grid
  already tints.
- The small cover (tufts, beach grass, flowers) keeps 16–40 m. The desktop is unchanged.

**Measured (phone):**
- Two cove poses, +1.7 % triangles (1.367 M → 1.390 M), +10 draws.
