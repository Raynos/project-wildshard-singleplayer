**Ask:** As I'm flying around the world in the World Explorer, I see lots of pop in. Detail popping in, like grass and coverage on the floor. I don't want any pop in. I just don't want any fucking pop in. Pop in makes it look shit.

**Found (2026-09-24, `scripts/popin-fly.mjs`: Explore World flown in a straight line, phone tier 390×844 @3× and desktop
1600×900, plus a frozen-time pop detector: the same camera just before / after one ground-cover refill):**
- **Ground cover (GroundCover.ts)** — the one the user saw. Tufts, ferns, hibiscus, daisies, bushes and pebbles stopped
  in a hard line ~25 m out (a 2D disc round the camera), grew in over a 6.5 m band, and — a bug — the refill window
  (27 m, every 4 m of travel) was narrower than the band plus a step, so every refill dropped a whole arc of plants in
  **already 40 % grown** right at the edge: the pop. From Explore's height the cover was a visible disc following you.
- **Blender cove cover tiles (BlenderIsland.ts)** — the same hard ring (32 m on the phone, 2D distance).
- **Palm LOD (BlenderIsland.ts)** — past 110 m whole 55 m tiles of palms swapped to the file's `_lo` palms (227 tris),
  which have another crown, a thinner trunk and another height: a tile of palms changed shape as you flew.
- **Animals** — hidden past 150 m (phone) with a hard blink (E125 made them draw in Explore at all, same evening).
- Checked, fine: the shadow cascade already fades out at its far end (`csm.fade`); pickups on Driftwood (the iron
  sword) have no draw distance; bushes / palms outside the cove / props are static meshes with no range.

**Fix:**
- GroundCover: every plant has **its own edge** somewhere in [near, far] (phone: tufts / ferns / hibiscus 14–34 m,
  bushes 16–38, daisies / pebbles 9–22, shells 10–26; desktop 1.25× that), drawn from its yaw, which the shader reads
  back from the instance matrix. It shrinks away over the last quarter before its edge, measured **camera to plant in
  3D** (so a high camera sees no disc). **Past `near` it turns into the ground it stands on**: the terrain's own colour
  (`lowPolyGroundColor`, per instance) and an up-facing normal, so by the time it shrinks it is the colour and shade of
  the grass round it and the thinning band has no edge. The refill copies every plant that can reach its edge before
  the next refill, so nothing is ever inserted part-grown. Paid for by dropping the stems' hidden end caps (bush
  112 → 70 tris, daisy 137 → 107, hibiscus 90 → 78).
- Blender cove cover: the same per-plant edges (a per-vertex `aEdge` per placement) in 3D; tiles switch off at far + 1.
- Far palms: the near palm simplified at load (meshoptimizer, ≤ 1 % of the palm's size, ~780 tris), so the swap at
  110 m can't be seen.
- Animals: shrink away over the last 15 % of the draw distance instead of blinking out.
- WebGPU port (gpu/materials.ts): the same per-plant 3D edge (not the ground blend).

**Switch (the one taste call):** `?coverblend=0` keeps the plants' own colours right up to their edge. With it on (the
default) the far cover reads as ground, not as red / white flecks: flowers past ~15 m lose their colour gradually. From
high up (Explore at 25 m+) the ground reads bare instead of a disc of plants under you.

**Cost (renderer.info, same poses):** phone 0.944 M tris → 0.943 M (cover 268 k → 269 k), draw calls unchanged; from
25 m up 0.90–0.95 M → 0.72–0.81 M (less cover drawn from the air); far palms +4 k tris at a view of mostly far tiles.
Desktop: cover 268 k → 388 k, frame 2.59 M → 2.77 M (+7 %). The headless frame time is vsync-bound (16.7 ms p50 both).
