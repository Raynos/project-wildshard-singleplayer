## Findings

- iPhone 17 Pro iOS 26.5 Simulator, actual Add to Home Screen PWA: a fresh Nine Dragon launch loaded once and reached first-person play. After E222, the normal Wildshard PWA opened on a renderer-free title; selecting Nine Dragon displayed one loading screen, reached first-person play, and Exit to Main returned to the title. A simulator pass does not establish the WebContent limit or behavior on Jake's physical iPhone.
- E216 (`2bb24342`) makes a shard switch a fresh document navigation and sets the `ShardHost` cap to one. It no longer keeps multiple WebGL worlds warm. E222 (`91084292`) avoids building Driftwood before selection and strips the selected `chunk` from the standalone URL before the costly world build, so a Props-stage page restart lands at the selector.
- Pine Hollow's E173 KTX2 commit `1def49ee` reduced the audited desktop texture allocation from about 1,205 MB to 619 MB. Nine Dragon has no equivalent KTX2 set yet; E217 instead reduced Nine Dragon phone texture allocation from 220.1 to 115.1 MiB and a Chromium post-GC heap proxy from 554.5 to 268.6 MiB. Those are not iOS WebContent RSS measurements.
- The download progress counts bytes read even when a response came from the service-worker cache. A 76% download bar does not by itself prove a cache miss. After play starts, `startShardPrefetch` can still download other shards' files into the service-worker disk cache; it does not create another WebGL world, but its network and decode costs remain a follow-up to audit on the physical phone.
