## Cause

1. **Chunks: the steps are deliberate (E89).** The shadow light's direction moves in 0.25° steps (`SHADOW_STEP`,
   src/world/DayNight.ts), not every frame. The video measures one jump every **1.93 s**; the frame-diff spikes land at
   0.65, 2.53, 4.43 … 18.05 s. Each jump moves the pennant's shadow ~2–3 cm, about 1.5–2 near-cascade texels, all at
   once. E89 sized the step for the old 1024² phone map with 18.5 cm texels, where a step was under a texel. E123's
   2c2k rig has **1.6 cm** texels (near cascade 33 m square, 2048²), so the same step is now a visible hop.
2. **Every frame is not the fix.** A continuous sun brings E89's flicker back. The fraction of frame pixels that
   change per frame rises from **0.079 % to 0.46 %** (close pose) and from 0.060 % to 0.22 % (wide pose). The heat map
   shows the flicker along every static edge: the bollard and the pole. The rotating texel grid re-rasterizes every
   edge on every frame.
3. **Soft, lumpy edge: resolution and kernel.** The near cascade's texel is 1.6 cm, and the 7×7 tent (E138) spreads
   every edge over about 11 cm. The flag's geometry is drawn at screen pixels, so its edge is crisp; its shadow comes
   from a coarser grid that is then blurred. The texel staircase survives as small bumps along the pole's shadow.
4. **Why it grates** (Abrams & Christ 2003): steady motion does not capture attention, but the **onset** of motion
   does. A shadow that jumps every 2 s in the middle of the view is a fresh onset every 2 s. Real sun shadows move
   continuously and far too slowly to see.
5. **The sun is not unusually fast.** Driftwood's day is 24 min, the same as BotW, Sea of Thieves and Genshin. GTA V
   and RDR2 run 48 min, Skyrim and Far Cry 5 run 72. The speed is ordinary for the genre. The visible steps are the
   problem.

## Industry practice (research agent; sources in the session)

- **Most engines step the light, as E89 does.** Unity HDRP redraws a cached light only after it turns past
  `cachedShadowAngleUpdateThreshold`. Unreal's virtual shadow maps invalidate on any sun rotation, and Epic's advice is
  to update the sun less often. Godot has an open "jumping shadows" bug with no fix.
- **Bethesda steps, then crossfades.** Skyrim and Fallout 4 fade the old shadow into the new over
  `fSunShadowUpdateTime` (1 s). The popular player fix stretches the fade to about 10 s.
- **Screen-space contact shadows** (Days Gone / Bend Studio, Apache-2.0) follow the exact sun every frame and add the
  fine detail that shadow maps lose on thin casters.

## Options (for Jake's pick)

- **A. Crossfade each step (Bethesda).**
  - Keep the step, and fade the old near-cascade shadow into the new one over ~1.5 s, perhaps with rarer 0.5–1° steps.
  - The far cascade needs no fade: its texels are 9 cm, so a 2 cm jump is below one texel.
  - Cost: a second near-cascade depth map, sampled only while a fade runs.
  - Fixes the chunks without bringing the flicker back.
- **B. Slow the day** to 48–72 min.
  - The steps come every 4–6 s instead of every 2 s. They get rarer but still pop.
  - Nearly free, and it can stack with A.
- **C. Crisper near shadows.**
  - Either a tighter near cascade (a third cascade, or a ~6–8 m split, for ~0.7 cm texels), or a 5×5 tent instead of
    7×7 near the player.
  - Costs a shadow pass or some softness. Measure it on the E142 GPU lane before shipping.
- **D. Screen-space contact shadows** for the near field.
  - Crisp, continuous detail on thin things like the flag and the pole.
  - The heaviest option: one fullscreen pass plus a depth buffer.

## Built (Jake's pick: A + B + C, "48 is good")

- **B, a 48-minute day** (src/world/DayNight.ts `CYCLE_S`): the 20 + 4 min day and night become 40 + 8. Driftwood only;
  Pine Hollow's PineDayNight keeps its 24 min. `?clock=` still overrides it.
- **A, each step crossfades** (src/world/shadowFade.ts, wired in Sky.ts):
  - Every cascade but the last has a ghost DirectionalLight (intensity 0) that holds its shadow at the direction before
    the step, on the same square, snapped to the texel grid as CSM snaps it.
  - The patched CSM light loop mixes ghost → cascade over 2 s (`uSunFade`, smoothstep). The ghosts' maps render only
    while a fade runs, and a settled frame skips them on a uniform branch.
  - DayNight holds its next step until the fade ends. A turn over 5° (the sun ↔ moon swap, a Time of day pick) still
    jumps at once.
  - `?sunfade=<s>` sets the fade length; `?sunfade=0` gives the old popping steps with no ghosts.
- **C, crisper near shadows** (Sky.ts `PHONE_SHADOW`): the phone rig goes from 2 cascades to **3, ending at 7 / 22 /
  80 m**. The texels are **0.8 / 2.5 / 9.2 cm**, where they were 1.6 / 9.2. `?psplit=14` gives E123's two cascades;
  `?psplit=5,18` is the sharper runner-up.
  - Board of the options: `art/shadows/round-2-flag-video/c-near-cascade-board.jpg`.
  - The 5×5 tent (`?pradius=1`) was barely different from the 7×7, so it was not picked.
