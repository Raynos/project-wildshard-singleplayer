## Why caches roll today (docs/design/cache-policy.md)

- `/assets/tex|models|hdri` and `/assets/baked` are not content-hashed; the SW's `ws-static-<assetsHash>` migrates old entries by **decoded size**, so a same-size change can stay stale and a changed file is re-fetched — fine per file.
- The phone boot reads **one content-hashed pack per shard / tier** (`/assets/packs/<slug>.<tier>-<hash>.bin`, Pine Hollow ~14 MB): ANY change to any file in it re-downloads the whole pack. That is the big invalidation.

## Budget (the user approved the recommendation, 2026-09-25)

- Cold time-to-play no worse than today (22 s Driftwood / 40 s Pine Hollow on 4G); first-play cold download at most 1.5x today (about 30 MB Driftwood, 55 MB Pine Hollow), everything else in the background after play.
- All three shards in the SW cache at most 300 MB; one-texture deploy re-downloads at most 2 MB.
- Warm time-to-play and shard switches must improve. Enforced by the scorecard (scripts/scorecard.mjs, scorecard.budget.json).
