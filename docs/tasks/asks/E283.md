## The ruler and the gate

- `scripts/nine-dragon-gpu.mjs` (`9eb232bf`, `0b5e8171`) uses pine-hollow-gpu.mjs's method. The frozen frame is timed
  on the M5 at Jake's 804×1624 phone buffer. It subtracts every fragment and scene group, the viewmodel and the post
  passes, and `--domes=all` adds the 72 dome cameras.
- **Gate: 1.5 ms per frame at every pose.** Jake's reading (gpu~ 18.7 ms p50 where the ruler reads ~3.15 ms at the
  spawn) puts the phone at ~6× the M5 when cool, and it throttles ~2× within minutes. So 1.5 ms is ~18 ms hot, which
  fits the 33 ms frame beside the phone's ~8 ms of CPU.

## Findings at 5cb1ecd

- Pass 0 (f9ca6490) against now, at mockup A / B / C / D: 2.56 / 1.64 / 1.58 / 1.66 against 3.15 / 1.82 / 1.97 /
  1.76 ms. The pass-0 baseline was already over the gate: ~15 ms cool and ~30 ms hot on the phone.
- 61 of 76 poses are over the gate. Worst: mockup A 3.2–4.2, C1·4 3.9, A1·5 3.6, A1·7 2.9, A2·5 2.7, A1·2 2.7,
  B1·6 2.6. Absolute numbers move ±25 % with other agents' GPU load, so compare A/B inside one run.
- The wet-ground streak cards are the E281 regression and the largest single cost: 1.34–1.44 ms at A (43 %), against
  0.80 at pass 0. There are 3,267 square cards (was 1,841) and 728 per stair plane × 5 (was 198). The cost is the
  tails toward the eye, which converge at the bottom of the screen: tail-near 0 saves 1.20 of 1.34 ms at A and 0.38 of
  0.39 at C.
- At A: all transparent 1.41 ms, all opaque 1.03 ms. The opaque cost is per-pixel shading of the Jiehua architecture
  program: swapping it for a flat material saves 1.00. Viewmodel 0.17–0.41 ms, post + sky ~0.6.
