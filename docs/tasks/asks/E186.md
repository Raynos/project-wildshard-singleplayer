## First findings (2026-09-25, in flight — scripts/stutter-run.mjs, phone tier 390×844 @3, the player sprinting pier → cove → interior, CPU 4×)

- **The near ground-cover refill uploads its whole cap every 4 m: 2.40 MB in 42 `bufferSubData` calls, every ~0.56 s at a
  sprint, even in the Blender cove where 0 of the 20.7 k near instances are live** (the cove is excluded from the cover,
  E156). With only the live count uploaded that frame is 0.01 MB in the cove and ~1 MB in the interior (8.8 k live).
- **The far swap (every 8 m) uploads 2–4 MB at once** (Normal range); with Foliage range 500 m the cover uploaded
  7.9 MB/s in total. Jake's 1.9–2.4 M triangles point at the 500 m range still saved on his phone (being deleted: E156).
- On this Mac neither shows as a spike (Chromium and Playwright WebKit both eat a 4 MB upload in < 1 ms; the upload frames
  and the 2 after them are +0.3–0.7 ms), so the proof on the phone is Jake's meter. What the Mac does show: the byte
  pattern is exactly periodic with travel (4 m / 8 m), the spike count Jake reads (13–21 / 30 s) sits between the near
  refills (~53 / 30 s sprinting) and the far swaps (~27 / 30 s).
- **The game allocates 16–27 MB/s of JS garbage** while running (V8 heap sampling): a GC every ~1–2 s. V8 on the Mac
  collects it without a hitch; JavaScriptCore on the iPhone runs its collections from the run loop, outside the game's
  update / render — which is where Jake's meter books a frame's time as `gpu~`. Being traced to its allocation sites.
- Not it: shader links (0 during the run), texture uploads (0.3–0.6 MB/s, steady), the sun shadow fade (+~2 ms GPU on
  the ~35 % of frames it runs, no spikes), long tasks (none outside the game loop).
