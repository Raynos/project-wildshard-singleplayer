## What was wrong

BAG ▸ MAP was black on every shard. Since E357 F8 (f92722eb6, 2026-10-01) the menu pauses the app (`Menu.open`) and a
paused app runs no update phase (`Game.runPhase`). The full map only drew when the play loop's update phase called
`FullMap.update`, so with the Bag open it never drew.

## Fix

`src/engine/ui/Map.ts`: `update()` records the pose; `show()` runs a frame loop on the map's open scope that draws from
it, and `hide()` disposes it. Regression test: `test/engine/full-map-paused.test.ts` (fails on the old Map.ts).
After-screens on Driftwood, Pine Hollow, Nalati, Nine Dragon (its room map), Far Reach and Sunscar:
`progress/e440-map/` (`before-driftwood-isle.jpg` is the black frame at 835e06d9b).
