## Phase 2 trigger design (not built yet)

- **When:** at the end of the reward view's 7 s hold (Finale.ts `reward > HOLD_S`). The game still owns the camera
  there (`player.carried`), the captain is dead and nothing else is aggro'd near the shrine, so the card never lands
  mid-fight. The finale keeps `carried` on and hands over to the card instead of releasing control. `seen:reward` is
  set, so the quest completes and the achievement toast plays under the card.
- **Pause:** while the card is up, the world freezes and the audio ducks like the pause menu (a `frameGate` term /
  the menu's hush). The music stays on the `chunk` sting / theme, and touch controls and the HUD are hidden.
- **Keep exploring:** the card closes, `carried` is released, the viewmodel and HUD come back, the clock runs on
  from golden hour. The quest chip then reads "Still to find · …" (the nearest unfound sea glass / place / treasure,
  with a distance and arrow), so the endless free play has a direction.
- **Skipped or never reached:** if `quest:driftwood-done` is set but `seen:complete` is not (for example an old
  save that finished before this shipped, or a reload during the hold), a toast offers "Driftwood complete · open
  the menu to see your island". It only fires out of combat, when no enemy is aggro'd within ~25 m.
- **Reopen:** a "Driftwood complete" row at the top of the menu's ACHIEVEMENTS tab reopens the same card at any time.
- **Save:** `seen:complete` is set in the shard's Flags (`ws.flags.v1`, per shard) the first time the card shows, so
  it auto-shows once only. Time played needs a new per-shard counter (Progress `ws.progress.v1` `playS`, ticked while
  `hud.entered`). It does not exist yet.
- **Next shard:** reload with `?chunk=nalati-grasslands`, as ENTER WORLD does for another shard. **Title screen:**
  `hud.exitToMenu()`, like the pause menu's "Exit to main menu".
