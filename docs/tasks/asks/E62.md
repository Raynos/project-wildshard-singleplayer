# E62

**Status:** open (2026-09-23)
**Ask:** iPhone check of E44 on a real device: audio decoded in the loading bar (before the first tap) plays after the first tap in Safari; an offline reload of the same shard plays with music + SFX

From E44 (preload + offline, archived `project/archive/2026-09-23-preload-offline.md`), verified headless only.

- The audio is decoded in an OfflineAudioContext during the loading bar and plays in the live context created on the first tap. That is fine per the W3C spec and WebKit source, but it has not been checked on an iPhone.
- On the phone: first launch, then tap and hear the music and SFX. Then turn on airplane mode, reload, and check it reaches playable with sound.
- If it fails, the fallback is to decode on the first tap. That work lives in src/audio/Music.ts, Stems.ts, Audio.ts and src/boot.
