  (the middle half of the width), in sight and with a landing. Then the "◇ DRAGON HOOK" marker shows, LOCK locks it
  ("◆ LOCKED · JUMP"), and JUMP zips. At the stair foot facing up the stair, one lock and one jump took the player
  from (18, 125, 6) to (26, 127.5, 5.1).
- At the spawn (mockup A) and at the Well rim (mockup B, where the mockup shows the claw firing across the Well),
  there is no hook in reach. LOCK there arms a miss shot, and JUMP fires and misses. Nothing on the LOCK disc says it
  is the grapple.
