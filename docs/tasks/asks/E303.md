No shard has a mouse-wheel swap. So two of the four shards have a real multi-weapon kit (Nalati, Pine Hollow) and use the
strip. Driftwood gets a second weapon (the iron sword) and uses a different control, the SWAP pill. Nine Dragon has one
weapon in the open world.

**The collision:** the strip's tabs started at a fixed `ws-top + 240 px` and ran down the left edge into HOVER, which
sits at 46 % (E152). On Nalati at 390×844, slot 3 sat 19 px over HOVER. At 375×667, slots 2–3 covered HOVER on Nalati
and on a full-kit Pine Hollow. The strip draws on top, so HOVER could not be pressed there. The same class of bug was on
Driftwood / Nine Dragon: the SWAP pill ran 6–15 px into the LOCK disc.

**Fixed (bug fix, shipped):**
- `89080b74`: HOVER keeps its one spot on every shard. The strip is now anchored at the bottom, 18 px above HOVER (14 px
  with 34 px tabs on screens ≤ 740 px tall), and grows up toward the status column.
- `5c15bfd1`: the SWAP pill now ends 6 px left of the MOVE | ATTACK divider, clear of LOCK.
- Measured on all four shards at 390×844 and 375×667 with the whole kit unlocked: 0 overlaps. That covers slots vs
  HOVER, slots vs the status column, slots vs the USE band, the pill vs every disc, and Nalati's 4-slot practice-room
  case.
- Captures: `progress/e303-weapon-swap/collision-fix.jpg` (before / after), `collision-fix-all-shards.jpg`,
  `swap-pill-fix.jpg`.

## One swap mechanism for every shard — board

`progress/e303-weapon-swap/board.jpg` (frames in `art/hud/round-14-weapon-swap/`). These are live iPhone-portrait
captures. Each variant is drawn in the page with the game's own HUD classes, so the text and icons are exact. The rows
are Nalati, Pine Hollow (full kit) and Driftwood (iron sword found).

- **A · Left rail**: today's Nalati / Pine Hollow strip on every shard (Driftwood and Nine Dragon drop the SWAP pill).
  Numbered tabs sit just above HOVER. Tap a tab = hold that weapon; tap the held one = back to the last.
- **B · Swap chip + wheel**: one chip on the bar edge shows the held weapon and dots. Tap = next weapon; hold = a wheel
  of every weapon, slide and let go. The left edge keeps only HOVER.
- **C · Right rail**: the same tabs on the right edge, above DODGE / JUMP, by the fire thumb. It shares that edge with
  Nalati's HORSE tab.
- **D · Slots in the bar**: a slim column on the MOVE | FIRE divider inside the bar. It costs no screen space, but the
  tabs are 34 px and it fits only 3 weapons.
- Dropped before the board: "swipe the FIRE disc to swap". A drag from ATTACK already turns the camera (E42), so a
  swipe gesture would break it.

**Recommendation: A.**
- It is one tap to any weapon; B needs a hold for anything but "next".
- It is already built and played on two shards, and after the fix it cannot touch HOVER.
- Its slot numbers are the desktop keys.
- Adopting A means five changes:
  - the strip on every shard (`hud.weaponStrip` becomes the base HUD's, and the SWAP pill goes);
  - on desktop, the hotbar on every shard and `Q` = last weapon everywhere (Nalati's rule);
  - the mouse wheel = next / previous;
  - keys `1`…`N` for as many slots as are owned (the practice room's 4th);
  - Nine Dragon shows its single jian slot like Pine Hollow's start.
- Not built until Jake picks. No Debug row was added: Jake doesn't flip A/B switches on the phone, and A is small to
  build once picked.
