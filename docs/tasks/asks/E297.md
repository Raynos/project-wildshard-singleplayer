## What changed (39e10fa3)

Driftwood only: `ChunkDef.fightRules: { maxAttackers: 2 }`. Nalati and Pine Hollow keep their fights. The rules live in
`src/entities/fightRules.ts` (pure, `test/fight-rules.test.ts`) and `AnimalManager` (the FIGHT RULES header).

1. **Boars fight instead of fleeing.** A boar gets a stalk, like the bears: it comes for you when it notices you and
   doesn't bolt. After every charge (landed, missed, broken by a blow) a boar or bear backs off past its ring (boar 6.5 m,
   bear 7.5 m, ≤ 2.2 s at 4.2 m/s), arcing to one side. It then circles you on the ring at 1.7 m/s, facing you. When its
   cooldown is over (3.4 s after a hit, 2.0 s after a miss) and a token is free, it squares up, winds up and charges
   again. A charge you sidestep ends once it has run past you. Only a nearly dead one (< 25 % health, not Old Ironhide)
   may break off, with 50 % per hit. Sheet A: before, 1 charge, then 40 m away; after, 3 charges in 13 s, never
   further than 9 m.
2. **At most 2 attack at once.** An attack needs one of 2 tokens: a charge's wind-up and run, a crab snap, a monkey
   bite or coconut throw, a cutlass swing. A token goes back when the attack ends. The others hold back: boars and bears
   circle on the ring, crabs at 3.6 m, monkeys at 3.4 m, the sailor at 3.0 m, the captain at 3.4 m. A crab whose turn
   it is now steps in to snap (before, crabs only snapped when you walked into them). The captain's burst from under
   the water needs no token. Sheet B, five crabs: before, 5 wind up at once; after, never more than 2.
3. **A warning before an off-screen hit.** Every attack's wind-up (`onWindup`) starts an amber chevron on a navy glass
   disc (`src/ui/WindupWarn.ts`, `combat.css` `ws-combat-warn`). It sits at the screen edge toward an attacker that is
   out of view or behind you, and stays until the attack lands, misses or is broken. Behind you = the bottom edge; on
   touch the marks keep above the controls. 4 marks, pooled. The wind-up sound cue still plays: the chevron is chained
   after it, 1 cue per wind-up in the probe. Sheet C.
4. **No bear swallows the camera.** Each frame, a boar, bear or deer body (rump to head) that gets closer than its
   radius + 0.68 m to the player is moved back out through its physics motor. Sheet D, a brown bear charge: the closest
   the camera got to its body was −0.05 m before (inside it, a brown screen) and +0.58 m after.
5. **The sailor goes round a beam.** When it is in reach but a beam or the mast blocks its line of sight (E296), it
   side-steps at 1 m/s and tries the other side every 1.6 s. It no longer waits there.
