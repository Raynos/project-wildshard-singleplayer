# E287

**Status:** done (2026-09-29) — `fca9e1a4`, live as build `fca9e1a-mumq2sgo`; waits on Jake finishing the taming step on the phone
**Ask:** You are the nalati agent I can't finish the quest line I can't crouch in the grass there's no crouch button

## Cause

The CROUCH disc showed only while `grassHeightAt(player) ≥ 0.7 m`, and that height includes trampling. The player's own
steps flatten the grass under them to 15 % (GrassTrample, recovering over 20 s). So after any walking the grass at your
feet read ~0.15 m, and the disc could not appear until you had stood still for ~13 s in 1.1 m feather grass. On the
0.66–0.72 m meadow it never appeared. TULPAR's first step ("Crouch, come slow…") builds TRUST only while crouched, so the
quest could not go on.

## Fix

- `src/nalati/stealth.ts`: the crouch availability and the GRASS cover read the grass as it stands round you
  (`grassBaseHeightAt`), not your own footprint. New `crouchHere` option: the disc also shows while you approach a
  stallion you can tame (Taming's 40 m approach, `ride.taming.view.trust !== null`), even on the meadow.
- `src/entities/wildEnv.ts`: `grassStandingAt` feeds the player's own-spot cover and the rustle in `playerVisibility` /
  `hearingRadius` (the same footprint bug made you always visible to the herds and the wolves).
- Checked headless (phone, `ride=herd`): after a 1.5 s walk the trampled height is 0.15 m, the disc shows, and a tap
  crouches (`progress/e287-crouch-by-stallion.jpg`).
