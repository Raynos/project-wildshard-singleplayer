# E89

**Status:** in flight (2026-09-23, pop-in / graphics agent)
**Ask:** Shadows flicker all over the place (two iPhone screen recordings, frame by frame) — fix the shadows

**Cause:** the day / night clock turned the CSM light a hair every frame (~0.15°/s). A shadow map that rotates each
frame re-rasterizes every shadow edge each frame, so on the phone's 1024² map the pier posts', fence posts' and palms'
shadows crawled and flickered even with the camera still. The recordings (60 fps capture of a 30 fps game) show the
sand / deck shadows changing on every rendered frame while the sky stays put.

**Fix:** `src/world/DayNight.ts` — the shadow light's direction moves in 0.25° steps (`SHADOW_STEP`), about one every
1.5 s, instead of every frame; boot and a Time of day pick snap it exactly; the sun ↔ moon swap is one step.

**Evidence (headless, phone tier, Blender cove beach pose, camera still, 90 hand-stepped 1/30 s frames):**
- before (live c5740d5): 90 / 90 frames flicker (0.415 % of pixels change > 30 per frame); clock frozen: 1 / 90
- after: 2 / 90 frames change (the two 0.25° steps), 0.023 % per frame on average
