**Ask:** Driftwood top-10 row 4: death and respawn — 1.2 s fade, a 'Killed by …' card in the HUD style, respawn at the last discovered place instead of the pier

## What changed (`d44bcf52`)

- **The fade** (`src/ui/DeathFade.ts`, styles in `src/ui/styles/combat.css` `ws-combat-death-*`): 0.5 s to dark, dark until
  1.2 s (the respawn happens at 0.5 s, under the dark), 0.6 s back in. Driven by the game's dt. While it runs the
  player is frozen (`player.carried`, weapons off) and no hit lands (`animals.onCharge` is gated; health is held at 100).
- **The card**: the HUD glass (navy `rgba(13,27,38,.82)`, 1 px cyan hairline, corner brackets). The headline is the
  killer in the old toast's words (`deathCause`, e.g. "MAULED BY A BROWN BEAR", "FELL TOO FAR"). Under it, letter-spaced
  cyan mono: "RESPAWNING AT WRECK COVE", or "WASHED BACK TO THE PIER" when the pier is the last place. It replaces the
  old death toast. It is shared by every shard. A boss-fight death (Nalati's King / Titan, Pine Hollow's Antler King)
  still goes to its own checkpoint, with no fade.
- **Where you respawn** (`src/game/LastPlace.ts`): the **last named place you walked into** (Driftwood's
  `DRIFTWOOD_PLACES`, the ones the map's discovery uses). Walking into a place is what discovers it, so a new
  discovery always becomes the checkpoint. Going back to an older place makes that one the checkpoint again.
  - The point is your own footing: the first grounded step inside the place after you entered it. The physics
    `floorBelow` must be within 0.3 m of your feet. It can't be taken while swimming, wading, hovering, riding the
    zipline or on a horse, and it must be at least 0.3 m above the sea.
  - You face the place's centre. With no place reached (or after Exit to main menu), you go back to the spawn as before.
  - Nalati, Pine Hollow and Nine Dragon keep their own spawn respawns.
- `respawnWhere(chunk, place?)` takes the place name. `Places.points` exposes the resolved places (quest/core.ts).
- Tests: `test/last-place.test.ts` (the respawn-point choice + the card's words).
