**Ask:** E46 follow-up from Jake's iPhone (13:19, home-screen app): the bottom HUD has a black bar cutting off the control bar; the layout must be 45% MOVE | 55% right section holding BOTH attack and look pad, attack button touching the 45/55 divider from the 55 side (not centred on it, not three equally spaced buttons)


Root cause (bisected in the iOS 26.5 simulator's home-screen app, a probe printing innerHeight / screen.height): drawn under
the status bar (`viewport-fit=cover` + black-translucent), WebKit loads the page at 874 px then shrinks the viewport to
812 px ~1 s later and keeps it short, even across reloads; fixed content is clipped to 812, the root background shows below
(the black strip). Triggers found: `overflow: hidden` on html / body, and the boot loader's relayout. E46's
`screen.height` override (src/core/viewport.ts) could not paint past the clip. Jake picked "below the status bar": no
`viewport-fit=cover` → the page starts under the clock and runs to the bottom edge; a dark band sits behind the clock.
This drops E48 (raising PAUSE / the minimap into the status band can't happen).

Fix: index.html viewport meta without viewport-fit=cover; base.css html / body `overflow: clip`; viewport.ts back to
innerHeight + `--ws-home` (34 px home-indicator margin in a standalone Face ID phone, where iOS now reports 0);
touch.css `--safe` uses it. Layout: MOVE = left 45 % (stick centred), ATTACK's left edge on the divider, LOOK pad centred
in the rest of the 55 % (equal gaps). Verified in the simulator's home-screen app: title + in-game run to the bottom edge,
also after background → resume (/tmp/e58diag sheets; proof progress/208-e58-pwa-strip-before-after.jpg).
