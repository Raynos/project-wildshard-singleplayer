# E138

**Status:** done (2026-09-25) — commit bd48540b, live build `bd48540-mugwpw3t`. Phone shadows on Driftwood use the 7×7 tent filter; A/B on the device with `?pshadowfilter=tent` (runner-up) · `?pshadowfilter=cheap` (before)
**Ask:** (in play, phone, the pier pennant's shadow on the deck) The shadows are super jagged for this flag. How do you make the shadows of the flag smooth?
Then, looking up at the flag: "To me, the flag itself looks like 10 times smoother." Direction change: "Can you do the
shadows properly and not use like a cheap phone filter?"

## Cause

- The phone's near cascade has 1.7 cm texels, but three r186's PCF is only 5 hardware-compared taps on a Vogel disc,
  rotated per pixel by interleaved gradient noise. That gives a narrow, dithered penumbra.
- The toon ramp (`smoothstep(0.25, 0.75, shadow)`, stylize.ts) sharpens that penumbra back into a hard line. The line
  follows the shadow map's texel staircase, stretched along the deck by the sun's angle.
- The warm terminator band sits on the same line, so it traced every step as an orange saw.
- Reproduced at phone tier at the user's spot: pier sea end, looking down at the pennant's shadow near the pole.

## Filters measured (phone tier, 390×844 @3×, the 2c2k rig)

GPU frame time comes from `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` around `composer.render`, as the median of 80 frames. The
table shows two runs on the Pine Hollow HEAD (18b3d6be+) on the M5 Max. `deck` is the pennant shadow filling the screen,
`wide` is the pier sea end, and `hut` is the hut deck under the palms.

| filter (`?pshadowfilter=`) | deck ms | wide ms | hut ms | look |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cheap`: three's 5-tap Vogel + IGN (before) | 5.92–5.98 | 5.45–5.50 | 5.87–6.07 | stair-stepped edge, dither, orange saw |
| **`soft`: 7×7 tent near / 5×5 far (default)** | **6.17–6.26** | 5.44–5.62 | 6.21–6.25 | smooth curved edge, no noise, the warm rim follows the curve |
| `tent`: 5×5 near / 3×3 far | 6.06 | 5.52 | 6.14 | smooth, a faint residual wave |
| `poisson`: 16 Poisson taps + IGN rotation | 6.16 | 5.57 | 6.27 | smooth outline but grainy, wider warm rim |
| `vsm`: three's variance shadow map | 9.11 | 9.55 | 8.33 | smooth, but +2.3–4 ms (blur passes on two 2048² maps) and light-bleed blots |

- **`soft`** is Castaño's optimised PCF (The Witness; MJP's shadow sample; the tent behind Unity's high-quality soft
  shadows). Hardware 2×2 compares are placed and weighted into an exact 7×7-texel tent: 16 taps for the near cascade,
  9 taps (5×5) for the far one.
  - It adds a clamped per-tap receiver-plane depth bias (Isidoro 2006), so the wide kernel does not reach under a sloped
    deck.
  - No acne at morning, golden hour or a 10° dawn sun on the pier, the boat deck, the hut deck or the sand.
  - Cost against `cheap`: about +0.2–0.3 ms (+4–5 %) of frame on the M5 Max. The iPhone GPU is several times slower, so
    expect roughly +1–2 ms a frame there; that is an estimate, not measured on the device.
- **PCFSoftShadowMap** is not an option: three r186 removed it (it warns and falls back to PCF).
- **Contact-hardening (PCSS)** needs raw depth for its blocker search. three binds the shadow map only as a compare
  sampler, so PCSS would take a second depth copy of both 2048² cascades per frame. Not done; say if it is wanted.
- The toon edge band (`uToonEdge` / `?pedge`) needs no change: on the smooth ramp it follows the curve, not the texel
  steps.
- The filter is patched into three's shader chunk at boot, before any material compiles, so nothing recompiles mid-play.
  It applies to Driftwood's phone rig only. Pine Hollow and Nalati keep their own settings, as the remaster set them.
  Desktop Driftwood still uses three's 5-tap filter; `?pshadowfilter=soft` tries the new one there.

## Pennant seam

The jagged vertical seam across the cloth was the edge of the dark sleeve (column 0). The swallowtail notch scaled
**every** column toward the pole at the centre line (`u × (1 − notch·…)`), so each column line, the sleeve's edge
included, was bent into a chevron. It was neither a lighting nor a normal seam, and the E111 flutter shader was not
involved. Fix (src/world/Pier.ts): the notch now pulls back only the fly half (`notchFrom: 0.4`). The fly end's
swallowtail is unchanged, and the sleeve edge and the diamond sit on straight lines.
