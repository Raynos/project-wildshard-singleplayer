**Ask:** Same with this blue flag (the pier pennant), that's the same cloth pattern. Also the blue flag is incredibly simple. We can do one or two steps better than this.

**Evidence:** commit `28b8d03`, live build `28b8d03-mugflzwh`. Before/after: `progress/225-e111-pier-pennant-before-after.jpg`.

- **The streaks:** the old flag was a front and a back triangle, coplanar, inside the pier's one mesh, which casts and
  receives shadows. It shadowed itself: the same acne as the boat's sail (E110). The cloth is now its own mesh
  (`Pier.pennant`) of single-sided triangles on the kit's DoubleSide material. It casts a shadow (through a matching
  sway depth material) and never receives one.
- **One step better** (src/world/Pier.ts `buildPennant`):
  - a 1.75 m swallowtail in the Lookout banner's blues, with a dark sleeve at the hoist and the white Wildshard
    diamond on both faces;
  - a 7×4 grid (about 72 tris), so it waves: it flies downwind and ripples from hoist to fly in the shared wind
    (wind.ts sway + a lateral flutter);
  - a taller pole with a brass finial and two rope ties.
- **Explore:** the cloth is a child of `pier.group`, which main.ts registers, so the registry needs nothing new.
