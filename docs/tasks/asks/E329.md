**Cause:** the SPEED chip acts on `click` (so do MAP, ORBIT, the tabs and ✕). The browser synthesises a click only for a
one-finger tap. iOS WebKit's tap recogniser and Chrome's gesture detector both drop the tap once a second touch is on the
glass. A CDP probe confirmed that Chrome sends no `click` for a button tapped while another finger is held. So with a thumb
on FLY, the chip's tap never fired. Three smaller faults:
- ▲ / ▼ shared one `cam.move.y`, so releasing one zeroed the other;
- they let go on `pointerleave`;
- a second finger on the stick took it over, and its lift ended the flight.

**Fix:**
- `src/explore/TouchFly.ts` `MultiTouchTaps`: every touch is tracked by pointerId. A button tap that shared the glass
  with another finger gets its click dispatched on the lift, and the browser's own click for that tap is swallowed if one
  comes. A one-finger tap is still the browser's.
- The stick keeps one thumb and captures it. Each world finger is captured on the canvas. A cancelled touch never counts
  as a tap-to-select.
- `holdButton`: ▲ / ▼ each keep their own pointer ids and capture. Only that finger's lift or cancel releases them.
- `src/explore/Explore.ts` rail wiring: holding both ▲ and ▼ keeps the camera level.

**Test:** `scripts/explore-multitouch.mjs`. It serves an iPhone 402×874 @3 page with CDP multi-touch and runs four
scenarios: speed, rail, look and all. Run it with `scripts/browser-lane.sh node scripts/explore-multitouch.mjs
--url=<served build> --chunk=<slug>`.
- **Baseline (no fix):** Driftwood 9/12, Nine Dragon 11/15. The chip got 0 clicks, so SPEED stayed Normal.
- **With the fix:** the relay dispatched the click (1 untrusted click).
- **Harness bug in both runs:** the first runs lifted one finger with a `touchMove` that left it out. CDP does not treat
  that as a lift. The lift only came at the final `touchEnd`, so the "▲ released" and "faster after the tap" checks failed
  on both builds. The harness now lifts with `touchEnd [id]`, which the probe confirmed works.
