**Ask:** Loading Driftwood for the first time: huge lag, huge frame drops. Turning around, a one-second frame drop. Something regressed horribly performance-wise. Try it, take videos, whatever it takes: find the regression and fix it.

## What regressed

1. **The freeze (the "huge lag" on a first load): E147's sun-fade ghosts (9b86179c).**
   - three allocates a light's shadow map only when that light first renders. The fade's ghost lights render only
     during a fade.
   - Until the first sun step (~2.3 s into play), every lit program's ghost `sampler2DShadow` was bound to three's
     RGBA empty texture. Every lit draw failed with `GL_INVALID_OPERATION: Mismatch between texture format and sampler
     type` and was dropped: for those seconds the pier, the island, the boat and the sword are not drawn at all
     (progress/259: the left half is the live build 2 s in).
   - So the boot's first frame built no lit pipeline. The first step's frame then built every one at once:
     **1563 / 1642 / 1800 / 1864 ms** frames in 4 cold runs (the baseline's worst frame: 44–57 ms).
   - It only shows with a cold shader cache: the first load after a deploy, the user's "first time". A second load
     reuses the Metal cache and hides it.
2. **Turn hitches.** The precompile links every program, but Metal builds a pipeline (program × target × vertex layout)
   at its first draw. The boot drew one frame facing the spawn, so the first turn built the rest in play. The
   waterfall's rings (E150) and the casters' depth draws in the new 3-cascade rig are new since the baseline. First
   turn: 35 ms worst on the M5 (the baseline: 19–23).
3. **Steady cost: the shadow pass grew 4.7×.** The rig went from one 1024² map to 3 cascades plus 2 ghosts at 2048²
   (E123 813cab2, E147 9b86179c), and a fade runs on **~74 % of frames**. The terrain and the merged vegetation and
   rock meshes span the island (spheres 160–280 m), so each map drew them whole. The 7 m near cascade alone drew 236 k
   triangles. The ghosts were not culled per cascade at all: 330 k each.

## Fixes (the user's picks all kept: 2c2k-style 2048 cascades, the tent filter, the fade, rocks B, bush A, far cover)

- **5ed85996**
  - shadowFade.ts: each ghost map is drawn at the first shadow pass, and `warm()` places the ghosts for the boot. The
    GL errors are gone.
  - Game.firstFrame draws the world 4 more times, a quarter-turn apart, through a 100° square view with the shadows
    placed for each view (`Game.warmTurn`, `Sky.warmShadows`).
  - cascadeCull.ts `cullToSlice`: a ghost culls its casters to its cascade's slice, like the cascades do.
- **0076cdb3** shadowChunks.ts: every static mesh over 40 m and 2 k triangles casts through shadow-only pieces, cut on
  an up-to-8×8 grid.
  - Each piece is a drawRange into one shared index. The vertex buffers are the mesh's own, so nothing is copied.
  - Per-cascade culling now works on them. Driftwood has 8 casters in 244 pieces, Pine Hollow 2 in 28, Nalati none.
  - The shadows are the same. Frozen-time A/B at 5 island poses differs only where animation differs (gulls, the
    boat, sway).
  - `?shadowpieces=0` switches it off.

## Open: Jake's call (A / B / A+B built as switches, benchmarked and on video)

How it was measured:

- **Where the cost goes.** One page, interleaved blocks of 40 frames per arm, 4 clean runs. Drawing the two ghost maps
  costs +0.48–0.75 ms. Sampling them costs +0.00–0.10 with the 7×7 tent and +0.00–0.14 with the 3×3. So B saves about
  0.04 ms: the taps are nearly free on this GPU, and the ghost maps' draw passes are the cost. A halves the time a fade
  runs, so it halves the cost. "Added GPU / frame" = the fading-frame cost × the fade share.
- **Turn + walk.** A 360° turn in 3 s, then 3 s of walking, every frame timed; each figure is the median of 3 rounds.
  Parked medians: C 2.21 (2.16–2.32), A 2.04, B 2.28, A+B 2.05, off 1.84.
- **Noise.** An image-generation job shared the GPU. Rounds spread ±0.1–0.3 ms, and rounds with an IQR over 1 ms were
  dropped. Wall frame time on the M5 is vsync-bound (16.7 ms) for all five.
- **Phone estimate.** ×7–9, E142's ratio of the A18's GPU to the M5's. It is an estimate, not a reading from the
  device.

What the video shows:

  the onset frame B changes ~30 % more pixels than C.
