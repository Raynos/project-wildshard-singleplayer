## Mockups (2026-09-29, claude session d2730b57): three boards, all Nalati, iPhone portrait

These are live 390×844 @3× captures of a served HEAD build (`?touch&tier=phone&skipintro&nolock&mute=1&chunk=nalati-grasslands`),
covering on foot with the bow, in the saddle with the sabre, and locked onto Kokbori's pack. Each variant is drawn into the page
with the game's own HUD tokens, so every label is real text and nothing came from an image model. Frames are in
`art/hud/round-15-nalati-clean/`. No game code changed.

Rules in every variant:
- HORSE sits above HOVER and appears only once a horse is tamed. In the saddle it reads DISMOUNT, in amber.
- SWAP sits under HOVER and appears only with 2 or more weapons.
- With neither, HOVER stands alone.
- HOVER is off in the saddle. Its spot stays empty, so nothing else moves.
- The column is centred at 46 %, and SWAP ends 220+ px above MOVE.
- The right edge is combat only.

**1 · Placement: `progress/e319-hud/placement-board.jpg`.** Rows: on foot, in the saddle, and a ×2 zoom of the column.
- **A · Three tabs**: three separate tabs in HOVER's own shape (38 × 62): HORSE, HOVER, and SWAP with a swap-arrows icon.
- **B · One rail**: one glass rail with three cells. The SWAP cell shows the held weapon with a swap badge, its ammo and one dot per
  weapon. HOVER greys out in the saddle.
- **C · Compact stack**: round discs like the right thumb's. SWAP is the big one: the held weapon inside a ring of swap arrows,
  with an ammo badge and the kit dots.
- **D · SWAP carries the ammo** (wildcard): a wide SWAP tab holds the held weapon, "24 / 24" and the kit dots. The ammo row
  under VITALS goes, so there is one box fewer at top-left.
- **Recommendation: A.** It is what Jake described (a swap icon under HOVER). It keeps HOVER's shape, which he already approved (E152). Three
  separate tabs with 10 px gaps are three distinct targets, which matters because accidental presses were the complaint. The held
  weapon is already in the ammo row and the view model.

**2 · Swap interaction: `progress/e319-hud/wheel-board.jpg`.** Each strip is closed → open → picked, and the game really swapped in the
picked frame. The SWAP tab shows the held weapon and its ammo.
- **A · Half-wheel**: tap = next weapon. Hold = a half-wheel that fans right from the edge, showing every weapon and its ammo. Slide
  onto one and let go.
- **B · Flyout cards**: tap = a column of cards beside the tab (key, icon, name, ammo, HELD). Tap a card to pick it. Hold = the last
  weapon.
- **C · Flick pie**: hold = a pie at the thumb. The flick's direction picks: up-right is the bow, right the sabre, down-right the
  spear. Tap = next.
- **D · Swipe cycle**: swipe down = next, up = previous. The tab grows into a strip showing the previous and next weapons. Tap = the
  last weapon.
- With the bow: the right thumb keeps FIRE held, and any swap drops the draw (the 0.5 s holster).
- Riding: GALLOP is on the right thumb, so the horse keeps its gait while the left thumb swaps. D's vertical swipe can misfire as
  a push on the stick.
- **Recommendation: A.** It is Jake's E303 pick B (tap = next, hold = wheel) with only the anchor moved from the bar chip to the left
  edge. With 3 weapons, a tap reaches any of them in 2 at most.

**3 · The cleanest Nalati HUD: `progress/e319-hud/clean-board.jpg`.** Rows: exploring, in the saddle, and a fight locked on.
- **A · Tidy**: today's HUD plus the new column (placement D). The ammo row and the right-edge HORSE go.
- **B · Only when needed**: VITALS hide at full health outside a fight. DODGE and LOCK show only with a threat near. PAUSE becomes an
  icon. The bar's MOVE / LOOK words and the LOOK box go, though SWITCH still shows while locked on.

- **D · Bare**: C plus B's rules. The minimap becomes a compass ribbon with the quest pin and distance (tap = the map).

- **Recommendation: B.** Every control stays where the thumbs already are, so there is nothing to relearn. Only idle things go,
  which is the biggest declutter for the least risk. C is the next step if he wants the world to run to the bottom edge.
