**Ask:** What does color grade do I can't tell send me a screenshot before and after / show me toon vs standard lighting / clean vs cinematic post

Answered with iPhone 16 Pro captures of the same spawn-cove frame (scratchpad, sent to the user):
- Colour grade off / on: the learned LUT fitted to the mockups — slightly paler, cooler sand, lighter rocks, softer
  greens (mean pixel diff 9.7 / 255).
- Lighting standard / toon: toon flattens the shading into bands — rocks and sand go flatter and brighter, shadows
  crisper (diff 9.1).
- Post cinematic / clean: cinematic adds haze, grain, a darker vignette and more contrast / saturation (diff 15.4).
