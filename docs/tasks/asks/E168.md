## What was made

- **The master:** `~/Movies/Wildshard/wildshard-steam-trailer-45s-1080p60.mp4`. It is 45.000 s, 1920×1080 at 60 fps, H.264 High at ~36 Mb/s, with AAC 320k audio at −13.9 LUFS. That meets Steam's spec (≥ 1920×1080, H.264, ≥ 5000 kb/s). A 57 MB preview of the same cut sits next to it.
  The master is not in git: it is 204 MB and the uplink is slow.
- **The structure:**
  - Driftwood Isle runs 0–15 s.
  - Nalati Grasslands runs 15–30.35 s and opens on the score's percussion hit.
  - Pine Hollow runs 30.35–42.33 s, then the end card: PROJECT WILDSHARD / WISHLIST NOW ON STEAM, with the licence credits.
  - The contact sheet is `art/trailer/round-1-steam/contact-sheet.jpg`.
- **The pipeline** is a clean room in `scripts/steam-trailer/`; its [README](../../../scripts/steam-trailer/README.md) has the details. In short:
  - The score is a new MiniMax Music 3 cue, take 204 of 4, spliced to 45 s on its beats.
  - 8 sound-design families were each made with both MOSS and SA3 Medium, and the CLAP pick was used.
  - The game's own SFX are synced to the picture's events, over shard ambience beds.
  - Capture is deterministic and in engine: 4K supersampled down to 1080p, 120 Hz sub-frames for real motion blur, and spline camera rigs for the cinematic shots. The gameplay shots drive the player: the sword combo, horse archery at the gallop, the crossbow on the ghost stag, and the bow against the Storm Titan.
  - The captures come from a production build of HEAD, which avoids dev-server HMR reloads and keeps other sessions' WIP out.

## Found along the way

- **Fixed in the trailer's capture (not game bugs):**
  - The old `scripts/trailer/capture.mjs` clock drifted: `elapsedTime` also advanced on frames the gate skipped. Wall-clock reads (animals, the gallop bob) also ran at the wrong speed.
  - A camera carried over land left the player inside the terrain, so the underwater grade came on over dry land.
- **Game notes; not changed, since each is a design or taste call (Jake's):**
  - Driftwood's chunk-boundary lines are 1 px `LineBasicMaterial` lines. At any DPR they read as hairline scratches across a frame.
  - The Storm Titan's storm wall reads as a flat grey slab with a hard top edge from inside the arena.
  - Pine Hollow seen from above looks bald: the forest thins out with distance.
  - The Driftwood lagoon is 1.5–3 m deep, and the underwater view there is a flat cyan wash.
  - The trailer frames around all of these: the boundary and the storm wall are hidden in its shots, and it has no Pine aerials or dives.
