## The model (boat subagent, 2026-09-24)

Split: the main session fixes the turntable's coarse phone shadow map (src/explore/ModelExplorer.ts); this part
rebuilds the boat itself (src/world/Boat.ts).

- **Why it was off:** the old hull was zero-thickness single-layer strakes on one two-sided material that cast and
  received shadows, so every face shadowed itself (triangle acne that crawled as the turntable turned). It was also a
  crude tub: no plank thickness, a flat sheer, a bare gunwale and a flat-sheet sail. 386 tris.
- **B (the rebuild, now the default)** follows `art/driftwood-isle/round-8-assets/ref-sailboat.jpg`:
  - The hull is one closed loft of solid rings: outer skin, gunwale cap, inner skin, floor boards. Only 6 open edges
    in the whole hull mesh, against 71 before.
  - It is single-sided (FrontSide), so three draws its back faces into the shadow map and a lit face can't shadow
    itself.
  - The sheer rises to the stem and the transom. Five two-tone strakes a side, with a dark wale band.
  - Timbers and gear: stem post, keel strip, 14 ribs, 3 thwarts, quarter posts, rudder + tiller, rowlocks, cleats,
    oars, a rope coil and a lantern.
  - The sail is a bellied triangle on a boom with one patch, lashed to the mast, with a forestay, shrouds and a sheet.
    It is its own two-sided mesh that casts but never receives, and it still sways in the shared wind.
  - Budget: 2,296 tris in 3 draw calls, plus the mooring lines: the hull (casts + receives), the sail (casts
    only), and the thin gear (receives only; see below).
  - Colliders, floor and bobbing are unchanged.
- **A (the old boat)** is still there with `?boat=v1`.
- **Board:** `art/boat/round-1-rebuild/board.jpg` shows A | B in four rows: the Explore Model turntable at 390×844
  from two angles, the in-world view by the pier, and the deck at eye height at phone tier with a low sun. The single
  shots are next to it (`A-*.jpg`, `B-*.jpg`).
- Shipped in 31a5400.

### Follow-up: diagonal stripes on the deck in-world (the user, on the phone)

"There's a whole bunch of diagonal stripes on top of this boat."
- **Cause:** that was the old boat's self-shadow acne. Its floor and inner hull were open sheets on a two-sided material,
  and the phone's one 1024² cascade made it worse.
- **B has no stripes:** its hull is solid and single-sided, so only back faces go into the shadow map.
- **Thin gear:** the oars, lashings, boom, tiller, rigging, rope coil and lantern are 3–6 cm across. At the phone's
  ~10–15 cm shadow texels they cast a dotted chain of blobs across the floor. They now sit in a third mesh that receives
  but doesn't cast. The sail, mast and hull still cast.
- **v1 (`?boat=v1`)** now uses the kit's `lowPolyMaterial`, so it gets E112's shadow-normal flip (c738aba). Its floor
  stripes are mostly gone; saw-tooth acne remains on its thin inner walls (`A-deck.jpg`).
- **Checked:** at eye height on the deck (`?explore=world&touch&tier=phone&cam=-4.2,2.72,-241.6,0,-0.6`, with and
  without `&tod=0.3`) and from the bow (`cam=-4.2,2.9,-246.4,3.14,-0.7`).
