## Model Explorer lighting, stuck bolts, Nine Dragon thumbnails (2026-09-29, Claude subagent)

- **Model Explorer lights the dummies like the arena** (`90e0c6c6`). The turntable and the catalog thumbnail use the
  studio set (`src/practice/DummyStudio.ts`). The emissive = base-map trick is gone from `TrainingDummyAssets.ts`: every
  dummy material reads emissive 000000. Checked on Driftwood and Pine Hollow. The thumbnail now fits a tall model's
  height (it cut the dummy at the neck and the shins).
- **Stuck bolts and arrows ride the dummy** (`90e0c6c6`). The new `TargetAnimal.stuckFrame(point)` hook gives the bone
  whose segment passes nearest the hit.
  - Projectiles (the bows) keep the arrow in that bone's frame.
  - The crossbow now sticks a bolt in a practice dummy and attaches it to that bone. It never stuck in a target before.
  - Animals are unchanged.
  - Measured: a Pine Hollow bolt and a Nalati arrow in the straw dummy's Spine bone move about 0.13 m with a charged
    rock and return.
- **Nine Dragon's catalog thumbnails were white silhouettes.** The thumbnail render moved the camera, but the shard's
  silk fog was measured from the eye that `frame()` last saw (`uCam`), so every Nine Dragon material fogged out to white.
  - Fix: the new `Game.shardFrame()` runs the shard's per-frame uniforms for the posed camera, before the thumbnail
    render.
  - The walker, the sitter and the lion now read. The turntable was never affected.
  - **Second cause (the coordinator's World ▸ Models route, and every exported PNG).** Nine Dragon's materials write
    the view depth into alpha for the post chain, so the copied frame had alpha 5–16 where they drew. PNG exports turned
    those pixels white.
    - Fix: the thumbnail copy is flattened over black, as the screen shows it.
    - Checked: every thumbnail reads alpha 255 on the World ▸ Models, hub ▸ Models and `?explore=model` routes.
    - Pine Hollow and Driftwood are unchanged.
  - **The Fei Zhua hook's grey card.**
    - Its quantized cast (int16) was baked through the node matrix into int16 storage, which clamped every coordinate
      to ±1. The attributes are now widened to float first. The same geometry feeds the world's three hooks.
    - The card itself is the cast's wall plate (model −Z, against the wall in the city). Model Explorer opened on it,
      so the explorer copy is turned round to face that first view.
    - It keeps the shard's flat grey wash on standard materials.
- **Hit flash on Driftwood:** measured at its peak on the straw dummy, it adds +0.03 mean luminance on Driftwood and
  +0.03 on Pine Hollow. It is the same on both shards and lasts about 0.1 s. Left as it is.
