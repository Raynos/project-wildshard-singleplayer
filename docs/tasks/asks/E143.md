## What changed (`src/chunks/pine-hollow.ts`, `pineHollowLayout.ts`)

- **No Hollow grove.** PH-L1 round 2's `HOLLOW_GROVE` (× 2.4 on the bowl's keep) and its second candidate grid
  (`ChunkForest.infill`) are gone. The machinery in `placement.ts` stays, but no shard uses it now.
- **The whole forest at 0.7 of its keep** (`FOREST_KEEP`). The old-growth is × 1.6, not × 4. The × 4 filled every
  8.5 m grid cell there, so it read as a wall. Its giants stay as landmarks, 85 → 63, with open floor between them. The
  trails, cabin pads, the King's arena and the density noise's clearings are open as before.
- **Understory** (`understory`): shrubs × 12 → × 2.5, ferns × 1.5 → × 1, and ferns no longer fill the dense shade
  (`fernCanopy` off). Ferns stay in their clusters and along the trail and pond verges.
- **Phone grass carpet** 56 → 40 slots per 4 m cell (E142's knob, `tier.ts PINE_HOLLOW_PHONE`). In Jake's photo the
  bottom half of the screen was blades.
- `terrain.bin` (the placement decision log) and `navmesh.bin` are re-baked. Fewer trunks leave bigger navmesh polys, so
  `NavMesh.randomPointNear` now draws up to 4 times and keeps a point within 1.5 × the asked radius. Before this, a
  20 m wander could land 46 m away.
