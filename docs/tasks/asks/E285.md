## Three bugs, found and fixed

1. **No weapon and no dodge after Explore ▸ Practice (every shard).** The title page is renderer-free, so EXPLORE WORLD
   boots the shard with `arrival.mode === 'explore'`. That path never gave the HUD its ENTER WORLD handler
   (`hud.showIntro(enter)` is only called on the menu-first boot). Practice → `enterArenaNow` → `enterNow` →
   `this.onEnter?.()` did nothing, so the weapon stayed disabled and hidden from Explore, and the touch DODGE disc
   returns early while `!weapons.enabled`. Reproduced on live `8c62342-mumo6tgc`: Driftwood, weapon
   `enabled=false visible=false`, the touch DODGE moved 0.00 m. Fix: `hud.setOnEnter(enter)` on that boot.
2. **Dodge dead over most of Driftwood's arena.** The room stands 900 m over each shard's spawn. On Driftwood that is
   over the sea, and `Player.deepAt` (the pier-edge probe that ends a dash before deep water) cast from 3 m over the
   surface. It never saw the arena floor, so every dodge over the water died in place. Fix: the probe casts from the
   feet when they are higher.
3. **~1 dodge in 6 cut short on flat ground, everywhere (not only the arena).** Rapier's character controller
   sometimes stops a whole move on the floor it already rests on (one contact, normal straight up, time of impact
   0). `Player.step` ends a dash on any step that moves < 30 % of what it asked, so the dodge stopped dead. Measured
   with 40 chained dodges: arena 33/40 full, Pine Hollow terrain 35/40 full. Fix: `CharacterMotor.move` retries a
   stall whose every contact is walkable floor with the move flattened. `test/physics-dash-stall.test.ts` reproduced
   it (22 of 1200 dash steps stalled) and now passes.

Also: entering the arena steps off the hoverboard (`spawn()` kept it, and on the board there is no dodge).
