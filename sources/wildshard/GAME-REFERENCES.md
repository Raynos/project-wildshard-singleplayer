## Project wildshard gameplay features

 - Staging only hoverboard for faster traversal
 - The mega grid highway between chunks, with cars for faster traversal
 - Auto pathing to far away chunks
 - First person, shooter mechanics
 - First person, melee knife ( PUBG / pan / Counterstrike )
 - NPCs as enemies
 - NPCs as quest givers
 - Guns / bow arrow / crossbow 
 - Quests
 - The ability to pickup weapons and items
 - The ability to equip armor and cosmetics to customize your look and feel
 - Health and damage. 
 - Respawning
 - Talking in chat, ( think the WoW style chunk, global chat, local chat proximity )
 - Puzzle mechanisms
    - Pressure plates, pushing blocks and barrels
    - Finding keys, opening closing locked doors.

 - Opening doors, interior / exterior / stairs, locked doors / rooms
 - Chests & locked chests ? 
 - A player in the world has a "Name" and like a "Title" they cna put under the name.
 - Parkour style gameplay like from Guild Wars 2
