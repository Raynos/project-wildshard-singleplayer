# W2 visual QA: Pine Hollow and Driftwood Isle, morning against HEAD (2026-10-14)

Lane sp-o203 (Opus, visual QA) for the SHARD-PLATFORM coordinator. A read-only, by-eye check of the ~118 commits that
landed between this morning and now (graph-material ports, baked geometry, shared quest / weapon / creature systems).
The bar is G415: no graphical glitches, graphically similar, no major regressions.

- **Before:** `f3c781683` (build `f3c7816-mv84eewk`). **After:** `91357eaf5` (HEAD at the start, build
  `91357ea-mv83rve7`).
- Each build came from a clean `git archive` export (`scripts/serve-build.sh --rev`: archive, `link-node-modules.mjs`,
  `gen.mjs`, `build-shardfiles.mjs`, vite build), one preview at a time, from the lane scratch.
- Every capture was an "iPhone 16 Pro" portrait (402×874 at 3×) at `tier=phone` with `touch`, muted, with
  `Math.random` pinned and every animal on `harnessHold` once the shard was ready. Pine's weather was set to `clear`
  for every pose except the rain pose. The same scripted poses ran on both builds: Pine 62 poses, Driftwood 57 poses
  (46 midday, 8 at sunset and 8 at night for each shard, set with `app.dayCycle.setTime`), plus 8 + 9 Model Explorer
  cards. I looked at every pair by eye, and at full size wherever the mean pixel difference stood out.
- Boards (labelled BEFORE / AFTER on each frame): `progress/shard-platform/w2-visual-qa-2026-10-14/`. Raw frames stayed
  in the lane scratch; the "frames" column names the board a pose appears on.

## Console (errors and warnings that differ)

- **No GL errors, no page errors** on either build, in play or in the Explorer, for either shard.
- **Pine, in play:** HEAD adds two `THREE.TSL: Declaration name 'irradiance' … Renamed to 'irradiance_1'` warnings.
  The morning build had none in Pine. It's the same harmless TSL rename that Sky and Signal already print (W1). It's
  likely from one of today's node-material ports. Minor; no visible effect.
- **Pine, Explorer:** HEAD adds the two `[models] a live 'deer' / 'elk' is no model on this level's roster` warnings
  (F1) and the TSL rename.
- **Driftwood:** the morning build warned `[nodes] a level fog patch (level.fog.driftwood-isle) does not reach graph
  materials: they get the engine fog only`. HEAD no longer does (fixed today; `7d3892998` / `c07624fc4` are likely).
- The same on both builds: `THREE.Clock` deprecated, and `THREE.Material: parameter 'displacementScale' /
  'displacementBias' has value of undefined`.
