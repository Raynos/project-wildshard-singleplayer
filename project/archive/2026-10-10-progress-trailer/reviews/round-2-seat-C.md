# Round 2 · Seat C (red team: a creative director who cuts game trailers and devlogs)

Object: `docs/plans/PROGRESS-TRAILER.md` (the beat sheet §2.2 above all). Checked against the material: the alpha trailer
(`site/media.json` → `trailer.mp4Phone`, 63.2 s; a 2 s contact sheet), `scripts/steam-trailer/cuts/alpha.mjs`,
`shots/{nine-dragon,dunes,grid}.mjs`, `edit.mjs`, `titles.mjs`, `alpha-jobs.json`, Sky Reach's archive (first / middle /
last `h2-windmill` and `aerial-overview` side by side), the day-1 stills and `progress/timelapse.mp4`, and the four
chapter SHAs' crossbow, camera, player and audio sources in git. The verdict up front: the skeleton is right, but as
written an executing agent makes a **competent, metronomic video whose rewind moment lasts half a second and hits
nothing**. The fixes below are what turns it epic.
