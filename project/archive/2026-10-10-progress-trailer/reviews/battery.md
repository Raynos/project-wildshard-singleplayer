# Battery: the progress trailer council (E468)

Scenarios the video (round 1) and then the plan (round 2 on) must answer. A seat walks each one; a step with no answer
is a finding. Seats may add scenarios; they stay.

| # | Scenario |
|---|---|
| S1 | **The scroll test.** A stranger on YouTube / X / TikTok sees the first 3 s with the sound off. Do they know what this is and want the next 3 s? |
| S2 | **"Is that real gameplay?"** A sceptical gamer pauses on every first-person shot. Does each one survive (a real build, a real player view, things happening, no rigged fly-through passed off as play)? |
| S3 | **Day-1 gameplay.** Film 5 s of first-person play on the day-1 build (`568a1463f`: a desktop crossbow game, pointer lock, stags and boars, no touch HUD). What drives the player, and how is it captured at a fixed step? |
| S4 | **Shard authoring.** Show one shard going from nothing to finished over its commits (e.g. Sky Reach: `progress/far-reach/` has 42 dated captures and time-lapses). Is the material there, is the change legible in 4–8 s, and what does the viewer see that says "authored with Claude Code"? |
| S5 | **Week 4.** Next week Jake wants the same trailer with one more chapter. How much is re-run versus re-made? |
| S6 | **Where it plays.** Jake watches it in Claude iOS on his phone (portrait); it is also posted to YouTube and X and maybe the site. Which master(s), which aspect, which length? |
| S7 | **Sound.** With sound on, does the cut land on the music, and do gameplay moments have SFX? |
| S8 | **No collision.** The trailer agent is mid-way through TRAILERS TR9 / CT1 and owns `scripts/steam-trailer/`. Can the progress trailer be built without editing their files under them or duplicating their rows? |
| S9 | **Epic.** Name the one moment a viewer would rewind to. If there isn't one, that is a finding. |
| S10 | **Receipts.** A commenter checks an on-screen number, date or SHA against the public commit log. Is every one generated from git by the render, with its SHA? (R1B, round 1) |
| S11 | **Same spot, four builds.** A viewer can see the game get better at one fixed place and verb; no later build reads worse. (R1C, round 1) |
| S12 | **Not the alpha again.** A viewer who saw the alpha trailer on wildshard.io: is this trailer visibly its own (history leads, its own sound and score)? (R2C, round 2) |
