# Battery: the scenarios every seat walks (only grows)

Walk each one for **both** new shards, side by side with the other four shards' captures of the same kind. A step where
the new shard reads clearly below the other four is a finding (with the frame, the comparison frame and a concrete fix).

1. **The title deck**: the shard's card and hero image in the deck next to the other five.
2. **The first frame** (`first-frame.jpg`): Jake taps Enter World. Does the first second read as a finished place, with
   a clear subject, depth, light and colour, at the level of the others' first frames?
3. **The first minute**: who or what starts the quest, is the goal named, is the next step marked on the minimap and in
   the world (Driftwood's Wendell is the reference)?
4. **The four hero views** (`h1`–`h4`): composition, materials, lighting, density near and far, the sky, against the
   others' four hero views.
5. **From above** (`aerial-spawn`, `aerial-overview`, `clip.mp4`): does the place hold up when the camera leaves the
   player's eye (no void, no unfinished edges, believable scale)?
6. **The hands and the weapon**: at rest in every first-person frame, and in the Practice Arena (idle, attack, heavy,
   special): model, material, pose, motion, against the other shards' viewmodels.
7. **The fight and the boss**: the creatures and the boss as models and in motion (the boards in `art/<slug>/`).
8. **The time-lapse** (`progress/<slug>/timelapse-heroes.mp4`): is each loop moving the shard toward the bar?
