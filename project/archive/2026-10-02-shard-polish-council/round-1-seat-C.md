**The red-team read in four lines.**
1. **Hero frames with no hero.** The other four put a lit, readable subject in the middle third of every hero view: the
   pier, the gate, the cabin, the kurgan. The new two still frame empty sand (SD h3), a mesa with the fire off-screen
   (SD h4), a striped ghost bridge where the windmill should be (SR h2), and a wall filling a quarter of the frame (SR h3).
2. **Primitive forms behind finished colour.** Four loops tuned the palette (ΔE00 per region) while the forms stayed
   procedural: 12-gon cones on a grid, flat kites, soft sine dunes. Colour converged and the silhouettes did not. The
   other four are dense in form, not only in colour.
3. **One hue per shard, and the same hue twice.** Both new shards are an orange or peach golden hour. In the title deck
   the two new cards look like siblings, while every other shard owns a hue. Both bibles already call for the cool
   half (Signal Dunes' violet shade and indigo zenith; Sky Reach's blue zenith, spring green and cyan glow). The grade
   washes it out.
4. **The first ten seconds.** Sky Reach's quest giver stands outside the portrait frustum, behind the HOVER tab. Signal
   Dunes names the person and not the goal. Driftwood's chip does both ("WHO LIT THE FIRE? | CASTAWAY 123 M").
