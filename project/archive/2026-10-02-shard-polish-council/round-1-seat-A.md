# Round 1 — seat A

Reviewed as an art director and player against the frozen ledger. Ranked by how strongly the point lowers the perceived finish on the phone. Signal Dunes means `sunscar-dunes`; Sky Reach means `far-reach`.

The review builds are **eaeb401f** (`progress/sunscar-dunes/20261002-0041-eaeb401f/`) and **c889fb2f** (`progress/far-reach/20261002-0043-c889fb2f/`). Reference frames are the four shards' `20261002-0011-0d59505c` captures. Code causes below were checked against those capture commits with read-only `git show`; subsequent working-tree edits are not evidence that a visual finding is fixed.

Evidence reviewed: all seven comparison sheets, full-size new-shard frames and selected full-size reference frames, portrait card assets, style bibles, plans, loop boards, sampled orbit sequences across their duration, both hero time-lapses, Practice Arena move boards, and the older boss videos. No live browser or new capture was run. A **pass** below means no supported below-the-bar finding in the available evidence, not certification of an unobserved input sequence. In particular, the round-6 boss videos predate the current model/look passes; neither proves current three-phase combat motion. The Nine Dragon orbit samples are overexposed/clipped, so they are not used as a successful aerial reference.

## Battery — Signal Dunes

1. **Title deck — Pass.** The current portrait, landscape and thumbnail in `src/shards/sunscar-dunes/thumbs/`, shown in `art/sunscar-dunes/round-13-loop-4/cards.jpg`, establish dunes, sunset, tower and flyer. They agree with the round-1 playable palette. The reference portrait assets were compared directly. Sparse desert space is not itself a defect.
2. **First frame — R1A-2, R1A-4.** Crest, distant tower, layered dusk and Sefa establish a place and a route. The viewmodel finish and already-damaged entry interrupt it. The six-second capture delay is acknowledged; this supports a first-minute failure, not a literal first-render timing claim.
3. **First minute — R1A-4.** Sefa is visible, waving and named in both the chip and world pin. `quest/install.ts` and `strings.ts` specify talk → logbook → oil → three waymarks → signal fire → Matriarch, with active markers, prompts and reward. This is a coherent chain using the baseline presentation. The immediate ray attack is the supported failure; I did not infer a broken marker chain from hero shots that teleport without completing Sefa's step.
4. **Four hero views — R1A-2, R1A-3, R1A-5.** H1 has a legible tower beyond the crests; H2's wagon lacks readable surfaces; H3's unlit waymark is expected before the oil/fire step; H4 restores coloured distant layers and shows the signal-fire prompt. Do not “fix” H3 by making unlit quest braziers burn from entry, or H4 by adding another landmark. Material/creature finish is the issue, not desert density.
5. **From above — Pass.** `aerial-spawn.jpg`, `aerial-overview.jpg` and sampled `clip.mp4` at 0, 2, 4, 6 and 8 s show continuous dune forms and distant mesa layers, without an exposed square void in those views. Compared with Driftwood and Nalati's corresponding frames/orbits. The dune rhythm and empty sand carry the intended scale.
6. **Hands and weapon — R1A-2.** Idle is directly evidenced in every round-1 hero frame. The light/heavy poses are supported by `art/sunscar-dunes/round-8-polish/board-whip.jpg` and the loop-2 Arena row; `Bullwhip.ts` supplies the unroll/double-crack/pull implementation. Those earlier boards do not prove current continuous animation or the pull's visual impact. The current idle asset is sufficient to establish the finding; do not close it on damage numbers alone.
7. **Fight and boss — R1A-3, R1A-4.** The ordinary ray is directly visible in the current H1; its model and initial aggression are below the bar. The generated Matriarch on `art/sunscar-dunes/round-11-loop-2/board-before-after-target.jpg` is a substantial improvement over the procedural boss in the round-6 video. No separate current-boss motion defect is asserted: the video is old, and current grounded/storm phases were not visually exercised. `combat/matriarch.ts` and `species/matriarch.ts` supply the phase design, not proof of its present visual quality.
8. **Time-lapse — Pass for direction.** `progress/sunscar-dunes/timelapse-heroes.mp4`, sampled through its history, moves from empty/poorly lit terrain and an unreadable handle toward crest shadows, readable quest staging, caravan structure, coloured depth and a dusk cloud deck. The round-13 before/after/target board corroborates the latest step. The direction is strong; R1A-2/R1A-3/R1A-5 identify the asset finish still lagging behind the improved world.

## Battery — Sky Reach

1. **Title deck — R1A-8.** Direct comparison of all six portrait assets shows Sky Reach's card is from the earlier look. Its subject is clear, but it represents less finish than the current world and the reference cards.
2. **First frame — R1A-6, R1A-9.** Bridge rails lead to the windmill, the sun separates the islands, and the gold/blue/pink sky establishes depth immediately. The clipped keeper and broad unfinished-looking fan weaken the otherwise clear composition. R1A-1 is visible in the island behind the mill, but the aerials establish that finding more decisively.

Markers and the later named navigation are evidenced, so the supported fix is first-step wording and placement. Full step completion was not played during this review.

5. **From above — R1A-1.** Both aerials and the orbit at 0, 2, 4, 6 and 8 s consistently expose repeated green lids and smooth inverted cones. There is no supported void finding: the cloud sea covers the background. Driftwood's varied low-poly land is the relevant finish comparison, not a requirement to adopt Pine Hollow's PBR style.

No current visual failure is invented from that old clip or from the lack of a boss in a posed hero frame. A current three-phase phone clip would be needed to upgrade this limited pass to a motion judgement.
