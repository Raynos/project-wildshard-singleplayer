# CONTENT-GAP council, round 1, seat B: evidence and coverage

**Overall read.** I checked the doc against its evidence, and most of it holds up. Every code path in §8 exists at the
tag, and the tag resolves to `dcd6a29a`. The art counts are exact: 3,294 files under `art/`, 1,306 under
`art/nine-dragon-stack/` and 26 under `art/quest/`. The clocks (48, 24 and 26 min), the `LastPlace` wiring
(`main.ts:950-951`), Driftwood's 92 / 90 coin cap and the `LOOK-LOOP.md` method all match the code.

The GW2 numbers I re-checked on the wiki (raw wikitext, 2026-10-01) hold as well:
- **Queensdale:** 17 hearts, 16 waypoints, 21 POIs, 9 vistas, 7 hero challenges, 1 jumping puzzle, 69 event entries.
- **Silverwastes:** 3 waypoints, 18 POIs, 7 vistas, 6 hero challenges, 47 event entries (some repeat), so ~39 is fair.
- **Dry Top:** 20 + 16 events.
- **Bloodstone Fen:** 22 event entries, 7 POIs, 3 vistas, 4 waypoints, 2 mastery insights.
- **Map bonus:** a reward every 200 points up to 8,000, every 20th reward ×10, an 8-week rotation that turns over
  Thursday 20:00 UTC.
- **Wizard's Vault:** 4 dailies at +10, the login at +5, the daily chest at +20, 8 weeklies at +50, 6 of them for a
  +450 chest, a 1,300 Acclaim soft cap.

The problems are in the claims the doc builds on that evidence:
- **Finishability already in a plan.** It says finishability is "already a row in a singleplayer plan". It isn't:
  FINISH-LINE S1 is a 20 s boot-and-walk smoke test, the plan is an unapproved draft, and "GAME-NORMALIZATION N9" does
  not exist as a gate row.
- **Two of the four mastery verbs.** Pine *cut* tracking by design, and diving is shared player code that already works
  everywhere.
- **What the doc is silent on.** The every-shard 14 m/s hoverboard, which breaks the density rule and the mastery gates;
  Nine Dragon's 500 m height, which VISION and the platform plan both flag as open; and the fact that its own nearest GW2
  analogue runs its meta on a per-instance timer, not a global clock.
- **The Beats gate is defined three different ways.** One of them (§13) still puts the look before the played greybox.
