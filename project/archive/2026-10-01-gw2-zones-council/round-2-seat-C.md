## Overall read

Round 1 fixed the doc's worst faults. The audit holds, the grammar is no longer a GW2 checklist, and the session unit
answers the phone. But the pendulum swung too far in three places, and each one is checkable against the repo.
**(1) Look-last is drawn from half of Driftwood's lesson.** The one fun shard was mockup-first. At 00:35 there were
eight real-HUD mockups. At 00:44 Jake picked the style the engine could hit, and at 00:50 a mockup map became the layout.
The first code started at 01:00. Pine shows the cost of a look chosen without a frame budget: its fights run at
35–48 ms on the phone. **(2) The per-shard models replace each shard's own pitch with a borrowed genre.** Nalati's pitch
(tame, hunt wolves from the saddle, hide in the grass, ride out storms, two kings) becomes "kokpar and races". Pine's
"eerie folklore" (PH-U20) becomes Monster Hunter with a currency Jake ruled out (PH-U16) and a daily clock he also ruled
out (PH-U21). Nine Dragon's designed solo climax, the Well Dragon, goes to "waits for players". **(3) Jake's control
shrinks to two gates.** Step 4 hands look decisions to the director, against his standing rule "everything is a
decision on my plate". Yet Driftwood had 42 steering prompts from him in under two and a half hours. On the lens questions:
- The daily seed is a floor, not a reason to come back. It only adds novelty when it changes a decision, and Day 30
  leaves out the game's own reason to return: new player shards.
- The 10–15 min unit does kill the big moments, because no layer holds an arc or a climax.
- The director-as-file can work, but the doc never says where the file lives, who opens a director session, or who may
  edit the file.

**The single most important thing still missing:** each shard's **pillars and signature moment, taken from its own
pitch and Jake's picks, and protected.** §4.3 step 0 asks for "the story players will tell", but §9 never fills it in,
and so §9's models quietly drop it. A shard feels designed by its own director when it is unmistakably itself, not when
it runs the right process.
