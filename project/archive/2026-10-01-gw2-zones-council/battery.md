# GW2-ZONES council: the scenario battery

> **State:** started 2026-10-01. It only grows: a seat may add a scenario, and it stays. Every round walks all of
> them through [docs/design/gw2-zones/GW2-ZONES.md](../../../docs/design/gw2-zones/GW2-ZONES.md). For each one, say step by step whether the doc answers it
> unambiguously. A step it doesn't answer, or answers in a way that would make a worse game, is a finding.

| # | Scenario |
|---|---|
| S1 | **Shard 5 from zero.** A new shard director (an agent) is told "make a desert canyon shard" and given only this doc and the grammar. Walk the first two weeks: what does it produce, in what order, what does Jake review, and when does the first mockup happen? |
| S2 | **Nine Dragon's P1.** Apply §4's content-first loop to Nine Dragon, which already has a world fragment and a big plan. What does its director do first, and what happens to the existing art? |
| S3 | **A 15-minute phone session.** A player opens Pine Hollow on an iPhone, on the bus, for 15 minutes, at the shard's current state grown per §9. Minute by minute: what do they do, and what do they leave with? |
| S4 | **Day 30.** A player who has finished all four first-party shards opens the game on day 30 of the MMO. Why are they here today, where do they go, and what do they earn? |
| S5 | **An outside author.** A player with Claude Code builds and uploads a shard. What does the grammar force them to make, what does the validator check, and how does a player ever find their shard? |
| S6 | **The first hour of Driftwood, re-directed.** Walk Driftwood's first hour as its director would redesign it under §4 and §9, against the density rule (a notice every 20–40 s, an interaction every 1–2 min, a set piece every 5–15 min). Where are the dead stretches today? |
| S7 | **Two players meet.** Two players arrive in Nalati at the same time once multiplayer exists. What is different for them from playing solo: events, the boss, rewards? |
| S8 | **Jake's week.** Jake is creative director over four shard directors. What lands on his desk each week, and what no longer does? |
| S9 | **Travel speed** (added by seat B, round 1). A player crosses a shard on the 14 m/s hoverboard. Does the content map's density rule hold, can verb or vista gates be skipped, and does a medal time count hover time? |
| S10 | **"Jake says boring"** (added by seat C, round 1). Jake plays a director's greybox for under 10 minutes on his phone and files one inbox note, "boring". What does the director do next, what evidence does it gather, and what rule decides between iterating and cutting the verb or beat? |
| S11 | **The climax in a phone session** (added by seat C, round 2). A player with ~20 minutes reaches a shard's climax (Jel Ata as a storm builds; the Well Dragon after the eighth envelope). Does the doc let it happen inside the session rules, how is the player told to start it, and where can they stop if the bus arrives? |
| S12 | **Jake decides on his phone in five minutes** (proposed by seat C, round 3). Jake opens the doc on his iPhone with five minutes. Can he find what he must decide, see one recommended answer for each, and answer without reading the reference sections? |
