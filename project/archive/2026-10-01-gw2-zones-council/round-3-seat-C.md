## Overall read

Round 2's fixes landed, and the doc is now right about each shard: the pillars, signature moments, the climax rule
and the Arc loop are real improvements, and §9 respects Jake's picks. **The pendulum did swing back in two places,
though.** First, Jake's load. The loop now routes "every taste axis … from a board, as today" to him (l.263). Five days
after the "everything is a decision on my plate" quote it rests on, Jake said the opposite about how picks reach him:
"whatever the picks are, I can't even… But the plans, I care about the plans" (2026-09-28). Today he has 29 asks
waiting on him (10 `needs pick`, 19 `needs you`). Second, the pillars. Round 2 made them "fixed at its pitch" (Q0),
and Pine's list now includes implementation picks (PH-U16, PH-U21). Three places in the doc give three different
rules for who may change a pillar. **The director is not too thin.** It owns beats, map, encounters, the climax and
rewards. That is the usual game-director / creative-director split, and it is enough to direct.

**On usability:** the doc is 10,495 words, up 17 % this round. Its "one page" summary (§1) runs 720 words and holds no
decision. The decisions sit at line 829 of 890. §14 also drops the decision the doc calls "the most valuable", and one
question has no recommended answer.

**The single highest-leverage gap** is that the director loop never has a human play the arc or the climax end to end
on the phone, even though the doc names exactly that as cause 1. Both gates play pieces of ≤ 15 min, and then step 5
builds the return loop on top.
