# CONTENT-GAP council — round 2, seat A

> **State:** independent review, 2026-10-01. Lens: veteran open-world / MMO / mobile game director. Reviewed `207e362 → e81d65c` for `docs/CONTENT-GAP.md`, the register, all round-1 reviews, the ledger and S1–S10. Singleplayer evidence is read-only from `wildshard-singleplayer` at `pre-normalization` (`dcd6a29a`); no build was played.

The revised loop could produce four directed, fun shards: it now proves a verb before planning a session, lets failed play change the space, and gives directors ownership without multiplying Jake's approvals. The models fit the existing strengths: Driftwood's puzzle reveals and swordplay, Pine's deliberate ranged encounters, Nalati's riding, and Nine Dragon's vertical route choices. Nine Dragon's stair-versus-hook choice is particularly concrete. These are credible pitches, not proof of fun on first-person portrait touch; a model name, timer and cosmetic sink still cannot establish that. The remaining problems are in the revised contracts: Jake can approve a session without reaching its payoff, Pine's new short hunt does not ensure its target or local reward presentation, and the daily-seed and hoverboard rules leave misleading implementation guidance. All findings below concern the diff; none re-argues the ledger or the parked decisions.
