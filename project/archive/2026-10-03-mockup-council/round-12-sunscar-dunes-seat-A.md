## Ledger §5 audit

- **Views/geometry:** fixed camera file and identical actual cameras; no re-aim. The added ridge and rebakes are normal
  world terrain, also visible in aerials. Subjects are real meshes; sky/ranges are distant backdrop and fire/smoke are
  effects on world structures. No painted substitute for a walkable subject was found.
- **B `logbook`: reachable by source inspection.** `stage()` sets the same SCOUT_FLAG as Sefa's first dialogue,
  leaves the logbook unread and snaps to the normal 0.50 dusk milestone, then the recorder waits eleven seconds.
  A player can wait at this milestone. There is no fabricated distant interaction prompt.
- **C `waymarks-lit`: reachable timing, with normal effects.** The helper invokes logbook, well pull/jar, oil and
  brazier light handlers, preserving raised bucket, removed jar, oil, flames, flags and notices. It settles the
  two-lit predecessor at dusk 0.74, then performs the final action and lets ordinary 0.02/s easing run during the
  three-second settle: approximately 0.80, not snapped 0.86 with fresh notices. The all-lit notice's 3.2 s life plus
  0.5 s exit accommodates the capture. Brazier order is unrestricted: a player can oil the pictured western brazier,
  leave it last, crack it from the normal 7–8 m reach and step back toward the roughly 11 m camera during the notice.
  No ignition-age-dependent replacement effect was found. This is source reachability, not a new phone playthrough.
- **D `waymarks-lit`: reachable settled milestone.** The same ordinary all-lit state persists; eleven seconds is
  sufficient for dusk 0.86 and faded notices. The terrain-supported crest camera can be reached by walking. B/C/D
  are all correctly listed in `meta.json.staged`; no QA retake changed the declared settle.
- **Pose/effects:** the viewmodel retains the single ordinary `HD_GLOVE` transform, with no camera branch or frozen
  transient attack. C includes flame, sparks, plume, light and quest notices. A/dusk-fire are reachable initial
  states with the normal always-burning keeper lamp, distinct from the unlit quest signal fire.
- **Phone/HUD:** recorder requests 390×844 @3, phone tier and touch, storing 780×1688 JPEGs; scored shots restore
  baseline HUD and viewmodel after aerials. The old mockup HUD differences are not authority to restyle this build.
- **No narrowing:** **the wider look regresses**, as R12-A-P1 and the paired orbit measurements establish. The
  score is diagnostic until that regression is repaired and the contextual evidence recaptured. Quest/weapon handlers
  inspected here are unchanged; calmed creatures (`active: []`) mean the orbit cannot establish combat regression
  coverage. This finding does not assert that combat itself broke.
- **Budgets/shipping:** reported parity and caller-supplied capture SHA are useful provenance, but measured phone
  performance, total loading/Explorer memory and exact deployment identity remain unverified on this surface.
  No measured budget breach is asserted.
