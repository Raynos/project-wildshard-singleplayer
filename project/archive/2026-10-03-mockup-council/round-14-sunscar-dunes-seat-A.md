## Ranked findings and concrete fixes

1. **Restore the large dune composition and light together — A/dusk x 0–100%, y 35–65%; D y 44–66%.** Rework the actual crest path/profile and key relationship so the named A camera sees the long lit left-to-right descending face, a lower shaded saddle and multiple receding sharp crests. Reduce the right mound's dominance; retain traversable lee profiles and dark transverse bands in D. Verify the tangents actually cross the key and that lee direction agrees with the shard wind. Judge the geometry in both aerials, all hero views and the orbit before touching exposure. The current uplift's sign is correct, but its form/light result is not.
2. **Fit the hand and reshape the idle cord — all views, lower half; especially D x 55–100%, y 65–85%.** Use D's compact closed oval, handle angle and partially obscured grip as the silhouette target. Remove the tall kink and two strands falling through the bottom of the frame; put the coil beside the glove rather than around the lower screen. Narrow the inflated knuckles, add readable stitched panel boundaries, wrist folds and restrained worn-edge highlights. Verify the shared idle pose in A/B/C as well as D; changing only a screenshot's pose would not satisfy the ledger.
3. **Match the sky's structure and glow placement — A/dusk y 18–36%; B/D y 25–49%.** Use the target's finer broken cloud banks, warmer dusty amber in dusk-fire and broad violet-pink D afterglow. In A, move the strongest visible glow toward the lower horizon instead of leaving the upper band brighter: target means over full-width y 24–30% / 30–36% are **102.6 / 109.8**, game **111.4 / 93.0**. Dusk-fire's sky mean is already Y 99.9 versus 75.9; further blanket brightening would worsen it.
4. **Make sand detail follow the target's material and landform — A/dusk y 55–85%; C/D near swales.** Suppress periodic ribs on the steep right face, and reduce coarse black stippling in favor of fine directional grazing highlights. Restore dusk-fire's bright near slope after the geometry/key correction. Keep C/D's quiet patches smoother; increased fine contrast alone is not success. Use the unobstructed luminance rectangles above to prevent the large cord from contaminating sand comparisons.
5. **Finish the waymark's fire, fuel, smoke and base — C x 20–50%, y 25–60%.** Add readable crossed blackened fuel in the bowl, a genuinely hot core with broken orange edges, and layered turbulent smoke catching orange light on its lit side. Retain moving embers and the fresh normal toast. Give the support soot-dark iron and a rougher, rounder stone foot, and concentrate the granular pool around it rather than lifting the whole foreground. Preserve the newly visible far waymark.
6. **Give the caravan convincing close-range construction — B x 25–78%, y 40–54%.** Deepen irregular canvas folds/frayed hems, weather and divide the broad tailboard, improve wheel/spoke depth and cargo contacts, and break up the plume. B's front brightness is now close; surface construction is the more useful next change. Keep the logbook unread and its real interaction radius.
7. **Recover the dusk-fire focal subjects — dusk-fire x 15–60%, y 18–40%.** Make the existing tower platform/fire silhouette readable at this distance and allow the existing ray's normal flight to yield the large recognizable silhouette above the tower. Capture a real moment, retaining its motion and normal quest state. Do not solve this by a static bird card, enlarged world-space label or HUD removal.
