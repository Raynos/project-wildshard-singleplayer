## Ledger §5: no-shortcut and reachable-state audit

- **Geometry / effects:** the named source and contextual views show real dunes, caravan, tent, horse, tower, braziers and weapon geometry. No painted stand-in for a walkable subject was found. Sky/ranges are distant backdrop; fire, smoke and ground glow are effects on real structures. Their rough finish is an art gap, not a screenshot-cheat finding.
- **Phone / baseline HUD:** the captured revision's recorder requests 390×844 @3, phone tier and touch; screenshots are downsampled to 780×1688. It restores the HUD and viewmodel after aerials. The mock frames show the baseline controls and tracker. Keep them: missing mockup vitals, different quest chips and the older dusk-fire ammo/fire layout do not justify a special capture HUD. No staged viewmodel pose/freeze is present.
- **B `logbook`: reachable by source inspection.** `plugin.ts:stage()` sets SCOUT_FLAG, the same effect as Sefa's first dialogue (`quest/scout.ts`). This leaves the logbook unread and names it as the next objective. Dusk 0.25 is reached in normal play after easing. The camera is outside the real 2.4 m interaction radius, so absence of the mockup's distant READ prompt is not a reason to fabricate one.
- **C/D `waymarks-lit`: reachable by source inspection.** The stage calls the normal logbook interaction, well pull, oil-jar interaction, then each brazier's oil interaction and light action. `world/build.ts` supplies the read label, raised bucket, removed jar, visible poured oil, flames, glow, flags and labels. Three seconds of C settling exceeds the well's 1.2 s lift; D settles eleven seconds. The later dusk is the ordinary target derived from three lit braziers (0.86), reached by the live update system. Both stages are listed in meta.json; C's toasts accompany the real completion actions. No new playthrough was performed by this seat.
- **Dusk-fire:** unstaged in the recorded camera and meta.json; its tracker remains at Sefa, and its tower is unlit. This is a reachable initial state but does **not** depict the target's burning tower. It earns the supplied image's lower score; it is not evidence that the target milestone was captured. The ray is visible in aerials/clip. The recorder calms animals, but the ray still glides around its home while held before Sefa; it is not frozen into a pose for this frame.
- **Re-aims:** B and C move toward the mockups' relationships as explained above. D's undocumented yaw change also has a visible target-matching purpose; document it. I found no evidence of a deliberate camera dodge. A is unchanged.
- **Narrowing / budgets / shipping:** trail-post removal is global, not camera-conditioned, and no quest/combat deletion was found in the inspected shard diff. Wider views still show the places and creatures. However, aerials contain conspicuous dark rectangular bands that should be explained/checked by the lead; an orbit does not establish normal-play regression coverage. Source commit `96c647c50` records parity at nearby `b69c79d16` (walk/combat/leak/pause-resume) and raises the measured phone GPU ceiling to 108.28 MB. That is useful evidence, but GPU bytes are not total loading/Explorer process memory, and it is not exact `a9e50413` verification. The round surface does not provide frame-time distributions, total memory measurements or deployment identity. The recorder takes the SHA from its caller and does not compare it to the served build. Zero page errors, a 30 fps HUD and a 30 fps encoded clip cannot close these gaps. This is missing proof, not a claim that the build exceeds the limits or regressed.

No confirmed screenshot shortcut warrants voiding the numerical comparison. The named staged milestones are supported as ordinary play states. Camera documentation and exact-build verification remain incomplete, and the pictures themselves remain clearly below the 8/10 bar.

SCORE signal-dunes: 5.7
