## Findings, ranked by score gained

### Signal Dunes

1. **Re-light the dusk so the sun rakes and the near ground isn't a black slab** (dusk-fire, A, D; lower half of every
   frame). Every mockup has a low, warm key light grazing the dune faces: lit ripples and soft terminators near the
   camera, and cool shadow only in the troughs. The game puts the whole foreground in one flat navy shadow with a
   straight hard edge, while the far dune is lit hot. Fix: lift the shadow fill and ambient toward the mockups' warm
   brown shadow tone, soften the shadow's edge, and set the sun's azimuth so the spawn crest and the near slope catch
   light the way A shows. For D, the darker after-sundown variant, lower the exposure. The light is the single biggest
   gap, in four of the five frames.
2. **Remove the pale vertical band and replace the hard-edged smoke billboards** (dusk-fire, A, D: below the tower; C:
   upper-centre sky). The column reads as a rendering bug, not smoke. Fix: fade the smoke quad's sides (its
   `fireFx.ts` billboard) and its foot, or hide the unlit signal fire's column. Before the lighting, the tower's crest
   must have no lit band.
3. **Rebuild the near-field clutter at spawn** (A, D, dusk-fire; lower left and right). The red, flat-shaded crate or
   tent shapes with black faces read as broken geometry, and the black scrub specks look like dirt on the lens. The
   mockups show clean sand. Fix: give the camp props the shard's material (the textured wood and canvas the wagon
   has), fix their black faces (normals or two-sided), and thin and warm the scrub so no sprite reads black at
   distance. Keep Sefa; he is the quest.
4. **The hand and whip** (A, B, C, D, dusk-fire; lower right). The mockup's whip is a fine plait with a tapering
   fall, in a stitched, worn leather glove, held low and smaller. The game's is a thick, stiff ring in a clay mitten.
   Fix: a thinner braided whip with a plait normal or texture and a tapering lash, a leather glove texture with stitches
   and a sheen, and the viewmodel lower and smaller to the mockups' framing.
5. **Make the waymark fire roar** (C, centre). Fix: a taller flame with log geometry in the bowl, the ember stream as
   dense as the mockup's and swept to one side, a visible dark smoke plume, and the sand around it dark enough at dusk
   that the fire's pool reads. Place the far waymark so it shows lit on the horizon from this view, as the mockup does.
6. **Dress the caravan as mockup B shows** (B, centre). Fix: the crates, barrels and sacks around the wagon, the
   hanging lantern with its glow, the tent and the pack animal on the right, and the smoke column. Stage the
   logbook step (real state) so the tracker and the prompt match. Replace the noisy sand normal near the camera with
   broad, soft ripples.
7. **The tower's scale and the sky** (dusk-fire, A; upper third). Fix: the mockups' tower reads two to three times
   bigger on its crest, with a lit lantern and ladder detail, so check the model's scale and its crest. Add the banded,
   lit dusk clouds to the sky gradient, which is now cloudless.

### Sky Reach

1. **Replace the bridge-head post and add its partner** (A, C, proposal B; lower left and centre). It is the
   nearest, biggest object in three views, and the crudest: a stack of faceted blocks with flat "rope". Fix: a round
   timber post (or two, as A shows) with rope coils and a stone footing, in the painted material the mill already has.
   Remove or texture the grey-green slab in A's lower right.
2. **Backlight the spawn shots** (A, C, proposal B; whole frame). All three mockups put the low sun behind the
   windmill or isles, giving rim light, haze and glow. The game lights the mill from the front with the sun up and
   left. Fix: move the sun or key direction for the spawn to behind the mill's axis, and add haze toward the sun so the
   isles recede.
3. **The fan held as mockup C shows** (C, centre right; and the fan in every view). Fix: the open fan across the
   frame at the mockup's angle, metal guard plates on the outer ribs, a worn, creased paper leaf, the red tassel, and
   a leather-gloved hand with a sleeve in place of the brown claw and cylinder. Give `mock-C-hands-fan` its own camera
   (the post at the left, the mill beyond) rather than A's.
4. **The storm crown's sky and the Roc** (D, top half). Fix: make the painted maelstrom read dark and spiralling over
   the arena from the dais's approach, with lightning, as the mockup's top half shows. Scale the Roc and stage it more
   side-on: a white head, banded wings and talons visible. Remove the vertical warm-to-lavender seam (likely the
   god-ray quad's edge, E398). Rough up the stones (lichen, carved runes), weather the dais, densify the grass, and
   let isles show in the cloud sea behind.
5. **The keeper and the book stand** (B, middle left). Fix: paint the keeper's materials (cloak folds, scarf,
   satchel) to the scene's painterly finish so he stops reading as flat low-poly. Carve the stand with an open book.
   Make the lantern's glow a soft falloff, not a disc.
6. **The waterfalls** (A, B, proposal B, h2, the clip; centre and top left). The sheet behind the mill and the strips
   off the isles are flat, opaque, pale blue. Fix: a scrolling, streaked, translucent water texture with mist at the
   foot, or hide the sheet that stands between the mill and the cliff.
7. **Proposal B's knoll view** (whole frame). Fix: give the view the mockup's camera, higher and back on a grass
   knoll over the chasm, with the long sagging bridge to the windmill isle. Add the hanging roots and moss under the
   isles and the sky-manta the mockup shows.
