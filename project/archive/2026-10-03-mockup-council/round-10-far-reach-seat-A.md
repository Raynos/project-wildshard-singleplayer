## No-shortcut and reachable-state audit

- **Views and geometry:** fixed mock cameras are retained; h3's correction is disclosed and restores its intended subject. Heroes, aerials and orbit samples show a coherent 3D archipelago; no newly pasted playable scenery, shot-specific visibility or model substitution was found. The clip shows the mill turning during the orbit. No obvious renewed o3/l4 intersection is visible. Painted infinity sky remains allowed.
- **Quest staging:** `h4-crown: quest-crown` and `mock-D-crown-arena: roc-opening` are declared in metadata. Completing the bridge quest while the independent Roc is alive is reachable. The helper directly finishes the quest and bridge; it does not prove every prerequisite encounter happened normally.
- **D opening: live animation, unequal initializer.** At the captured SHA, a fresh brain starts `angle=0`; normal `begin`/`think` sets fighting/timers without setting its angle from the perch. `stageOpening()` also snaps actor position/yaw and sets angle to the north perch, approximately −π/2. During the four-second branch, normal fresh entry consequently steers toward the east lap point (13,−196), whereas the staged entry steers toward the north lap point (approximately 0,−209). `restart()` leaves previous angle intact. The builder's assertion that this is the same opening on walking in is therefore not established by the shared duration. This is the round-9 equivalence finding still open, not proof that no possible play history can ever yield a similar image.
- **Timing and approach evidence:** the 10 s clip is an aerial world orbit, not an unstaged crown arrival or boss trace. The route stops near (−6.52,44.02,−180.69), not D's raised knoll viewpoint (0,eye 47.99,−176). It neither times that final climb/approach nor records the Roc. Normal entry includes a 1.5 s first intro (0.3 s retry), so elapsed time must be referenced to actual fight/take-off onset, not just bridge landing. The supplied evidence cannot verify the claimed 3.1 s walk-in/3.3 s capture equivalence.
- **Pose and effects:** D is declared active with calm=false; the four-second flight and wing animation advance. C is an ordinary shared idle hold, without a frozen GUST or dropped GUST effects. D requests a normal storm strike at 1 s; the later capture does not demonstrate that a particular bolt persists at 3.3 s. No new effect-suppression edit was found.
- **No narrowing / route:** the zero-stuck route supports crossings and winch movement under its harness conditions. Each leg still spawns at its start, zeroes velocity, directly enables hover where requested and freezes creature updates. The game's quest reward relocation is disclosed. This is a staged movement-route check, not the promised uninterrupted ordinary quest/combat run. Restoring meadow glow and enlarging the global hold introduce visible tradeoffs; D's dais occlusion must be considered across play, not dismissed because A remains usable.
- **Phone/build/budgets:** capture metadata and 780×1688 exports agree with phone/touch framing, naming 750b533af, load 6 s, 100 programs and zero page errors. They do not independently establish production-serving identity, sustained physical-iPhone 30 fps or total-process loading/Explorer memory limits. The HUD's “30” and MP4's 30 fps encoding are not performance measurements. Missing evidence is not a demonstrated budget violation.
