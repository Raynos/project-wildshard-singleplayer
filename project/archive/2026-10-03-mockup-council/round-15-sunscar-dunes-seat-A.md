## Findings, ranked

1. **R15A-SD-1 — Must-fix, ledger §5 no narrowing: restore the late world.** **C/D and the full orbit; ground y 34–86% of the clip.** The new `farLate` cuts direct and indirect light by up to 70% beyond 70 m at late dusk, on top of facing darkening. The wide ground falls from Y 26–30 to 4–6 and becomes mostly black. This repeats the distance-fade failure discussed in rounds 10/12, after rounds 13/14 restored readable ground; the new capture provides direct regression evidence. **Remove the new distance cut from both light terms.** Obtain dark lee bands from real transverse dune forms and their facing under the shared light, preserving legible crest/windward faces and local fire pools. Recapture all five views and the identical late orbit. A global shader can still violate no narrowing; being shipped and reachable does not exempt it. Under the frozen ledger, this surface requires a re-run.

2. **R15A-SD-2 — Must-fix, ledger §5 views: restore D's fixed examination point.** **D, horizon/land y 43–70%.** The README states that the flipped field put a dune face over the old stand, and relocates the camera from (38,122) to (36,92) to obtain the tower view. The new spot is real: baked ground is about **24.27 m**, its eye is ground +1.70 m, and the direct ground profile from spawn peaks at about **14.4°**. But that demonstrates reachability, not the permitted reason for changing a fixed view. Clearing an obstruction introduced by the world change does not establish that the mockup camera was wrong. **Restore (38,122), yaw 21.7°, and repair the real sightline/terrain there.** If the lead instead proposes a corrected mockup-camera match, supply measured camera/subject correspondence and the old view on this same terrain before accepting a new fixed point. Disclosure alone does not clear the ledger rule. D's number above is diagnostic for the supplied relocation.

3. **R15A-SD-3 — Should-fix: finish the crest system, then its lighting.** **A/dusk pick, land y 35–60%.** Correct wind/key geometry is useful, but the views still read as a central rounded mound plus a ribbed near slope. A's intended golden diagonal is Y 48.5 against 96.5. Shape the actual height field into continuous connected asymmetric crests with the target's projected bends and broader cool lee faces, rather than another local knob or a grade. Keep the tower mound separate from the near crest. Judge both unchanged spawn cameras together, plus h1, h4 and both aerials; re-bake and walk the changed routes. Keep the newly coherent key/glow relationship.

4. **R15A-SD-4 — Should-fix: rotate and articulate the shared hand silhouette.** **D lower right, and every held-object region.** Small scale and a closed ellipse have not produced the target's fitted leather hand. Turn the visible surface toward the back/knuckle plane, articulate finger joints and thumb grip, give the cuff a believable forearm continuation, and add broad folds, seams and worn panel edges. Reduce the loop's regular printed chevron appearance and route the fall behind the grip visibly. Fit one playable common hold to the set and keep it above the buttons; do not assign a pose per camera. A/B's heavier low coils and D's compact pose are a conflict to compromise honestly.

5. **R15A-SD-5 — Should-fix: build the sky's structure and value distribution.** **A/dusk pick, y 18–36%.** The dusk sky window remains Y 100.6 against 75.9. Replace soft high peach islands with lower, thinner, broken overlapping banks, darker interiors and restrained warm rims; keep the dusk pick's bank dusty grey-brown while approaching A's red-orange edges in their shared sky. Concentrate glow spatially rather than lifting the whole frame. Check the tower-deck horizon too. The later sky/LUT must be reviewed on its own capture, including the orbit.

6. **R15A-SD-6 — Should-fix: fire that burns actual fuel, with its normal smoke and floor light.** **C, centre-left y 29–70%.** Break the broad graphic tongues into overlapping irregular flame, concentrate a hot region immediately over visible charred crossed logs, and preserve gaps that reveal the bowl/fuel contact. Restore a substantial warm grey-brown billow at the foot and let it dissipate with the world wind. Make the support straight worn iron on an irregular round stone base. Tighten the local pool rather than brightening all the sand; the measured warm-floor patch is Y 70 against 59.8. Inspect multiple running frames and side views, retaining smoke, sparks and notices.

7. **R15A-SD-7 — Should-fix: resolve the caravan's construction and local light.** **B, centre/left y 40–55%, smoke above.** Add cloth thickness, irregular torn overlaps, hoop/board edges, weathered tailboard structure, readable crate boards and sack folds to the existing subjects. Let lantern light reveal the arch and nearby cargo with contact/falloff, rather than a broad wash. Widen and break up the cookfire plume over the wagon. Preserve the backdrop improvement, and soften its conspicuous ribs. Do not add a distant READ interaction: at the captured 22 m it is outside the book's normal reach.

8. **R15A-SD-8 — Should-fix: make existing beacon and flyer silhouettes read.** **Dusk pick, tower/sky y 18–37%.** The keeper flame is a pin and the platform is a sparse tall cage; strengthen the existing flame/platform silhouette at this ordinary distance. The target's large ray should come from its real moving route at a reachable time, with normal animation, rather than a pasted silhouette or frozen pose. Its small right-edge appearance is not absence, but it is not the target's composition.
