The first meshopt pass collapsed the skinned positions into a signed unit cube, putting half the figure under the floor. The shipped GLBs keep float positions (only UVs, normals and weights are quantized): each spans 0–2.65 m in Y, faces +Z, and the game scales it to the player's 1.8 m (`TRAINING_DUMMY_SCALE`, E252, 4fdcca55) with the hit volumes and Rapier solids to match. Rebuild all three with `scripts/practice/build_dummies.sh` (`GEN=1` re-runs TRELLIS.2 under the model lock): `bake_dummy.py` → `rig_dummy.py` → `pack_dummy.mjs`; the rig gate is `dummy_rig_gate.py` + `gate_sheet.py`.

TRELLIS.2 and Hunyuan3D-2 are both allowed; use whichever gives the better figure (the game ships in North and South America only, so territory licence limits do not apply).
