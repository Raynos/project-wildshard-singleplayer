| R2-A01 | must-fix | Design fuel rules; H2 | **Calling another entry can refill fuel without recursion.** Budget initialization at exported entries needs protection from guest-originated calls. Separate memories and protected publication answer the latter steps; entry refills, forged binding attribution and surviving scratch defeat the earlier guarantees.
