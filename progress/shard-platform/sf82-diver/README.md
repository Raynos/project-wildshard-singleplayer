Dunes' patrol policy in `engine/ai/diver.ts`. Their old defining paths remain
parameter presets, including frozen consumers. One runner, chest target,
observation fence and atomic save owner serve the three patterns. Pattern laws
retain their original distance tests, calm/held resets, steering constants and
contact order; native body authority remains with the host.

The source-hashed shipping oracles cover six scenarios per species, 10,000 ticks
per scenario. Each compares the ordered frame SHA-256 plus contacts, sounds,
starts, burst pushes and final RNG against both the preset and direct parametric
policy. Hash equality is measured within the same runtime; this is not a claim
that transcendental math has a portable cross-platform golden digest.

Nine restored suffixes retain exact flight, runner and actor state without
restore-time contact or RNG draws. Each pattern also accepts its historical
serialized continuation directly and refuses a different observation before
effects. Nonfinite tuning, incompatible homes and zero-step interrupts remain
covered by the existing fixtures.

flyer admission/installation. Root strict, workspace layer strict, touched typed

payloads and outcomes in 11.5 seconds. No input-only manifests are committed.
