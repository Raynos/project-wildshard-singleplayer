# CM1 Explorer resident lifetime

The Explorer releases a scene specimen, its variants and tier comparisons
when the view closes or changes. Only one thumbnail intake runs at once.
An abandoned intake finishes under its captured scope and retires without
publishing into the resident world's source maps. Borrowed world materials
keep their identity; preparing a borrowed surface does not upload it again.
Scan textures use the existing scene/asset lease and retire at the last owner.

The world retains its selected tier/profile. Its Debug quality selectors
already reload the world; admission records that selection and refuses a
later unmatched profile rather than allocating against an older estimate.
Full metadata and every declared alternative remain validated and transported.
The projection reserves four complete maximum choices: the displayed choice,
two tier comparisons and one cancelled decoder. Each includes intake storage,
GPU geometry and the CPU attributes copied into draw geometry.
