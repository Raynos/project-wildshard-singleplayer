# G227 cut list: Pine and Nalati entered centres

E435, 2026-10-08. **Neither centre is proved in cap.** The accepted single-cold Pine baseline is **1185.167 MB** (939.495 WC + 245.672 labelled GL, `0e6d69988`); the last verified Nalati Memory saver ON baseline is **1101.026 MB** (806.654 + 294.372, `65106ea87`). To reach 950 MB with margin needs roughly **235 MB Pine / 151 MB Nalati**, not merely a smaller allocator number. These historical runs are not a same-pin comparison and cannot price a new cut by subtraction.

**Recommendation: finish the measured look-neutral cuts first, and price a smaller grid-only region sky plus distant platform presentation as the first taste choices.** No current neutral cut proves the full remaining gap. Texture-resolution, view-distance, creature-count and music changes need their own choice and matched measurement. Preserve 2× phone render scale and the 1.0 GB cap.

## Rulers and provenance

- **M** = measured allocation / exact object payload, **E** = estimate or sensitivity, **U** = unassigned. All MB are decimal. CPU backing bytes, shallow heap classes, native categories and GL are different views of overlapping allocations; **never sum the rows below**. Neither a class subtotal nor a whole-runtime claim is a removable owner.
- Pine [baseline and source owners](pine-centre-0e6d69988/README.md): `0e6d69988`, phone / 2× / Developer / Memory saver ON, Auto/KTX2. Mac audio clock RUNNING; native audio clock INTERRUPTED at 0. Newer Pine GL ranking on `cdad59776` is **228.777 MB**, after independent platform changes; it is not paired with the old native WC. Its [exact resource list](pine-centre-0e6d69988/gl-ranking-cdad59776.json) is the current GL ranking used here.
- Nalati [centre attribution](nalati-centre-attribution.md): Mac WebKit `0e6d69988`, same phone configuration; **292.245 MB** labelled GL and exact retained-object joins. The Simulator [Memory saver comparison](memory-saver-centre.md) is a different pin/platform; do not subtract the Mac heap from its WC.
- [cut-list-evidence.json](cut-list-evidence.json) preserves every captured heap class, direct CPU user group and GL owner group at least 5 MB, plus native category ranges and source hashes. Ambiguous `engine/scene` labels remain ambiguous. Known smaller neutral cuts are included because they have owners; their credit is not rounded up to 5 MB.
- A native saving needs **≥3 cold boots per side**, isolated pins, identical route/settings and median plus full min–max spread. Three samples in one boot are not three boots. WebKit malloc may retain freed pages: avoid allocating unused data, or release before the next large build. A smaller live heap is not automatically lower WC.
