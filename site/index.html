what is a shard?

A world made of shards.

Each shard is 500 × 500 × 500 metres, built by one author and placed on a shared grid. A server-owned highway runs between them; glowing seams stitch them together. The first grid is five by five around a fixed centre.

questions

FAQ.

What can I play it on?

Any modern browser, on desktop and phone. No install.

Is it free?

Playing is free, in your browser. Building is free too, and we never charge for uploading a shard.

Do I need a Claude subscription to build?

Yes: you build with your own Claude Code, on any plan that includes it. Playing needs nothing.

Who owns my shard?

You do. You keep the rights to what you build; uploading lets us host it and show it in the world. Abandoned shards can be renovated by new authors, and your name stays on as the original author.

What can't a shard do?

Run code that can touch your browser, go over its budgets, block the roads into it, or mint loot that breaks other shards: a shard ships data and baked assets, checked by the validator before it can join the grid.

can I keep it on my phone?
