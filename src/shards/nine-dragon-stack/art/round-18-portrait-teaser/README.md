# Nine Dragon Stack portrait teaser (E169 F3 / E204)

`nine-dragon-portrait.mp4` is the **preliminary fall cut**, retained as a compact review encode: 1080 × 1920, 60 fps, H.264/AAC. The contact sheet shows the Well rise, square breach, Neon Jian, stair street, fall through the Well, and end card. The later [grapple recut](../round-19-portrait-grapple/nine-dragon-portrait-grapple.mp4) is the trailer awaiting final review.

The footage was captured from the partial shard by `scripts/steam-trailer/shots/nine-dragon.mjs` and conformed with the earlier `cuts/nine-dragon.mjs`, MiniMax Music 3 take 302, and the selected MOSS / Stable Audio trailer SFX. Capture report: six shots, zero page errors. The original render has 900 video frames; its audio measures −14.5 LUFS integrated and −1.6 dBFS true peak. `contact.jpg` samples one frame per second, left to right, top to bottom.

This is prototype footage. It predates the playable grapple and the full 500 m cube.
