# V20: Signal Dunes under the grid's one sun and sky (E438, SHARD-PLATFORM SF19a / SF49, 2026-10-04)

**Question:** Signal Dunes is a desert at dusk; the grid draws one sun, sky, fog and exposure for the whole view (dev mode
puts the dunes at cell (−1, 0), west of Driftwood's midday). How should the dunes look under that one frame?

Art direction only, codex `image_gen` edits of a fresh live capture: production build, `?chunk=sunscar-dunes`, iPhone 16
Pro portrait (402 × 874 at 2×), phone tier, touch HUD, the spawn view toward the signal tower. codex re-framed the camera
onto the highway at the Driftwood seam looking west, with empty gloved hands (safe zone: no whip, no ATTACK button).
The dunes keep their sand palette and realistic-cinematic finish.

- `board.jpg`: the A / B / C decision board.
- `A-dusk-override-blend.jpg`: **A**, the dunes keep their own dusk (stars, orange-red horizon band) as a per-shard
  time override, blended through a wide gradient into Driftwood's blue midday sky toward the camera side.
- `B-shared-daylight.jpg`: **B**, the shared clock's afternoon daylight over everything; the dunes keep their ochre and
  rust palette and the dark tower.
- `C-warm-haze-band.jpg`: **C**, shared late-afternoon sky, with a tall band of warm golden dust haze rising from the
  strip; the dunes behind it are dusky orange.

Re-rolled: A, B and C (the first takes gave photographic bare hands instead of the game's gloved first-person hands).

**Recommendation: C.** The haze band keeps the dunes' warm dusk mood inside one shared sky without a sky override (A
breaks the one-frame rule and shows two skies at once), and it doubles as the seam's transition; B is honest to one
frame but turns Signal Dunes into a generic daytime desert.
