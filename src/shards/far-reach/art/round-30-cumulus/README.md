# Sky Reach top-10 row 5: cumulus between the isles (E407)

**Shipped: painted banks.** look/render.ts cloudBanks() lays 48 seeded cumulus banks between and beyond the sky isles (170-560 m out from (0, -110), -2 to 34 m up, nearer ones lower), drawn in look/puffs.ts' painted golden-hour cumulus (tex/clouds.webp, the same hand as the panorama and the keel wisps); each clears every isle's rock, stays 90 m from the playable islands, and leaves the sun disc open from the spawn and the crown (its angular size + 5 deg). Their fade reaches 1.6x the field's, so the far layers stay.

- board.jpg: mockup / round 13 / after (cap57), A, C, proposal B. Measured (Rec. 709): top 1 % A 238.9, C 239.0, proposal B 239.5, B 238.9 (mockups 238.4 / 235.7 / 238.8 / 240.3); over 230: 2.5 / 2.6 / 2.9 / 2.3 % (mockups 2.1 / 1.9 / 3.8 / 2.2).
- keels-aerial.jpg: the playable keels round 13 / after (seat C: the keels fitted inside their decks, rock only, the mill on the rock mass).

**Tried and dropped: volumetric cumulus** (volumetric/). clouds.py rendered eight cumulus as Cycles volumes, lit front and back, packed by atlas.py into a 2048 x 1024 atlas; a card blended the two lightings by its angle to the sun. In the game they read as clean CG cotton against the painterly sky: aerial-painted-vs-volumetric.jpg (round 13 painted / volumetric / shipped), A-volumetric-field.jpg (cream cut-outs in front of the painted sky's lit cumulus); the keel wisps became stickers on the roots, and a high layer read as pale lavender blobs. Three fixes on each, no convergence (the skill's bounded loop), so the shard keeps one painted cloud hand.
