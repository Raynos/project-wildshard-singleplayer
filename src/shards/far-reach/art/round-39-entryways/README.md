# round-39-entryways: How does Sky Reach build its four legal entryways down to the road?

Ask E438 (SHARD-PLATFORM mockups, wave 8), after G99: every shard is a 500 × 500 × 500 cube with four entryways at its
edge midpoints that meet the road at y = 0, or it is illegal. Sky Reach has no ground at its edges (cloud sea at −40 m,
islands at +27…+44 m), so it must build them (SF49). The rest of its edge gets the engine's railing + wall (shown here as
the stone parapet of `art/grid/round-14-railing-wall/B`). Follows on from `round-38-grid-edges` (V19).

First person on the boulevard's edge, looking across the road at the entryway at the midpoint of Sky Reach's edge;
Sky Reach keeps its Ghibli-like painterly-toon golden-hour look. Bare hands and the safe-zone HUD (G78).
